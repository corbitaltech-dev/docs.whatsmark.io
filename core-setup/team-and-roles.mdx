At first you are the only person in your workspace. Add everyone else who works with you from this page.


## Adding a team member

Choose one of the following ways to add a team member:

### Invite them by email

Invite a member by email so they set up their own account. See [Invite Team Members](/core-setup/invitation-acceptance) to see how it works.

### Create Member

Create the account yourself by entering the team member's details and assigning a [role](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions). They can sign in once the account is created.

<Figure
  src="/core-setup/img/create-member.png"
  alt="The Add Member form with fields for name, email, WhatsApp number and password"
  caption="Image shows the Add Member form."
/>

First their details:

- **Name** — their full name.
- **Email** — they sign in with this, and it should be unique.
- **WhatsApp number** — should also be unique. Each member needs their own number.
- **Password** and **Confirm Password** — at least 8 characters. Use **Generate** if you would rather not think of one.

Click **Next**. Then turn on **Is Admin**, or choose a [role](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions). After you choose a role, you see the permissions it includes. You also choose the member's **Platform access**. See the next section.

## Choose which platforms a member can use

Your workspace can talk to customers on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram. The **Platform access** field decides which of these a member can work on. You must fill it in.

- The field appears after you choose a role. The role fills it in for you. You can change it for this member.
- Tick the platforms the member should use. If you tick all of them, the field shows **All platforms**.
- When an owner or admin chooses **All platforms**, it also includes any platform your workspace adds later.
- If nothing is ticked, you see "Select at least one platform." Tick at least one before you save.

The invite form has the same field. See [Invite Team Members](/core-setup/invitation-acceptance).

Owners and admins can always use every platform. They don't need this field.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /core-setup/img/team-member-platform-access.png — The Role step of the Add Member form, with a role chosen and the Platform access field open, showing WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram ticked and "All platforms" in the field */}

To check a member's platforms later, open their details. The **Platforms** row lists them.

### What a member sees with fewer platforms

- They don't see the menu group for a platform they can't use.
- If they open one of its pages, for example from a bookmark, they are moved to a platform they can use. They see the message "You do not have access to that platform."
- The **Inbox**, lists, counts, notifications and dashboard numbers show only their platforms.
- If they can't use WhatsApp, they don't see contacts' phone numbers. A phone number belongs to WhatsApp. It stays hidden even when the same contact also messages you on Messenger or Instagram.
- You can't assign a conversation to someone who can't use that conversation's platform.

<Note>
A member who is not an admin can still add or edit team members if their role allows it. They can only give the platforms they can use themselves.
</Note>

## Additional permissions for one member

You can add extra permissions to a single member without making a new [role](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions).

When adding or editing a team member, you can assign additional permissions that are not included in their [role](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions). These permissions apply only to that team member.

A member needs both the permission and access to the platform. For example, a member with campaign permissions but only Instagram access sees only Instagram campaigns.

## Editing and removing members

Click a member's name to open their details, or use the menu at the end of their row. You can change their [role](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions) at any time.

<Warning>
When you remove a member, their contacts and chats are not given to anyone else. Give their contacts and chats to another member first.
</Warning>

## Team member limits

Your plan decides how many members can be in your workspace. Pending invitations also count.
