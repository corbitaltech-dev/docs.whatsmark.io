At first, you are the only person in your workspace. Add the people who work with you on the **Team** page. Open it from **Setup → Team**. *(Adding team members needs a paid plan. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).)*

## Adding a team member

There are two ways to add someone.

### Invite them by email

Click **Invite Member**. Enter their email and choose a role. They get an email and set up their own account and password. Details: [Invite Team Members](/core-setup/invitation-acceptance).

### Create the account yourself

Click **Create Member**. The **Add Member** form opens. You fill in the person's details and choose a [role](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions). They can sign in as soon as you save.

On the **Member Details** tab:

- **Name**: their full name.
- **Email**: they sign in with this. No two members can use the same email.
- **WhatsApp number**: no two members can use the same number.
- **Password** and **Confirm Password**: at least 8 characters. To get a strong password, click **Generate strong password**, then **Use this password**.

Click **Next** to open the **Role** tab. Here you either turn on **Is Admin**, or choose a **Role**. When you choose a role, you see the permissions it includes. You also set the member's **Platform access**. See the next section. Then click **Save**.

## Choose which platforms a member can use

Your workspace can talk to customers on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram. The **Platform access** field decides which of these a member can work on. You must fill it in.

- The field appears after you choose a role. The role fills it in for you. You can change it for this member.
- The list shows only the platforms your workspace has.
- Tick the platforms the member should use. If you tick all of them, the field shows **All platforms**.
- If you can use every platform yourself and you tick them all, the member also gets any platform your workspace adds later.
- If nothing is ticked, you see "Select at least one platform." Tick at least one before you save.

When you invite someone by email, the invite form has the same **Platform access** field.

Admins can always use every platform, so they don't have this field.

![The Role tab of the Add Member form with the Agent role chosen and the Platform access list open, showing WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram all ticked, with Select All and Deselect All at the top](/core-setup/img/team-member-platform-access.png)

To check a member's platforms later, click their name. The **Platforms** row lists them.

### What a member sees with fewer platforms

- They don't see the menu group for a platform they can't use.
- They may open one of its pages, for example from a bookmark. Then WaMatrix.io takes them to the same page for a platform they can use, or to the dashboard. They see the message "You do not have access to that platform."
- **Chat**, lists, counts and notifications show only their platforms.
- If they can't use WhatsApp, they don't see contacts' phone numbers. A phone number is WhatsApp information. So it stays hidden even when the same contact also writes to you on Messenger or Instagram.
- You can't assign a chat to someone who can't use that chat's platform.

<Note>
A member who is not an admin can add or edit team members if their role allows it. They can only give out the platforms they can use themselves.
</Note>

## Additional permissions for one member

You can give one member extra permissions without making a new [role](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions).

On the **Role** tab, the permissions list starts with the role's permissions. Tick more boxes to add extra permissions. These extras apply only to this member.

To use a feature, a member needs its permission **and** access to its platform. For example, a member with campaign permissions and only Instagram access sees only Instagram campaigns.

## Editing and removing members

Click a member's name to see their details. To change them, choose **Edit** from the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of their row. You can change their [role](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions) at any time.

<Warning>
When you remove a member, their contacts and chats stay in your workspace. But WaMatrix.io does not give them to another member, so nobody is assigned to them. Give them to another member before you remove the person. You can't undo a removal.
</Warning>

## Team member limits

Your plan decides how many members your workspace can have. Invitations that nobody has accepted yet also count. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
