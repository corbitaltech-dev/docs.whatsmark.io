Block a customer who keeps sending you unwanted messages. Until you unblock them, they can't message your WhatsApp number and you can't message them.

Example: you block a customer on your Sales number. They can no longer message Sales, but they can still message your Support number. To stop that too, block them on Support as well.

## Block a customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the chat">
    Go to **Chat** and open the conversation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Block">
    Open the **⋯** menu at the top of the chat and choose **Block**.

    ![The menu at the top of a chat, with Block, Clear chat and Delete conversation options](/core-setup/channels/img/blocked-users-chat-menu.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Check the name and number, then click **Block**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The chat now shows **Blocked on this number**. To undo it, choose **Unblock** in the same menu.

<Note>
  You can only block a customer who sent you a message in the last 24 hours. If their last message is older, you can't block them. This is WhatsApp's rule.
</Note>

## Block many customers at once

1. On the **Contacts** page, select the customers you want to block.
2. Click **Bulk Actions**.
3. In **WhatsApp block**, pick the WhatsApp number and choose **Block**.

Customers who haven't messaged you in the last 24 hours are skipped. To unblock many at once, choose **Unblock** instead. See [Bulk actions and merge](/contacts/bulk-actions-and-merge).

## See who is blocked

1. Go to **Channels** and open a connected number.
2. Click the **Blocked users** card.

The list shows each blocked customer, the number, the date, and who blocked them. Use **Filters** to see one number, and the action on a row to unblock someone.

![The Blocked users list with a Sync from WhatsApp button and a table showing four customers with their WhatsApp user, number, blocked date, source and who blocked them](/core-setup/channels/img/blocked-users-list.png)

### Blocked outside WaMatrix.io

If you blocked someone in the WhatsApp Business app, they won't show in this list yet. Choose the number in **Filters**, then click **Sync from WhatsApp** to add them.

<Warning>
  You may not be able to unblock a customer who hasn't messaged you in the last 24 hours. If WhatsApp refuses, the customer stays blocked and you see the reason.
</Warning>
