Connect your Instagram account to WaMatrix.io and answer your direct messages (DMs) from the same Inbox as your other chats. A DM is a private message that someone sends to your Instagram account. *(Available when your plan includes Instagram. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).)*

Each Instagram account you connect is called a **channel**. It uses one of the connected-account slots in your plan.

## What you get

<CardGroup cols={2}>

  </Card>

## Before you start

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An Instagram professional account">
    You can connect a **Business** or **Creator** account. These are called professional accounts.

    You can't connect a personal account. If yours is personal, switch it to a professional account in the Instagram app settings first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You are the workspace owner">
    Only the workspace owner can connect an Instagram account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A Facebook Page (only if you have one)">
    You don't need a Facebook Page. There are two ways to connect:

    - **Your Instagram account is linked to a Facebook Page.** You log in with Facebook.
    - **Your Instagram account has no Facebook Page.** You log in with Instagram.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A free slot in your plan">
    Each Instagram account uses one connected-account slot. If all your slots are in use, disconnect an account or upgrade. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Connect your Instagram account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Instagram Channels">
    In the main menu, open **Instagram**, then click **Channels**. The **Instagram Channels** page opens.

    Click **Connect Instagram**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose how to log in">
    A panel opens with up to two choices. Pick the one that matches your account and click **Continue**.

    - **Continue with Facebook**: for Instagram accounts linked to a Facebook Page.
    - **Continue with Instagram**: for Instagram accounts without a Facebook Page.

    You only see the choices that your workspace can use. If you see the message **Not set up yet**, Instagram is in your plan but is not ready yet. Please contact support.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: /core-setup/channels/img/instagram-connect-sheet.png — The Connect Instagram panel with two cards: Continue with Facebook and Continue with Instagram, each with a Continue button */}

  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in and give access">
    The login screen for Facebook or Instagram opens. Log in with the account that manages your Instagram. Then give the permissions it asks for.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick your account (Facebook only)">
    If you chose **Continue with Facebook**, WaMatrix.io looks for Pages that have an Instagram account linked.

    - **One Page found:** the account connects straight away.
    - **More than one Page found:** you see **Pick an Instagram account**. Each row shows the Instagram @username and the Page name. Select one and click **Continue**.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: /core-setup/channels/img/instagram-pick-account.png — The Pick an Instagram account screen listing two accounts, each showing an @username and "Page:" with the Facebook Page name, and a Continue button below */}

  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for Active">
    You return to **Instagram Channels** and see the message **Channel connecting**. The new account shows **Pending** while WaMatrix.io sets it up.

    The list updates by itself. When the status changes to **Active**, the account is ready.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: /core-setup/channels/img/instagram-channels-list.png — The Instagram Channels page with one Instagram account in the list, its status showing Active, and the Connect Instagram button at the top right */}

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  **Instagram → Channels → Connect Instagram**, choose Facebook or Instagram, log in, and wait for **Active**.
</Check>

## If the connection fails

You see the message **Could not connect channel** with a reason. Here is what each reason means.

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You have reached your channel limit. Please upgrade your plan. | Disconnect an account you no longer use, or upgrade. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid). |
| The login was cancelled before the account was connected. | Click **Connect Instagram** and try again. Finish the login this time. |
| The connect link expired or was already used. Please start again. | Click **Connect Instagram** and start again. |
| This Instagram account is already connected in this workspace. | Nothing to do. The account is already in your list. |
| No Instagram professional account was found for that login. | Switch the account to Business or Creator in the Instagram app. If you used Facebook, check that your Instagram account is linked to your Page. Or try **Continue with Instagram**. |
| Your Facebook Pages could not be loaded. Please try again. | Try again. If it keeps happening, contact support. |

## What arrives in your Inbox

Once the account is **Active**, these come into your [Inbox](/messaging/live-chat):

- **DMs** that people send to your account.
- **Story mentions.** The chat shows "Mentioned you in their story".
- **Story replies.** The chat shows "Replied to your story".
- **Shared posts and reels.** The chat shows "Shared a post" or "Shared a reel".

When a new person messages you, WaMatrix.io adds them as a contact. Their source is set to **Instagram**. You don't need their phone number.

## Instagram rules to know

These rules come from Instagram (Meta), not from WaMatrix.io.

- **You can reply for 24 hours.** Instagram lets you reply within 24 hours of the person's last message. After that, you can reply again in WaMatrix.io when they write to you. Instagram has no pre-approved templates like WhatsApp, so you can't restart the chat yourself.
- **The person must message you first.** You can't start a new chat with someone on Instagram. To reach people who comment on your posts, see [Reply Bots](/automation/reply-bots).
- **Text can be up to 1,000 bytes.** A byte is a unit of text size. Most English letters use 1 byte. An emoji uses 4. A longer message is not sent at all. It is not cut short.
- **Files have size limits.** You can send:
  - images (PNG, JPEG or GIF) up to 8 MB
  - videos (MP4, OGG, AVI, MOV or WebM) up to 25 MB
  - audio (AAC, M4A or WAV) up to 25 MB
  - PDF documents up to 25 MB
- **Text and a file go separately.** If you add text to a photo or file, WaMatrix.io sends the text as a second message right after it.
- **You see "seen", not "delivered".** Instagram tells WaMatrix.io when a person has seen your message. It does not say when a message was delivered.

In Instagram chats, the location, contact card and voice recording buttons are hidden. Instagram can't receive these from WaMatrix.io.

<Note>
  Meta explains these rules in its [Instagram messaging guide](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api). Meta changes them from time to time, so check there for the latest version.
</Note>

## Manage a connected account

On **Instagram Channels**, each account has a menu at the end of its row:

- **View account**: open the account page (see below).
- **Contact defaults**: choose the default details for new contacts who message this account.
- **Manage ice breakers**: set the questions people can tap when they start a chat. See [Ice breakers](/core-setup/channels/ice-breakers).
- **Retry sync**: shown only when the account is **Suspended**. Try to set it up again.
- **Disconnect**: stop receiving messages from this account. This frees its slot in your plan. For a **Suspended** account, this button says **Remove**.

### What each status means

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Connected and working. |
| **Pending** | WaMatrix.io is still setting up the account. |
| **Disconnecting** | You disconnected the account. WaMatrix.io is closing the connection. |
| **Disconnected** | The account is no longer connected. |
| **Suspended** | WaMatrix.io could not sync the account with Meta. Use **Retry sync**, or remove it and connect it again. |

### The account page

Click **View account** to open the account page.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /core-setup/channels/img/instagram-account-page.png — The Instagram account page: profile card with a Business badge, follower, post and following counts, and Open in Instagram, Refresh and Disconnect buttons; four stat cards below; then the Account details card and the Webhooks card showing "Subscribed and receiving events" */}

At the top you see the profile: name, @username, **Business** or **Creator**, followers, posts and bio. There are three buttons:

- **Open in Instagram**: open the profile on Instagram.
- **Refresh**: get the latest account details from Meta. This also fixes missing message connections.
- **Disconnect**: disconnect the account.

Below that are four numbers:

- **Conversations**: all chats with this account, all time.
- **Open 24h windows**: people you can reply to right now, because they wrote in the last 24 hours.
- **Messages received** and **Messages sent**: counts for the last 30 days.

The **Account details** card shows how the account is connected:

- **Connected via**: **Facebook Page** or **Instagram Login**.
- **Linked Facebook Page**: shown only for accounts connected with Facebook.
- **Connected on** and **Last synced**.
- **Access**: for Instagram Login accounts, this shows "Valid for" and the number of days left. If the access has run out, it shows "Expired — reconnect". For Facebook accounts it shows "Facebook Page token".

The last card checks that Instagram is sending this account's messages to WaMatrix.io. It is titled **Webhooks**. If it says **Subscribed and receiving events**, all is well. If it says **Some events are not subscribed**, click **Refresh** at the top of the page. Then click **Check** on the card to test again.

<Tip>
  If you connected with Facebook, keep your Instagram account linked to that Facebook Page. Your Instagram messages reach WaMatrix.io through that link.
</Tip>

### Keeping Instagram Login access active

Accounts connected with **Continue with Instagram** have access that lasts for a limited time. WaMatrix.io renews it for you automatically, before it runs out. You don't need to do anything.

If Meta refuses to renew it, the account becomes **Suspended**. Open the row menu and click **Retry sync**. If that doesn't help, click **Remove**, then connect the account again.

<Warning>
  **Remove** deletes the account and its data from your workspace. Try **Retry sync** first.
</Warning>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect a personal Instagram account?">
    No. Instagram only allows Business and Creator accounts to be connected. Switch your account to a professional account in the Instagram app settings, then connect it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a Facebook Page?">
    No. If your Instagram account has no Facebook Page, choose **Continue with Instagram**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which option should I choose?">
    If your Instagram account is linked to a Facebook Page, choose **Continue with Facebook**. If not, choose **Continue with Instagram**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I reply to this person?">
    More than 24 hours have passed since their last message. This is Instagram's rule. The chat shows a message that the reply window has closed. You can reply again as soon as they send a new message.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send the first message to someone?">
    No. On Instagram, the person must message you first. To reach people who comment on your posts, use [Reply Bots](/automation/reply-bots).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why don't I see 'Delivered' on my messages?">
    Instagram does not tell WaMatrix.io when a message is delivered. It only tells us when the person has seen it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My account has been Pending for a long time. What do I do?">
    The list updates by itself, so you don't need to reload the page. If it stays on **Pending**, contact support. If it changes to **Suspended**, open the row menu and click **Retry sync**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect more than one Instagram account?">
    Yes. Click **Connect Instagram** again for each account. Each one uses a connected-account slot in your plan. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I disconnect?">
    The account stops receiving Instagram messages in WaMatrix.io. Its slot in your plan becomes free again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
