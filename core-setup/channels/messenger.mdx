Connect a Facebook Page so you can send and receive Messenger conversations through WaMatrix.io, right alongside your WhatsApp channels.

## Before you connect

<Tip>
Make sure you have the following ready:

- A Facebook account.
- A Meta Business account — Business Verification is not mandatory to connect, but it is strongly recommended; without it your account stays on limited messaging tiers.
- A Facebook Page that you manage and want to connect to Messenger.
- Your Page ID, Page Access Token, Meta App ID, and Meta App Secret (for manual connections only).
</Tip>

## Connecting Messenger

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Connect Messenger">
  In the main menu, go to **Messenger → Channels**. Click **Connect Messenger**.

  <Figure
    src="/core-setup/channels/img/channels-connect.png"
    alt="Channels page header with the channel count and the connect button at the top right"
    caption="Image shows the top of the Channels page."
  />
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a connection method">
  You'll see two tabs:

  <Figure
    src="/core-setup/channels/img/messenger-connect-methods.png"
    alt="Messenger connect flow showing Embedded Signup and Manual connection options"
    caption="Image shows the Messenger connect flow with its Embedded Signup and Manual tabs."
  />

  - **Embedded Signup** — connect with Meta's secure Facebook login flow.
    - Sign in using your Facebook credentials.
    - Select the Page you want to connect.
    - Confirm the requested permissions.
    - The connection completes automatically and the popup closes when done.
  - **Manual** — use this for advanced setups with existing Meta credentials.
    - Enter the **Page ID**.
    - Paste the **Page Access Token**.
    - Provide the **Meta App ID** and **Meta App Secret**.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm it's connected">
  Once connected, the Page appears in your Channels list marked **Active**, and incoming Messenger conversations start reaching your workspace automatically.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Figure
  src="/core-setup/channels/img/channels-list-connected.png"
  alt="Channels list with a connected channel marked Active, the method used to connect it, and the date it was connected"
  caption="Image shows the Channels list with a connected channel and its Active status."
/>

<Note>
Both connection methods can be individually disabled by your plan. If a tab is missing or disable, that method isn't available on your current plan.
</Note>

## Managing a connected Page

Open a connected Messenger channel to:

- **Review Page information** fetched from Meta, including Page name, Page ID, category, and connection method.
- **Check** channel health and see whether messages are reaching WaMatrix.io.
- **Disconnect** the Page.

<Figure
  src="/core-setup/channels/img/messenger-view.png"
  alt="Connected Messenger channel list showing Active status and connection method"
  caption="Image shows a connected Messenger channel's Page information and webhook status."
/>

<Tip>
You can connect more than one Facebook Page. Messenger has its own **Channels** page in the **Messenger** menu group. All your Pages are listed there.
</Tip>

## Ice breakers

Ice breakers are short questions a customer can tap to start a chat with your Page. To set them up, click the three dots at the end of a Page's row and choose **Manage ice breakers**. Details: [Ice breakers](/core-setup/channels/ice-breakers).

## Answer comments on your Page

Reply Bots can answer comments on your Page's posts for you. *(Available when your plan includes Reply Bots. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).)* Details: [Reply Bots](/automation/reply-bots).

<Note>
Did you connect your Page before Reply Bots were added? Then you may need to connect it again. If so, the Reply Bots page shows a **Reconnect to keep replying** message. To fix it, go to **Messenger → Channels** and click **Connect Messenger**. Use the **Embedded Signup** tab, choose the same Page, and approve every permission Facebook asks for.
</Note>
