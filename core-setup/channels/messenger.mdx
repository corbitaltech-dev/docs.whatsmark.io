Connect a Facebook Page so you can answer its Messenger messages in WaMatrix.io, next to your WhatsApp chats. Each Page you connect is called a **channel**. A channel is one connected account that WaMatrix.io sends and receives messages through.

## Before you connect

<Tip>
Have these ready:

- The Facebook account that manages your Page.
- The Facebook Page you want to connect.
- For the **Manual** method only: your Page ID, Page Access Token, Meta App ID and Meta App Secret.
</Tip>

## Connecting Messenger

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Connect Messenger">
  In the main menu, open **Messenger → Channels**. Click **Connect Messenger** at the top right.

  <Figure
    src="/core-setup/channels/img/channels-connect.png"
    alt="Channels page header with the channel count and the connect button at the top right"
    caption="Image shows the top of the Channels page."
  />
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a connection method">
  The **Connect Facebook Page** panel opens. It has two tabs:

  <Figure
    src="/core-setup/channels/img/messenger-connect-methods.png"
    alt="Messenger connect panel showing Embedded Signup and Manual connection options"
    caption="Image shows the Connect Facebook Page panel with its Embedded Signup and Manual tabs."
  />

  - **Embedded Signup**: Meta's own pop-up window walks you through it. This is the easiest way.
    - Click **Launch** next to **Continue with Facebook**.
    - Sign in with the Facebook account that manages the Page.
    - Choose the Page you want to connect.
    - Allow the permissions Facebook asks for.

    When you finish, WaMatrix.io creates the channel for you.
  - **Manual**: use this only if you already have your own Meta app.
    - Enter the **Page ID**.
    - Paste the **Page Access Token**.
    - Enter the **Meta App ID** and **Meta App Secret**.
    - Add a **Page Name** if you like. If you leave it empty, WaMatrix.io gets the name from Meta.
    - Click **Connect Page**.
</Step>

<Step title="Check that it's connected">
  The Page appears in your Channels list with the status **Active**. New Messenger messages now arrive in [Chat](/messaging/live-chat).
</Step>
</Steps>

<Figure
  src="/core-setup/channels/img/channels-list-connected.png"
  alt="Channels list with a connected channel marked Active, the method used to connect it, and the date it was connected"
  caption="Image shows the Channels list with a connected channel and its Active status."
/>

<Note>
If a tab or button shows **Locked**, your plan does not include that method. If it shows **Currently unavailable**, please contact support.
</Note>

<Note>
A Facebook Page can be connected to only one WaMatrix.io workspace at a time. If another workspace is using it, you see the message "This Facebook Page is already connected to another account." Disconnect the Page in the other workspace first. If it still does not connect, contact support.
</Note>

## Managing a connected Page

You can connect more than one Facebook Page. All of them are listed on **Messenger → Channels**.

To open a Page's account page, click its name, or choose **View account** from the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of the row. There you can:

- See the **Page information** from Meta: the Page name, category, Page ID, how you connected it, and when.
- Check that Meta is sending the Page's messages to WaMatrix.io.
- Click **Disconnect** to remove the Page from WaMatrix.io.

## Ice breakers

Ice breakers are short questions a customer can tap to start a chat with your Page. To set them up, open the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of the Page's row and choose **Manage ice breakers**. Details: [Ice breakers](/core-setup/channels/ice-breakers).

## Answer comments on your Page

Reply Bots can answer comments on your Page's posts for you. *(Needs a paid plan. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).)* Details: [Reply Bots](/automation/reply-bots).

<Note>
Sometimes Meta refuses a Reply Bot's reply because the Page connection is missing a permission. Then the Reply Bots page shows **Reconnect to keep replying**. This can happen if you connected the Page before you made a Reply Bot for comments.

To fix it, open **Messenger → Channels** and click **Connect Messenger**. On the **Embedded Signup** tab, choose the same Page. Allow every permission Facebook asks for.
</Note>
