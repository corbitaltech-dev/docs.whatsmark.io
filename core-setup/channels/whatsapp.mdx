Connect your WhatsApp Business account so you can send and receive WhatsApp messages in WaMatrix.io. Each account you connect is called a **channel**. A channel is one connected account that WaMatrix.io sends and receives messages through.

## Before you connect

<Tip>
Have these ready:

- A Facebook account.
- A Meta Business account.
- A phone number for WhatsApp. It must be one of these:
  - **not used on any WhatsApp account yet**, or
  - **already on the WhatsApp Business app**. You can keep using the app. See [Coexistence mode](#coexistence-mode).
- For the **Manual** method only: your WhatsApp Business Account ID, access token, Meta App ID and Meta App Secret.
</Tip>

You don't need to verify your business with Meta to connect. But verifying is one way to raise how many people you can message each day. This daily limit is a WhatsApp rule, not a WaMatrix.io one. Meta explains it under [messaging limits](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits).

## Connecting WhatsApp

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Connect WhatsApp">
  In the main menu, open **WhatsApp → Channels**. Click **Connect WhatsApp** at the top right.

  <Figure
    src="/core-setup/channels/img/channels-connect.png"
    alt="Channels page header with the channel count and the connect button at the top right"
    caption="Image shows the top of the Channels page."
  />
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a connection method">
  The **Connect a WhatsApp Account** panel opens. It has two tabs:

  <Figure
    src="/core-setup/channels/img/whatsapp-connect-methods.png"
    alt="Connect a WhatsApp Account panel shown twice: the Embedded Signup tab with a Standard Signup Launch button, and the Manual tab with fields for channel name, Business Account ID, access token, Meta App ID and Meta App Secret"
    caption="Image shows the Connect a WhatsApp Account panel with its Embedded Signup and Manual tabs."
  />

  - **Embedded Signup**: Meta's own pop-up window walks you through it. This is the easiest way.
    - Click **Launch** next to **Standard Signup**.
    - In Meta's window, log in to Facebook and choose your business.
    - Choose or add your WhatsApp number.
    - Allow the permissions Meta asks for.

    When you finish, WaMatrix.io creates the channel for you.
  - **Manual**: use this only if you already have your own Meta app.
    - Enter a **Channel Name** and your **Business Account ID (WABA ID)**.
    - Paste your **Access Token**.
    - Enter your **Meta App ID** and **Meta App Secret**.
    - Click **Connect**.
</Step>

<Step title="Check that it's connected">
  The channel appears in your Channels list with the status **Active**. New WhatsApp messages now arrive in [Chat](/messaging/live-chat).
</Step>
</Steps>

<Figure
  src="/core-setup/channels/img/channels-list-connected.png"
  alt="Channels list with a connected WhatsApp channel marked Active, the method used to connect it, and the date it was connected"
  caption="Image shows the Channels list with a connected channel and its Active status."
/>

<Note>
If a tab or button shows **Locked**, your plan does not include that method. If it shows **Currently unavailable**, please contact support.
</Note>

### Is the account already used in another workspace?

A WhatsApp Business account can be used in only one WaMatrix.io workspace. If another workspace already uses it, WaMatrix.io does not connect it. You see a message that asks you to contact support.

To move the account, disconnect it in the other workspace first. Then connect it here. If it still does not connect, contact support.

### If a channel shows Suspended

**Suspended** means WaMatrix.io could not sync the channel with Meta. Open the channel to see the reason. To fix it, choose **Remove** from the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of the row. Then connect the account again.

## Coexistence mode

You can connect a number that already uses the WhatsApp Business app on your phone. This is called **coexistence mode**. You keep using the app as before. Messages stay in sync between the app and WaMatrix.io.

To use it, choose your existing WhatsApp Business app number inside Meta's pop-up window, on the **Embedded Signup** tab.

Your contacts and recent chats from the app are copied into WaMatrix.io once, when you first connect. These contacts count toward your plan's contact limit. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).

## Ice breakers

Ice breakers are short questions a customer can tap to start a chat with you. To set them up, open the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of the channel's row and choose **Manage ice breakers**.

If the channel has more than one number, the menu shows one item for each number, such as **Ice breakers · +91 98765 43210**. Details: [Ice breakers](/core-setup/channels/ice-breakers).

## The account page

To open a channel's account page, click its name, or choose **View account** from the three-dot menu (⋮).

### WABA Health

<Figure
  src="/core-setup/channels/img/waba-health.png"
  alt="WABA Health panel showing an overall Available status, with Waba, Business, and App each listed as Available alongside their IDs"
  caption="Image shows the WABA Health panel."
/>

The **WABA Health** card shows whether your WhatsApp Business account can send messages. If Meta finds a problem, the card names it. For example: **Payment method required**, **Messaging limited** or **Business not verified**. Click **Fix in Meta Business Manager** to solve it on Meta's site.

The account page also shows:

- **Analytics**: how many template messages Meta counted as sent and delivered. Meta's numbers arrive about a day late.
- One card for each phone number, with its quality rating and messaging limit.

### Business profile

<Figure
  src="/core-setup/channels/img/business-profile.png"
  alt="Phone number card showing the profile photo and number, a GREEN quality badge, messaging throughput and daily limit, and the business name, description, and location"
  caption="Image shows a phone number card with the business profile."
/>

Your business profile is what customers see when they open your details in WhatsApp. To change it, click the pencil button (**Edit profile**) on a phone number card. You can change:

- the **Profile Photo**
- **About**, a short line under your name
- **Description**
- **Address** and **Email**
- up to two websites

### Send a test message

<Figure
  src="/core-setup/channels/img/send-test-message.png"
  alt="Send Test Message panel with a Template picker showing an approved marketing template and a Recipient Phone Number field, opened from the Test button on an active channel"
  caption="Image shows the Send Test Message panel."
/>

A test message proves the connection works before you rely on it. Click **Test** at the top of the account page. Choose an approved **Template**, enter a **Recipient Phone Number**, and click **Send Message**.

This needs at least one approved template on the account. A **template** is a message format that Meta has checked and approved, with blanks for details like a name. See [WhatsApp Templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates).
