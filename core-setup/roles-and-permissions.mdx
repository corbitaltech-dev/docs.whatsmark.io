A role is a set of permissions. Permissions decide what a team member can see and do. Make roles like **Sales Agent**, **Support Agent** or **Manager**. Then give each role to one or more members. When you change a role's permissions, everyone with that role gets the change.

A role also sets the starting **Platform access** for new members. If you change it later, people who already have the role keep their own platform access. See [Platform access](#platform-access) below.

Open **Setup → Roles**, or click **Manage Roles** on the Team page.

## Create a role

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Create Role">
  The button is at the top right. The **Create Role** form opens on the right side of the screen.
</Step>

<Step title="Name the role">
  In **Role Name**, enter a name such as **Support Agent**.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose the platforms">
  In **Platform access**, tick the platforms this role's members can use: WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram. The list shows only the platforms your workspace has. New members with this role start with these platforms.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose what this role can do">
  Under **Permissions**, tick the boxes for each feature.

  <Figure
    src="/core-setup/img/roles-create.png"
    alt="The Create Role form with a role name field and a list of features with permission checkboxes"
    caption="Image shows the Create Role form."
  />
</Step>

<Step title="Click Save">
  The role is ready to give to members.
</Step>
</Steps>

## How the permissions work

Most features have the same four permissions:

- **View**: see the page and what is on it.
- **Create**: add something new.
- **Edit**: change something that already exists.
- **Delete**: remove something.

If you tick any other box for a feature, **View** is ticked for you. A member can't work on a page they can't see.

### Permissions that work differently

Some features have extra permissions:

| Feature | Extra permission |
| --- | --- |
| **Contacts** | [**Import:**](/contacts/import-export#importing-contacts) add contacts from a file. [**Initiate Chat:**](/contacts/managing-contacts#actions-on-a-single-contact) start a new chat with a contact. **View Own:** see only the contacts assigned to them. |
| **Channels** | [**Connect**](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp#connecting-whatsapp) and [**Disconnect**](/core-setup/channels/messenger#managing-a-connected-page) channels. Only the workspace owner can connect an [Instagram account](/core-setup/channels/instagram). |
| **Team** | [**Invite:**](/core-setup/team-and-roles#invite-them-by-email) send an email invitation. |
| **Templates** | [**Load:**](/messaging/whatsapp-templates#adding-a-template) click **Sync Templates** to bring in your approved WhatsApp templates from Meta. |
| **WhatsApp Flows** | [**Submission View:**](/appointment-and-flow/whatsapp-flows#submissions) see what customers filled in. |
| **Reply Bots** | No extra permissions. It has the usual **View**, **Create**, **Edit** and **Delete**. [Reply Bots](/automation/reply-bots) need a paid plan. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid). |
| **Message Activity Log** | Only **View** and **Delete**. Members can see the [message activity log](/core-setup/whatsapp-activity-log) and delete entries from it. |
| **Chat** | Decides what members can do in **Chat**. It works differently. See [Chat](#chat) below. |

### Chat

The **Chat** row decides what a member can do in **Chat**. It has no **View** box. A member can open **Chat** if they have **any** Chat permission, or if a chat is assigned to them.

- **Send Message**: reply in a chat. Without it, the member can read but not reply.
- **Send Template**: send a template.
- **Run Flow**: start an automation or send a WhatsApp Flow in a chat.
- **Close**: close the chat they have open.
- **Clear**: delete every message in a chat.
- **Delete**: delete a whole chat.
- **Delete Message**: delete single messages, and bring deleted ones back. *(Bringing a message back needs a paid plan.)*

## Platform access

Each member can work only on the platforms in their **Platform access**. The role gives the starting value.

- When you give someone a role, their platform access is filled in from the role. You can change it for that person.
- If you change a role's platform access later, only new members get the new value. Members who already have the role keep their own platform access. To change it for them, edit each member on the [Team](/core-setup/team-and-roles#choose-which-platforms-a-member-can-use) page.

To use a feature, a member needs its permission **and** access to its platform. For example, a member with campaign permissions and only Instagram access sees only Instagram campaigns.

## Admins always have full access

Roles apply only to team members. A member with **Is Admin** turned on can do everything, whatever role they have. They can also use every platform.

<Warning>
You can't delete a role that members are using. First give those members a different role, then delete it. The **Members** column shows how many people have the role.
</Warning>
