## Before you start

Two things to have ready:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A phone number for WhatsApp" icon="smartphone">
    It needs to be **either**:

    - a number that isn't on WhatsApp at all, **or**
    - a number you're already using in the **WhatsApp Business app**.

    <Tip>
      Already running your business on the WhatsApp Business app? You **don't** have to give it up. WaMatrix.io can connect alongside it — you keep using the app on your phone, your old chats come across, and new messages appear in both places.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A Facebook account with a business profile on Meta" icon="facebook">
    WhatsApp belongs to Meta, so connecting a business number goes through them. A **Meta Business account** is enough to get started. Getting your business verified by Meta isn't required on day one, but it lifts how many people you're allowed to message, so it's worth doing soon.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Set up in 5 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your free workspace" icon="user-plus">
    Pick a plan on the [pricing page](https://wamatrix.io/pricing) and click **Get Started**. Fill in your name, email, password, and WhatsApp number, then choose a name for your workspace — you get your own web address, like `acme.wamatrix.io`.

    <Note>
      Free Forever workspaces open straight away. If you chose a paid plan, you'll be asked to pay first. See [Free vs. paid plans](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
    </Note>

    Details: [Register](/getting-started/register)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp number" icon="message-circle">
    Nothing can be sent or received until a number is connected. Go to **WhatsApp → Channels** and click **Connect WhatsApp**. Then choose the **Embedded Signup** tab. Meta's own pop-up window walks you through logging in and picking your number.

    When the pop-up closes, your number should show as **Active**. That's it.

    <Tip>
      You can connect more than one number. You can also connect a Facebook Page for Messenger and an [Instagram account](/core-setup/channels/instagram). You answer them all from the same **Chat** screen. *(Instagram needs a paid plan. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).)*
    </Tip>

    Details: [Connecting WhatsApp](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get one message approved" icon="file-text">
    Before you can send the first message to someone, WhatsApp requires you to use an **approved message template**. This applies to every business on WhatsApp, not just WaMatrix.io.

    A **message template** is a reusable message that you can personalize with details like the customer's name, order number, or appointment time.

    - **Starting fresh?** Go to **WhatsApp → Templates** and click **Create Template**. Pick your WhatsApp account. Then choose **Header Template**. This is the type for everyday messages, such as offers and updates. Write your message and submit it. Approval usually takes a few minutes.
    - **Already using another WhatsApp provider?** On the same **WhatsApp → Templates** page, click **Sync Templates**. Every message you already had approved comes in, so you don't need to wait for approval again.

    <Warning>
      A template has to show **APPROVED** before you can send it. This is the step most likely to keep you waiting, so start it early — the review runs in the background while you carry on with the next step.
    </Warning>

    Details: [WhatsApp templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the people you want to message" icon="contact">
    Go to **All Contacts** and click **Create Contact**. One person is enough to test with. You can use your own number. If you already have a customer list, click **Import Contacts** instead and upload a CSV or spreadsheet.

    Details: [Managing contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts) · [Importing a list](/contacts/import-export)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send your first message" icon="send">
    Two easy ways to start:

    - **To one person** — find them in **All Contacts**, open the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of the row, and choose **Initiate chat**. Pick your approved template and send.
    - **To everyone** — go to **WhatsApp → Campaigns**, click **Create Campaign** and pick your WhatsApp channel. Then choose **Template Campaign**. It sends your approved template to a whole group of contacts.

    Then watch the delivered and read counts come in.

    <Note>
      You'll also see a **Message Campaign** option, which sends free-form messages in your own wording. It only reaches people who messaged you in the last 24 hours, so it can't be your *first* message to a new contact. Start with a Template Campaign.
    </Note>

    Details: [Campaigns](/messaging/campaigns)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  **Done.** Connect a number → get one message approved → add contacts → send. Everything else in WaMatrix.io builds on top of those four things.
</Check>

## When people reply

Replies arrive in **Chat**, which your whole team shares. Anyone on your team can pick up a conversation, assign it to a colleague, leave a private note, or send a saved reply. *(Saved replies and more than one team member need a paid plan.)*

One thing worth knowing about WhatsApp: once a customer messages you, you can write back **freely** for 24 hours, in any wording you like. After 24 hours of silence from them, you can only message them again with an approved template. That one rule explains most "why can't I send this?" moments. Instagram has [its own rule](/getting-started/concepts#instagram-has-its-own-rule). [The short version of the rules →](/getting-started/concepts)

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does it really cost nothing to try?">
    Yes. The **Free Forever** plan needs no credit card — you get connected numbers, contacts, chatbot flows and a monthly allowance of AI usage without paying anything. When you outgrow it, the paid plan raises every limit. See [Free vs. paid plans](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will I lose my existing WhatsApp number or my old chats?">
    No. If your number is on the **WhatsApp Business app**, WaMatrix.io can connect alongside it — you keep the app on your phone, your past conversations are brought in, and new messages show up in both places.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I just type whatever I want to a new customer?">
    WhatsApp only lets businesses open a conversation with wording it has approved in advance — that's what a template is. Once the customer replies, you can write freely for the next 24 hours. [More on the rules](/getting-started/concepts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does approval take?">
    Usually a few minutes. Occasionally longer. You'll get a notification the moment the status changes, and you can carry on setting everything else up while you wait.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm already with another WhatsApp provider. Can I move across?">
    Yes, and you don't start from zero. Connect the same number. Then go to **WhatsApp → Templates** and click **Sync Templates** to pull in every message you've already had approved. Your contacts come across from a spreadsheet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect WaMatrix.io to my own software?">
    Yes, if you want to. There's a [developer API](/overview) for sending messages and managing contacts from your own systems. Nothing in this guide needs it — everything above is done from the screen.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
