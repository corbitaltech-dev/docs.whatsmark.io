WhatsMark.io supports online and offline payment, coupon codes at checkout, and a full invoice history — all from **Billing**.

## Paying online

Online payments cover subscribing to a plan, changing plans, renewals, and add-on purchases. You don't choose a payment gateway — the right one for your billing currency is picked for you:

- **INR** — pay by card, UPI, netbanking, or wallet through **Razorpay**.
- **USD** — pay through **Dodo Payments**.

Checkout shows a live total that updates instantly when you apply a coupon. **Your plan activates as soon as payment succeeds** — you're taken straight back to your workspace.

<Tip>
  Paid but don't see it yet? Give it a moment and refresh the page — online payments usually
  reflect within a minute.
</Tip>

<Note>
Prices are shown **before tax**. Any tax that applies is added on top and shown as its own line at checkout and on your invoice — so the final amount charged is higher than the plan or add-on price on its own.
</Note>

<Note>
Paying in **USD**? Dodo Payments handles tax as merchant of record, so it's calculated when Dodo takes payment rather than previewed beforehand. Your total at Dodo's checkout — and your invoice — show the exact amount charged, tax included.
</Note>

## Paying by bank transfer

Prefer to pay by bank transfer instead of online? You can. Your payment is safe throughout —
here's exactly what happens:

<Steps>
<Step title="Transfer to the bank details shown">
  You'll see the bank account (or UPI ID) to pay, along with any instructions shown there.
</Step>

<Step title="Submit your proof">
  Upload proof of the transfer — a screenshot or receipt — and the reference number.
</Step>

<Step title="We review and activate">
  Your request shows as **Pending** while an admin checks it against the payment received. Your
  workspace shows the turnaround time to expect. Once it's confirmed, your plan or add-on activates.
</Step>
</Steps>

Because a person checks each transfer, this isn't instant like an online payment — it activates
once the payment is confirmed. If anything's off, the request shows as **Rejected** with the
reason alongside it, so you can fix it and submit again. Nothing is lost either way.

<Note>
Offline payment is available for plan subscriptions, plan changes, and add-on/contact-limit purchases alike — not just initial signup.
</Note>

<Warning>
While an offline payment is **under review**, you can't change your plan. Wait for the decision, and if it's rejected you're free to try again.
</Warning>

## Coupons

At checkout — whether subscribing, changing plans, buying an add-on, or checking out a cart — you can apply a coupon code. The total recalculates live so you can see the discount before you confirm payment.

<Note>
Coupons apply to your **current purchase only**. They don't carry over to your next renewal, so a future renewal will be charged at full price.
</Note>

<Accordion title="My coupon code didn't work" icon="info">
  A code is usually turned down because it's **expired**, has **already been used** on your
  account, or **doesn't match** the plan or billing cycle you're buying. The checkout tells you
  which — read the message, adjust if you can, and try again.
</Accordion>

## Invoices

<Figure
  src="/billing/img/invoice-history.png"
  alt="Invoice History table listing invoices with invoice number, amount, gateway, reason, Paid status, and date, with a row menu open showing View and Download"
  caption="Image shows the Invoice History table with a row menu open for viewing or downloading an invoice."
/>

Every billed transaction generates an invoice under **Invoices**:

- Browse a table of past invoices.
- **View** an invoice's full breakdown.
- **Download** it as a PDF for your records.

<Note>
Team members need permission to see invoices, and downloading is a separate permission from viewing — so someone may be able to open an invoice without being able to save the PDF.
</Note>

<Warning>
Only the workspace **owner** can pay or apply coupons.
</Warning>
