This page is about looking after a subscription you already have — changing it, renewing it, or
cancelling it. To compare what each plan includes, see [Free vs. paid plans](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).

<Info>
  Only the workspace **owner** can change, cancel, resume, or renew a subscription, or manage
  payment methods. Other team members can be given permission to **view** billing and invoices,
  but not to make changes.
</Info>

## Where to manage it

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu:

- **My Subscription** — your plan, billing cycle, status, and renewal date at a glance.
- **Billing** — where you change your plan, cycle, contacts and add-ons.
- **Invoices** — your payment history.

![The account menu open from the sidebar showing My Subscription, Billing and Invoices](/billing/img/account-menu-billing.png)

<Note>
  On the **Free Forever** plan there's nothing to bill, so the **Billing** and **Invoices** menu
  items are hidden. You'll find the upgrade button on **My Subscription** instead.
</Note>

## Move from Free to Professional

The one plan change you make is moving from **Free Forever** up to **Professional**. On
**My Subscription**, use **Upgrade Plan**, pick your billing cycle, and confirm.

Because the Free plan costs nothing, there's nothing to carry over from it — you simply pay for
Professional for the billing cycle you chose, and it's active as soon as payment goes through.

<Tip>
  Everything you've already built carries over untouched — your contacts, chats, templates and
  flows are all still there on Professional.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Already on Professional?** Professional is the paid plan, so there's no higher plan to move up
  to. What you *can* change is your **billing cycle** (below) and your **contacts and add-ons** —
  raise those whenever you need more room.
</Note>

## Adjust your contacts

Your contact allowance is set by the slider on **Billing**. Moving it up or down takes effect at
your **next renewal** — you keep your current room until then, and it changes over when the plan
renews. The slider won't drop below the number of contacts you're actually storing, so nothing
you've saved is ever at risk.

<Tip>
  Need more room **right now**, before your next renewal? Buy the **Extra Contacts** add-on — it
  adds capacity straight away. See [Add-ons & upgrades](/billing/addons-and-upgrades).
</Tip>

## Change your billing cycle

You can pay **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Yearly**, and switch between them whenever you like —
this is separate from changing your plan. On **Billing**, use **Change Billing Cycle** and pick the
one you want.

![The Current Subscription panel with Monthly, Quarterly and Yearly options and a contact slider](/billing/img/subscription-cycle.png)

A cycle change is **never charged straight away**. It's saved as a pending change and takes effect
at your **next renewal** — until then you carry on exactly as you are, and a small **"Changing
to…"** note shows next to your cycle so you can see it's queued.

<Note>
  Changed your mind? Any pending change — a new cycle, or a reduced contact count — can be
  cancelled before it takes effect. You'll simply carry on with what you have now.
</Note>

## Renewing

Your subscription renews on the date shown on **My Subscription**. There are two ways that happens.

- **Automatic renewal (default)** keeps your workspace running without you doing anything — it
  renews on the date and your saved payment method is charged.
- **Manual renewal** — if auto-renewal is off, you renew yourself from the **Renew Subscription**
  page before the date. A **Renew** button also appears on Billing as the date gets close.

### Turning auto-renewal off

On **My Subscription**, next to **Auto-Renewal**, use **Turn Off**. Your subscription won't renew
on its own — you'll need to renew it yourself before it expires to keep your workspace active.

<Warning>
  Turning auto-renewal back **on** may mean re-confirming your payment method — depending on your
  payment provider, you may only be able to do that at your next renewal.
</Warning>

## Cancel your subscription

On **Billing**, use **Cancel Subscription**. You'll be asked to confirm.

**You keep full access until the end of the period you've already paid for** — nothing is switched
off today, and you can change your mind and **Resume** any time before that date. On My Subscription
the **"Renews on"** date changes to **"Access until"**, so you can always see when it ends.

<Warning>
  Cancelling always takes effect at the **end of your current billing period** — there's no
  part-way cancellation or refund for unused days. After that date, if you haven't renewed or
  resumed, your workspace becomes **inactive**: you won't be able to send or receive, and after a
  grace period it's eventually removed. Renew or resume before then and everything is exactly as
  you left it.
</Warning>

## Resume a cancelled subscription

Changed your mind? While your subscription is still within its paid period (or its grace period
after), open **Billing** and choose **Resume Subscription**. Your plan carries straight on, with
everything intact — no need to set anything up again.
