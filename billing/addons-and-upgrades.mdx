Sometimes you need more of just **one** thing — a few more contacts, another teammate, more AI —
without moving to a different plan. That's what **add-ons** are for: each one raises a single limit
and leaves everything else exactly as it is.

<Note>
  Add-ons are available on the **Professional plan**. Only the workspace **owner** can buy and
  manage them, from **Billing**.
</Note>

Professional is the top plan, so there's no higher one to move up to — **add-ons are how you get
more** when you need it.

## What you can add

  src="/billing/img/plan-addons.png"

- **Extra Contacts** — room to store more people.
- **Extra Team Member** — another seat, so one more colleague gets their own login and their own place in the inbox.
- **Extra AI Tokens** — more monthly headroom for AI Chat Assistant and AI flow generation.
- **Custom Domain** — run your workspace on your own web address instead of a `whatsmark.io`
  subdomain. See [Custom domain](/settings/custom-domain).

Each one takes effect **immediately** — the moment your payment goes through, the limit goes up.

## Buying an add-on

From **Billing → Plan Add-ons**:

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick what you need">
  Choose an add-on and set how many units you want.
</Step>

<Step title="Check out">
  Complete payment — you can apply a coupon here too. See
  [Payments & Invoices](/billing/payments-and-invoices).
</Step>

<Step title="Use it right away">
  Your limit goes up as soon as the payment succeeds.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Already have that add-on?** Buying it again simply raises the number you've added — you won't
  end up with two separate add-ons to keep track of.
</Tip>

## How add-ons are billed

Add-ons ride along with your subscription — they renew on the **same cycle** as your plan and
appear on the **same invoice**. There's nothing separate to manage.

## Stopping an add-on

An add-on can't be cancelled partway through — it stays active for the billing period you've
already paid for. You choose which add-ons to keep when your subscription **renews**: at renewal,
deselect any you no longer need and they won't carry into the next period. Everything is kept by
default, so add-ons simply keep running until you drop them.

<Warning>
  You can't drop an add-on you're still relying on. If an Extra Contacts add-on is holding your
  contact list above your plan's own limit, trim your contacts back under that limit first —
  until then, that add-on stays required.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Not sure which limit you're close to? The [Feature Usage](/reporting/dashboard-and-usage) page
  shows exactly how much of each you've used, so you know which add-on you need.
</Tip>
