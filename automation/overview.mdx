You can't reply to every message the moment it arrives, but customers still expect a quick answer. Automations reply for you straight away. They answer common questions, collect leads and keep conversations moving while you're busy or away. You build each flow once in the drag-and-drop Flow Builder. It then runs every time its keyword or event happens.

Automations work on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram. Each platform has its own list of automations.

<Note>
First, connect an account on that platform. That means a [WhatsApp number](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp), a [Facebook Page](/core-setup/channels/messenger) (for Messenger) or an [Instagram account](/core-setup/channels/instagram). *(Instagram needs a paid plan. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).)*
</Note>

To reply when someone comments on your post, use [Reply Bots](/automation/reply-bots), not automations. On Instagram, a Reply Bot can also answer mentions and story replies. A Reply Bot can then start one of your automations. *(Reply Bots need a paid plan.)*

## Three ways to build your flow

Design the flow in whichever way suits you:

- [**Build it yourself**](#creating-an-automation) — create a flow and add each step in the drag-and-drop Flow Builder.
- [**Start from the Flow Collection**](#flow-collection) — click **Flow Collection** at the top of the automations page. Copy a ready-made flow, then change it to suit you.
- [**Generate with AI**](/automation/generate-with-ai) — inside the Flow Builder, click **Generate with AI** and describe what you want in plain English.

## Creating an automation

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Automations">
  In the main menu, open the platform group and click **Automations**. For example, **Instagram → Automations**. The page is called **Instagram Automations** (or **WhatsApp Automations**, **Messenger Automations**).
</Step>

<Step title="Click Create Flow">
  The button is at the top right of the page. It first shows a list called **Your Instagram channels** (or your WhatsApp or Messenger channels). Choose the account the automation will run on. A **New Automation** form then opens on the right side of the screen, with that account already chosen.
  {/* SCREENSHOT: retake /automation/img/automation-form.png from WaMatrix.io — the New Automation form opened from Create Flow, with the chosen channel already filled in under Channels */}
  <Figure
    src="/automation/img/automation-form.png"
    alt="Automation form with a Name field, a Channels box showing Select a channel type, and a Description box"
    caption="Image shows the automation form."
  />

</Step>

<Step title="Fill in the details">
  - **Name** — a clear name, such as **Welcome Flow**.
  - **Channels** — the accounts where this automation runs. You can add more accounts from the same platform.
  - **Description** — optionally add a short description to help your team understand what this flow does.
</Step>

<Step title="Save and build">
  Click **Save**. The flow is created switched off (**Inactive**). Open it in the [Flow Builder](/automation/flow-builder) to design the flow. Then turn on its switch in the table.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
An automation belongs to one platform. You can't move it to another platform's accounts later. If you try, you see a message such as "This automation runs on Instagram. Choose Instagram channels." To use the same idea on another platform, add a new automation there.
</Note>

## Flow Collection

The **Flow Collection** is a set of ready-made flows made by WaMatrix.io. You don't have to start from an empty Flow Builder. Copy a flow and change it to suit your business. Click **Flow Collection** at the top of the automations page to open it.

Flows are grouped by category. Choose a category from the list at the top to see the flows for your type of business:

- **General** — general-purpose flows any business can use, including Smart Appointment Booking, WhatsApp Sales Pipeline CRM, Shopify Cart Recovery, and WooCommerce Cart Recovery.
- **Business & Professional Services** — consultancy, web agency, recruitment, financial advice, and interior design.
- **Food & Hospitality** — restaurants, cafés, and hostel booking.
- **Shopping & Retail** — general retail, jewellery, electronics, and furniture.
- **Healthcare** — hospitals, dental clinics, and pharmacy or diagnostics.
- **Travel & Transportation** — travel agencies, bus travel, and cab booking.
- **Education** — admissions, online courses, and coaching centres.
- **Fitness & Lifestyle** — gyms, salons and spas, and yoga studios.
- **Events & Entertainment** — wedding and event planning, photography and videography.
- **AI & Automation** — AI Lead Qualification Funnel, AI Customer Support Agent, and E-commerce Order Flow.

Click the star on a flow to save it to your favourites. Click **My Favourites** to see only those flows.

To use a flow, click its card. A **Choose a channel** panel asks which channel or channels the flow should run on. Choose them and click **Create Flow**. WaMatrix.io adds a copy to your automations as a **Draft**. You can then rename it, change its steps and choose what starts it.

Most of these flows were made for WhatsApp. If you copy one to a Messenger or Instagram account, the Flow Builder marks any step that the platform can't send. Remove those steps, then save.

## Managing your automations

{/* SCREENSHOT: retake /automation/img/automations-table.png from WaMatrix.io — the WhatsApp Automations page with the Flow Collection and Create Flow buttons (the current image says Flow Library) */}

<Figure
  src="/automation/img/automations-table.png"
  alt="Automations page with a Flow Library button, a Create Flow button, and the All Automations table showing flows with status badges, on/off switches, and a row menu with Flow Builder, Edit, and Delete"
  caption="Image shows the Automations page with the All Automations table and the row actions menu open."
/>

The **All Automations** table lists every flow, with tabs to show **All**, **Draft**, **Active**, or **Inactive** ones.

- **Turn on or off** — click the switch on a flow's row. Only active flows run.
- **Flow Builder** — click the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of the row, then click **Flow Builder** to open the builder.
- **Edit** — click the three-dot menu, then click **Edit** to change the name, channels or description.
- **Delete** — click the three-dot menu, then click **Delete**. You can't undo this.

<Note>
Sometimes one message matches more than one active flow. Then all the matching flows run and reply, not just one. But a stronger match wins: **On exact match** comes first, then **When message contains**, then **First message from lead/customer**. A **No keyword matches (fallback)** flow runs only when nothing else matches.
</Note>
