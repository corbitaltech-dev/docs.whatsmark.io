You can't reply to every WhatsApp message the moment it arrives, but customers still expect a quick response. Automations reply instantly on your behalf, answer common questions, capture leads, and keep flow moving while you're busy or away. Build each flow once using a simple drag-and-drop flow builder, and it runs automatically whenever the selected keyword or event is triggered.


<Note>
Before you can add a automation, you need to [connect a WhatsApp channel](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp) to your workspace.
</Note>

## Three ways to build your flow

Design the flow in whichever way suits you:

- [**Build it yourself**](#creating-an-automation) — Create a flow and add each step using the drag-and-drop flow builder.
- [**Start from the Flow Collection**](#flow-collection) — click **Flow Collection** at the top of the Automations page and clone a ready-made flow, then adjust it.
- [**Generate with AI**](/automation/generate-with-ai) — inside the Flow Builder, click **Generate with AI** and describe what you want in plain English.

## Creating an automation

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Automations">
  Click **Automations** in the sidebar.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Create Flow">
  The button sits at the top right of the page. A form opens on the right side of the screen.
  <Figure
    src="/automation/img/automation-form.png"
    alt="New Automation form showing fields for name, channels, and description"
    caption="Image shows the New Automation form."
  />
  
</Step>



<Step title="Fill in the details">
  - **Name** — a clear name, such as **Welcome Flow**.
  - **Channels** — select the WhatsApp or Messenger accounts where this automation will run.
  - **Description** — optionally add a short description to help your team understand what this flow does.
</Step>

<Step title="Save and build">
  Click **Save**. The flow is created as a **Draft** — open it in the [Flow Builder](/automation/flow-builder) to design the flow, then **Active** it.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Flow Collection

<Figure
  src="/automation/img/flow-collection.png"
  alt="Flow Collection dialog with My Favourites and All tabs, category filters such as General, Healthcare, and Education, and flow cards each showing a description, an Automation label, a favourite star, and a Use This button"
  caption="Image shows the Flow Collection dialog."
/>

The **Flow Collection** is a set of ready-made flows built by WaMatrix.io. Instead of building from an empty builder, clone one and adjust it to your business. Click **Flow Collection** at the top of the Automations page to open it.

Flows are grouped by category, so you can jump straight to the ones written for your industry:

- **General** — general-purpose flows any business can use, including Smart Appointment Booking, WhatsApp Sales Pipeline CRM, Shopify Cart Recovery, and WooCommerce Cart Recovery.
- **Business & Professional Services** — consultancy, web agency, recruitment, financial advice, and interior design.
- **Food & Hospitality** — restaurants, cafés, and hostel booking.
- **Shopping & Retail** — general retail, jewellery, electronics, and furniture.
- **Healthcare** — hospitals, dental clinics, and pharmacy or diagnostics.
- **Travel & Transportation** — travel agencies, bus travel, and cab booking.
- **Education** — admissions, online courses, and coaching centres.
- **Fitness & Lifestyle** — gyms, salons and spas, and yoga studios.
- **Events & Entertainment** — wedding and event planning, photography and videography.
- **AI & Automation** — AI Lead Qualification Funnel, AI Customer Support Agent, and E-commerce Order Flow.

Use the **All** tab to browse everything, or click the star on any flow to save it to **My Favourites** for quick access later.

To use a flow, click **Use This**. You'll be asked which connected channel or channels the flow should run on, and once you confirm, it's cloned into your workspace as a fully editable automation you can rename, rewire, and trigger however you like.

## Managing your automations

<Figure
  src="/automation/img/automations-table.png"
  alt="Automations page with a Flow Collection button, a Create Flow button, and the All Automations table showing flows with status badges, on/off switches, and a row menu with Flow Builder, Edit, and Delete"
  caption="Image shows the Automations page with the All Automations table and the row actions menu open."
/>

The **All Automations** table lists every flow, with tabs to show **All**, **Draft**, **Active**, or **Inactive** ones.

- **Turn on or off** — toggle the switch on a flow's row. Only active flows run.
- **Flow Builder** — click the menu icon at the end of a row and click **Flow Builder** to open the builder.
- **Edit** — click the menu icon and click **Edit** to change the name, channels, or description.
- **Delete** — click the menu icon and click **Delete**. This cannot be undone.

<Note>
If an incoming message matches the trigger conditions of more than one active flow, all of them run and reply in the chat — not just one.
</Note>
