These are the flow steps you'll reach for most — the ones that actually send something to your customer. Open the **Basic** tab in the Flow Builder tray to find them.

## Text Message

**What it does.** Sends a plain text reply — no buttons, no image. The simplest way to say something.

**When to use it.** Greetings, confirmations, opening hours, an address, a thank-you at the end of a flow.

**How to set it up.** Click the card and type your message in the **Message** box. Type **@** anywhere to insert a merge field.

**Example**

> Hi {'{{contact.first_name}}'}, thanks for reaching out to Sunrise Cafe! We're open 8am–10pm every day. How can we help?

<Tip>
Keep it to two or three short lines. Long paragraphs get skimmed on a phone.
</Tip>

## Button Message

**What it does.** Sends a message with up to three tappable quick-reply buttons. Each button becomes its own path in the builder, so what happens next depends on which one the customer taps.

**When to use it.** Any time you want to offer choices instead of hoping the customer types the right thing. A main menu, a yes/no question, or "which service do you need?".

**How to set it up.**

1. Type the message in the **Message** box.
2. Under **Buttons**, add each button and fill in its **Button Text** — for example, `Yes, continue`.
3. **Value (payload)** is optional; leave it blank and it defaults to the button text.
4. Back in the builder, each button has its own dot on the right. Drag a line from each one to whatever should happen next.

**Example**

> **Message:** What would you like to do today?
> **Buttons:** `Book a table` · `See the menu` · `Talk to us`

Connect `Book a table` to an **Ask Question** asking for the date, `See the menu` to a **Media Message** with your PDF menu, and `Talk to us` to a **Text Message** saying someone will reply shortly.

## Call To Action

**What it does.** Sends a message with a button that opens a web link. Unlike quick-reply buttons, this one takes the customer out to your website.

**When to use it.** Sending someone to a product page, a payment link, a booking page, or a form.

**How to set it up.** Fill in the **Message**, the **Button Text** (such as `Learn More`), and the **URL**. You can also add an optional **Header** above and **Footer** below.

**Example**

> **Header:** Autumn Sale
> **Message:** Everything in store is 20% off until Sunday. Tap below to browse.
> **Button Text:** Shop now
> **URL:** `https://example.com/sale`
> **Footer:** Free delivery over ₹999

<Note>
Call To Action is a WhatsApp-only step. It's hidden on Messenger flows.
</Note>

## WhatsApp Template

**What it does.** Sends one of your approved WhatsApp templates.

**When to use it.** Whenever you need to message someone *outside* the 24-hour window — after an order is placed, a delivery update, an abandoned cart reminder. WhatsApp only allows approved templates in that situation, so a plain **Text Message** would not be delivered.

**How to set it up.** Click the card, search for your template by name, pick it, then fill in any variables it contains. Type **@** in a variable box to insert a merge field such as the contact's name or order number.

**Example**

A Shopify-triggered flow starts with the `order_shipped` template:

> Hi {'{{1}}'}, your order {'{{2}}'} has shipped and should arrive in 2–3 days.

Map `{'{{1}}'}` to the contact's first name and `{'{{2}}'}` to the order number.

<Note>
You need at least one approved template first. See [WhatsApp Templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates). This step is hidden on Messenger flows.
</Note>

---

## Messenger Template

**What it does.** Sends a Messenger template on a Facebook Messenger flow.

**When to use it.** The Messenger equivalent of the step above, for flows running on a connected Messenger account.

**How to set it up.** Search for and select the template, then fill in its variables.

<Note>
This step only appears on Messenger flows and is hidden on WhatsApp flows. See [Messenger Templates](/messaging/messenger-templates).
</Note>

## A worked example

A small clinic wants a flow that greets new patients and routes them:

1. **Start Trigger** — First message from lead/customer.
2. **Text Message** — "Hi {'{{contact.first_name}}'}, welcome to Green Valley Clinic."
3. **Button Message** — "How can we help?" with `Book an appointment`, `Opening hours`, `Speak to reception`.
4. From `Opening hours`, a **Text Message** with the timings.
5. From `Speak to reception`, a **Text Message** saying a team member will reply.
6. From `Book an appointment`, continue into the [Ask](/automation/flow-steps/ask) steps to collect the details.
