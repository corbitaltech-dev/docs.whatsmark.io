People comment on your posts and reply to your stories at all hours. A Reply Bot answers them for you. It can post a reply under the comment, send the person a private message, or start a flow. *(Available when your plan includes Reply Bots. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).)*

Reply Bots answer comments, mentions and stories. When someone sends you a direct message (DM), which is a private message, your [Automations](/automation/overview) answer it instead.

## What a Reply Bot can answer

Each Reply Bot listens for one **Trigger**:

| Trigger | When it runs | Instagram | Facebook Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Comment on a post** | Someone comments on one of your posts or reels. | Yes | Yes |
| **Mention in a comment** | Someone @mentions your account in a comment on their own post. | Yes | No |
| **Story mention** | Someone mentions your account in their story. | Yes | No |
| **Story reply** | Someone replies to one of your stories. | Yes | No |

A Facebook Page can only use **Comment on a post**. Meta sends mentions and story events for Instagram only.

## Add a Reply Bot

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Reply Bots">
  Go to **Instagram → Reply Bots** or **Messenger → Reply Bots**. Click **Add Reply Bot**, then choose the account from the list (**Your Instagram accounts** or **Your Messenger accounts**).

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /automation/img/reply-bots-list.png — The Instagram Reply Bots page with the Add Reply Bot button and the All Reply Bots table (All, Active, Inactive tabs; Name, Trigger, Account, Active and Updated columns), including one row showing the Waiting for your next post badge */}

  If you see **Reply Bot Limit Reached**, your plan has no room for another Reply Bot. Click **Upgrade Plan**, or delete a Reply Bot you no longer need.

<Step title="Trigger & matching">
  The form has three steps: **Trigger & matching**, **Content** and **Review**. On a wide screen, the **Live Preview** panel on the right shows what people will see.

  Fill in:

  - **Name**: a name for you, such as "Launch week comment replies".
  - **Channel**: the account this bot works on.
  - **Trigger**: what the bot answers (see the table above).

  For **Comment on a post**, you also choose which posts and which comments it answers. See [Choose which comments it answers](#choose-which-comments-it-answers). Click **Continue**.

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /automation/img/reply-bots-trigger-step.png — The Trigger & matching step with Name, Channel and Trigger set to Comment on a post, Scope set to All posts, Match mode set to Contains a keyword with two keyword chips, the Only on first interaction and Case-sensitive matching switches off, and the Live Preview panel on the right */}
</Step>

<Step title="Content">
  Choose what the bot sends: a **Public reply**, a **Direct message**, or both. At least one must be on. See [Choose what it sends](#choose-what-it-sends). Click **Continue**.

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /automation/img/reply-bots-content-step.png — The Content step for a comment trigger, with the Public reply section switched on showing a Reply text box and the Add another version and Generate with AI links, and the Direct message section with the Send a message and Start a flow tabs */}
</Step>

<Step title="Review and turn on">
  Check the summary. Leave **Turn on now** switched on if you want the bot to start answering at once. You can turn it off later from the list. Click **Save**.

## Choose which comments it answers

These settings are on the **Trigger & matching** step.

### Which posts (comments only)

**Scope** decides which posts the bot listens to:

| Scope | What it does |
|---|---|
| **All posts** | Answers comments on every post. |
| **A specific post** | Answers comments on one post. Pick it under **Select post**. Search by caption, or click **Refresh from Meta** if a new post is missing. |
| **My next post** | Waits until you publish, then stays on that post only. |
| **Every new post from now on** | Moves to each new post you publish, until you change this setting. |

For **My next post** and **Every new post from now on**, only posts you publish after you save the bot count. Until then, the list shows **Waiting for your next post**. On Instagram, it can take up to about an hour for a new post to attach.

When you edit a **My next post** bot that is already on a post, tick **Wait for my next post again** to move it to your next post.

### Which comments (comments only)

**Match mode** decides which comments get an answer:

| Match mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Any comment** | Answers every comment. |
| **Contains a keyword** | Answers when the comment includes one of your keywords. For example, "price" matches "What is the price?". |
| **Is exactly a keyword** | Answers only when the whole comment is one of your keywords. |

To add a keyword, type it and press Enter. Matching ignores capital letters unless you turn on **Case-sensitive matching**.

Turn on **Only on first interaction** to answer only a person's first comment. Then choose its **Scope**: **First comment on this account** or **First comment on this post**.

### Which people (all triggers)

**Contact types** limits the bot to **Lead**, **Customer** or **Guest** contacts. Leave it empty to answer everyone.

<Note>
  Someone who has never messaged you is not a contact yet. A bot limited to contact types will not answer them. Leave **Contact types** empty if you want to reach new people.
</Note>

## Choose what it sends

These settings are on the **Content** step.

| Response | What it does | Available for |
|---|---|---|
| **Public reply** | Posted under their comment. Everyone can see it. | **Comment on a post**, **Mention in a comment** |
| **Direct message** | Sent privately to the person. | **Comment on a post**, **Story mention**, **Story reply** |

For a new bot, **Direct message** is on and **Public reply** is off. For **Mention in a comment**, a public reply is the only option.

<Warning>
  Everyone can read a public reply. Never put private details, such as a phone number or email address, in it.
</Warning>

### Direct message options

| Tab | What it sends | Available for |
|---|---|---|
| **Send a message** | A text message you write. | All triggers that allow a direct message |
| **Send a template** | One of your saved [Instagram templates](/messaging/instagram-templates), with its buttons, images or cards. | **Story mention** and **Story reply** only |
| **Start a flow** | Starts one of your active flows that uses the **Reply Bot** trigger. | All triggers that allow a direct message |

For a comment, the direct message goes out as Meta's one private reply to that comment. It is plain text only. See [Rules from Meta for comment replies](#rules-from-meta-for-comment-replies).

### Write more than one version

You can write up to 10 versions of each message. Click **Add another version** to add one. For each comment or story, one version is picked at random. Posting the exact same words again and again can look like spam to Meta, so a few versions help.

Click **Generate with AI** to have AI rewrite your first message into up to 3 new versions. This uses your monthly AI allowance. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).

You can add [merge fields](/messaging/merge-fields), such as the person's first name. In a reply to a mention, merge fields stay empty, because that person is not a contact.

### Ask for a follow or an email (story triggers only)

For **Story mention** and **Story reply**, with **Send a message** or **Send a template**, you can ask for something first. You can use one of these, not both:

| Option | What happens | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| **Require a follow** | The message is sent only after the person follows your account. WaMatrix.io waits up to 24 hours. | **Follow request**, **Confirm button**, **Profile button** (optional), **Link button** (optional) and its **Link URL** (must start with https) |
| **Collect an email** | Asks for their email address before sending. If they already have an email on their contact, the message is sent straight away. | **Email is**, **Email request**, and **Skip button** when the email is optional |

For **Email is**, choose **Required to continue** (the message waits until they send an email address) or **Optional** (they can tap the skip button and still get the message). The email is saved to the contact if it has no email yet.

## Rules from Meta for comment replies

When a Reply Bot sends a direct message about a comment, Meta treats it as a private reply. These are Meta's rules, not WaMatrix.io's:

- You can send **one** private message per comment.
- The comment must be no more than 7 days old. Reply Bots ignore older comments.
- The private reply does not open the 24-hour window. You can send more only after the person writes back.
- If the person does not follow you, the message lands in their message requests, not their main inbox.

WaMatrix.io sends the private reply as one plain-text message. So a flow that answers a comment must start with one plain-text message, then a [**Wait for Reply**](/automation/flow-steps/advanced#wait-for-reply) step. The Flow Builder shows an error if it doesn't.

<Note>
  Meta explains these rules for [Instagram private replies](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/private-replies) and [Facebook Page private replies](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/discovery/private-replies).
</Note>

## When two Reply Bots could answer

Only one Reply Bot answers each comment, mention or story. If more than one could answer, WaMatrix.io picks in this order:

1. Bots with **Only on first interaction** turned on.
2. Bots for a specific post.
3. Bots with keywords.
4. Bots that answer every comment.

If two bots would answer exactly the same comments, the form shows **This overlaps with another Reply Bot**. Change the keywords or the scope, or turn the other bot off. You can turn it off or delete it right from the warning.

## Manage your Reply Bots

The **All Reply Bots** table lists every bot for that platform. Use the **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** tabs to filter it.

- **Turn on or off**: use the switch in the **Active** column.
- **Edit**: click the bot's name, or open the row menu and click **Edit**.
- **Delete**: open the row menu and click **Delete**. The bot stops replying at once. This cannot be undone.

A warning icon next to a name means the bot is on but can't send anything. Point at the icon to see why:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| The flow it starts was deleted. | Edit the bot and choose another flow. |
| The flow it starts is not active. | Turn the flow on in Automations. |
| The template it sends was deleted. | Edit the bot and choose another template. |
| It has no message to send. | Edit the bot and write a message. |

### Reconnect to keep replying

If Meta refuses a reply because a permission is missing, the page shows **Reconnect to keep replying**. Reconnect the account from **Channels** and approve every permission Meta asks for. Facebook Pages connected before Reply Bots were added need this once.

### See what your bots sent

Open **Activity Log** in the Instagram or Messenger menu. Messages sent by a Reply Bot show the category **Reply Bot**. See [Activity Log](/core-setup/whatsapp-activity-log).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why didn't my Reply Bot answer?">
    Check these:

    - The bot is switched on in the **Active** column.
    - The comment came from another account. Comments from your own account are ignored.
    - The comment is less than 7 days old.
    - The comment matches your **Scope** and **Match mode**.
    - **Contact types** is empty, or the person is already a contact of that type.
    - Another Reply Bot didn't answer first. Only one bot answers each event.
    - If the bot starts a flow: the person is not already in the middle of a flow on that account. If they are, the bot skips them.
    - The page doesn't show **Reconnect to keep replying**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my message land in their message requests?">
    This is Meta's rule. If the person doesn't follow your account, Meta puts the private reply in their message requests, not their main inbox.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send more messages after the first private reply?">
    Yes, after the person writes back. Meta allows only one private message per comment until then. A flow can use a **Wait for Reply** step to continue the chat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does everyone who comments become a contact?">
    No. Only people a Reply Bot answers are added as contacts. Comments no bot answers don't create contacts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to a story reply that no Reply Bot answers?">
    It goes to your inbox like any other message, and your [Automations](/automation/overview) can answer it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use story triggers on my Facebook Page?">
    No. Meta sends mentions and story events for Instagram only. A Facebook Page can use **Comment on a post**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I connected with Continue with Instagram. Why did a mention reply arrive as a direct message?">
    With this kind of connection, Instagram sometimes sends a mention to your inbox instead. When that happens, the bot sends its reply text there as a direct message.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
