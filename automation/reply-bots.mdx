People comment on your posts and reply to your stories at any time, day or night. A Reply Bot answers them for you. It can post a reply under the comment, send the person a private message, or start a flow.

## What a Reply Bot can answer

Each Reply Bot listens for one **Trigger**:

| Trigger | When it runs | Instagram | Facebook Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Comment on a post** | Someone comments on one of your posts or reels. | Yes | Yes |
| **Mention in a comment** | Someone mention your account (@yourname) in a comment on someone else's post. | Yes | No |
| **Story mention** | Someone mention your account in their story. | Yes | No |
| **Story reply** | Someone replies to one of your stories. | Yes | No |

A Facebook Page can only use **Comment on a post**. Meta only shares mentions and stories for Instagram accounts, not for Facebook Pages.

## Choose which comments it answers

These settings are on the **Trigger & matching** step.

### Which posts (comments only)

**Scope** decides which posts the bot listens to:

| Scope | What it does |
|---|---|
| **All posts** | Answers comments on every post. |
| **A specific post** | Answers comments on one post. Pick it under **Select post**. Search by caption, or click **Refresh from Meta** if a new post is missing. |
| **My next post** | Waits until you publish, then stays on that post only. |
| **Every new post from now on** | Moves to each new post you publish, until you change this setting. |

#### How "My next post" works

Use **My next post** to set up a bot before you publish.

1. Choose **My next post**, switch on **Turn on now** and save. The list shows **Waiting for your next post**.
2. Publish your post. Only posts you publish after you save the bot are used.
3. WaMatrix.io attaches the bot to that post. On Instagram, this can take up to about an hour.
4. The bot stays on that post. It does not move to later posts.

Want to use the bot again on your next post? Edit it and tick **Wait for my next post again**.

**Every new post from now on** works the same way, but it moves to each new post you publish.

### Which comments (comments only)

**Match mode** decides which comments get an answer:

| Match mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Any comment** | Answers every comment. |
| **Contains a keyword** | Answers when the comment includes one of your keywords. For example, "price" matches "What is the price?". |
| **Is exactly a keyword** | Answers only when the whole comment is one of your keywords. |

To add a keyword, type it and press Enter. You can add up to 20. Matching ignores capital letters unless you turn on **Case-sensitive matching**.

<Tip>
  **Contains a keyword** also finds a keyword inside a longer word. For example, "sale" matches "wholesale". Use **Is exactly a keyword** if that is a problem.
</Tip>

Turn on **Only on first interaction** to answer only a person's first comment. Then choose which first comment counts: **First comment on this account** or **First comment on this post**. WaMatrix.io counts only the comments it has received.

### Which people (all triggers)

**Contact types** limits the bot to **Lead**, **Customer** or **Guest** contacts. Leave it empty to answer everyone.

## Choose what it sends

These settings are on the **Content** step.

| Response | What it does | Available for |
|---|---|---|
| **Public reply** | Posted under their comment. Everyone can see it. | **Comment on a post**, **Mention in a comment** |
| **Direct message** | Sent privately to the person. | **Comment on a post**, **Story mention**, **Story reply** |

<Warning>
  Everyone can read a public reply. Never put private details, such as a phone number or email address, in it.
</Warning>

### Direct message options

| Tab | What it sends | Available for |
|---|---|---|
| **Send a message** | A text message you write. | All triggers that allow a direct message |
| **Send a template** | One of your saved [Instagram templates](/messaging/instagram-templates), with its buttons, images or cards. | Instagram **Story mention** and **Story reply** only |
| **Start a flow** | Starts one of your active flows whose **Trigger Source** is **Reply Bot**. | All triggers that allow a direct message |

For a comment, the direct message is sent as a private reply. Meta allows only one private message in reply to a comment. WaMatrix.io sends it as plain text. See [Rules from Meta for comment replies](#rules-from-meta-for-comment-replies).

### Write more than one version

You can write up to 10 versions of each message. Click **Add another version** to add one. WaMatrix.io picks one version for each comment or story. Sending the exact same words again and again can look like spam, so a few versions help.

Each version can be up to 1,000 bytes.

Click **Generate with AI** to have AI rewrite your first message into up to 3 new versions. This uses your monthly AI allowance. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).

### Ask for a follow or an email (story triggers only)

For **Story mention** and **Story reply**, the bot can ask for something before it sends your message. This works only when you send a message or a template, not a flow. Choose one option, not both.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Require a follow" icon="user-check">
    The bot sends your message only after the person follows your account.

    1. The bot sends your **Follow request** with a confirm button.
    2. WaMatrix.io asks Instagram whether the person follows you.
    3. When they tap the button or write back, WaMatrix.io checks again. If they follow you now, your message is sent.
    4. If they still don't follow you, the bot sends the request again. It does this at most once every 10 minutes.

    The bot waits for up to 24 hours. After that, it stops waiting, and your message is not sent. If they reply to your story or tag you again, the bot starts over.

    Fill in:

    - **Follow request**: the text that asks them to follow you.
    - **Confirm button**: the button they tap after they follow you.
    - **Profile button** (optional): opens your Instagram profile.
    - **Link button** (optional): opens a web page. Add the address under **Link URL**. It must start with https://.

    Each button can have up to 20 characters. This is a WaMatrix.io limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Collect an email" icon="mail">
    The bot asks for the person's email address before it sends your message. If you already have their email address, the message is sent straight away.

    Under **Email is**, choose:

    - **Required to continue**: the message is sent only after they send an email address. If they write something else, the bot asks again, at most once every 10 minutes.
    - **Optional**: the request has a **Skip button**. If they tap it, or reply without an email address, they still get the message.

    Write your question under **Email request**. The email address is saved to the contact. Like the follow request, the bot waits for up to 24 hours.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Rules from Meta for comment replies

When a Reply Bot sends a direct message about a comment, Meta treats it as a private reply. These are Meta's rules, not WaMatrix.io's:

- You can send only **one** private message in reply to a comment.
- The comment must be no more than 7 days old. Reply Bots ignore older comments.
- After the private reply, you can't send more messages until the person writes back. Then you can write back freely for 24 hours.
- On Instagram, if the person does not follow you, the message goes to their message requests, not their main inbox.

WaMatrix.io sends the private reply as one plain-text message. So a flow that answers a comment must start with one plain-text message or a question. If it sends more, add a [**Wait for Reply**](/automation/flow-steps/advanced#wait-for-reply) step after the first message. If the flow doesn't start this way, the Flow Builder shows an error. The Reply Bot form also marks the flow **can't answer comments**.

<Note>
  Meta explains these rules for [Instagram private replies](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/private-replies) and [Facebook Page private replies](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/discovery/private-replies).
</Note>

## When two Reply Bots could answer

Only one Reply Bot answers each comment, mention or story. If more than one could answer, WaMatrix.io picks in this order:

1. Bots with **Only on first interaction** turned on.
2. Bots tied to one post (**A specific post**, **My next post** or **Every new post from now on**).
3. Bots with keywords.
4. Bots that answer every comment.

If two bots are still level, the older bot answers.

### Fix an overlap warning

WaMatrix.io stops two switched-on bots from answering exactly the same comments. Two bots overlap only when all of these are true:

- They use the same account and the same **Trigger**.
- They listen to the same post. **All posts** overlaps every post. A bot still waiting for your next post overlaps nothing yet.
- They have the same **Only on first interaction** setting.
- Their **Contact types** share a type, or one of them is left empty.
- Their keywords clash (see below). A keyword bot and a bot for every comment never overlap, because the keyword bot is always tried first.

When they overlap, the form shows **This overlaps with another Reply Bot**, and you can't click **Continue**. The reason is shown in brackets. Find it below to see how to fix it. Under **Resolve by turning one off or deleting it**, you can switch the other bot off or delete it.

![The Trigger & matching step of a new Reply Bot that also listens for the keyword price. A red box says This overlaps with another Reply Bot, explains that only one Reply Bot can answer the same comment, and lists the other bot, Price question replies, with an on/off switch and a delete button](/automation/img/reply-bots-overlap-warning.png)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title='both answer every comment' icon="copy">
    Both bots answer everything. Their **Match mode** is **Any comment**, or they are both mention or story bots.

    To fix it, do one of these:

    - Turn one of them off.
    - For comment bots, give one of them keywords.
    - Give them different **Contact types**, such as **Lead** for one and **Customer** for the other.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='both listen for "price"' icon="tag">
    Both bots use the same keyword.

    To fix it, use a different keyword, remove it from one bot, or turn the other bot off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='"sale" and "wholesale" overlap' icon="text-search">
    One keyword is inside the other, and a bot uses **Contains a keyword**. A comment with "wholesale" would match both bots.

    To fix it, do one of these:

    - Use keywords that are not inside each other.
    - Set both bots to **Is exactly a keyword**.
    - Turn the other bot off.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

The same check runs when you turn a bot on from the list. If it overlaps, you see **Could not turn on**. A bot saved with **Turn on now** switched off is checked when you turn it on.

## Manage your Reply Bots

The **All Reply Bots** table lists every bot for that platform. Use the **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** tabs to filter it.

- **Turn on or off**: use the switch in the **Active** column.
- **Edit**: click the bot's name. Or open the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of the row and click **Edit**.
- **Delete**: open the three-dot menu and click **Delete**. The bot stops replying at once. This cannot be undone.

A warning icon next to a name means the bot is on but can't send anything. Point at the icon to see why:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| The flow it starts was deleted. | Edit the bot and choose another flow. |
| The flow it starts is not active. | Turn the flow on in Automations. |
| The template it sends was deleted. | Edit the bot and choose another template. |
| It has no message to send. | Edit the bot and write a message. |

### Reconnect to keep replying

If Meta refuses a reply because a permission is missing, the page shows **Reconnect to keep replying**. Connect the account again from **Channels** in the same menu, and approve every permission Meta asks for. The message goes away after the next reply is sent.

Facebook Pages need two extra permissions to answer comments. If you connected your Page before you added your first Facebook Reply Bot, you may need to connect it again once. On Messenger, the message names these two permissions. See [Answer comments on your Page](/core-setup/channels/messenger#answer-comments-on-your-page).

### See what your bots sent

Open **Activity Log** in the Instagram or Messenger menu. Messages sent by a Reply Bot show the category **Reply Bot**. If a message failed, the log shows why. See [Message activity log](/core-setup/whatsapp-activity-log).
