Your customers can see your products, fill a cart and pay, all inside WhatsApp. You see each cart as an order in WaMatrix.io, and you can ask for payment with one click.

## What the customer sees

1. You send them a product, a list of products, or your whole catalog.
2. They open it, pick what they want, and send you a cart.
3. You ask them to pay. They pay in WhatsApp or through a payment link.
4. You tell them when the order is on its way.

A **catalog** is the list of products you sell, with a photo, name and price for each one. It is stored in Meta, the company behind WhatsApp, and WaMatrix.io shows it to your customers.

## Before you start

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A connected WhatsApp number" icon="message-circle">
    Products are sent from a WhatsApp number. Connect one first. See [Connecting WhatsApp](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A paid plan" icon="lock">
    Catalogs, orders and payments are not on the Free Forever plan. When your plan doesn't include them, **Commerce** is not shown in the menu. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Access to your catalog in Meta" icon="key-round">
    WaMatrix.io needs permission to manage catalogs for your WhatsApp account. If it doesn't have it, the **Catalog** card shows an error that tells you what to do. See [Connect your catalog](/commerce/catalog#if-something-goes-wrong).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A way to get paid" icon="wallet">
    You need your own payment account. There are two ways:

    - **Payments inside WhatsApp** work only in India, in Indian rupees (INR). You need a Razorpay, PayU, BillDesk or Zaakpay account.
    - **Payment links** use your own Razorpay account and work in any currency your Razorpay account accepts.

    You can skip this step if you only want to take orders. See [Get paid](/commerce/payments).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect your catalog">
  Open your WhatsApp account and create a catalog, or connect one you already have in Meta.

  Details: [Connect your catalog](/commerce/catalog)
</Step>

<Step title="Add your products">
  Add each product with a photo, name and price, or import them from your Shopify store. Meta checks every product before customers can see it.

  Details: [Products](/commerce/products)
</Step>

<Step title="Send products to customers">
  Send products from **Chat**, in a campaign, or from an automation.

  Details: [Send products](/commerce/send-products)
</Step>

<Step title="Handle the orders">
  Each cart a customer sends becomes an order. Follow it on the **Orders** page and change its status as you go.

  Details: [Orders](/commerce/orders)
</Step>

<Step title="Get paid">
  Connect your payment account once, then ask for payment on any order.

  Details: [Get paid](/commerce/payments)
</Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
Connect a catalog → add products → send them → a customer sends a cart → ask for payment → mark the order as shipped.
</Check>
