- Read the two governance files (`AGENTS.md`, `.claude/skills/WaMatrix-docs/SKILL.md`) to learn the intended voice, brand rules, and banned-words list.

The site also has a full auto-generated API reference under the **Api** tab (from `api.WaMatrix.io/openapi/...`); this audit focuses on the hand-written guide pages, not the generated endpoint pages.

**2026-07-27 follow-up note:** a re-grep while installing the `docs-manager`/`docs-audit` tooling confirmed Finding B1 (`/automation/chatbot-flows`) is **wider than originally sampled** — it appears in at least 9 files, not the 2 listed below (also hit in `core-setup/whatsapp-activity-log.mdx`, `messaging/messenger-templates.mdx` ×2, `messaging/live-chat.mdx`, `reporting/activity-logs.mdx`, `settings/google-accounts.mdx` ×2, plus the two below). ## 4. Findings

Each finding: **what**, **where**, **why it matters**, **fix**.

### A. Flagship self-consistency

**A1 — Quick start says "5 steps" but the recap says "four."**
`index.mdx` — heading `## Set up in 5 steps` renders five `<Step>`s, but the `<Check>` recap reads *"Everything else in WaMatrix.io builds on top of those **four** things"* and lists only four (it drops "create workspace").
*Why:* It's the first page every user reads; a visible miscount undermines trust on step one.
*Fix:* Make the recap say five and list all five, **or** reframe the recap as "the four things you *do after signing up*." Pick one and align the number.

---

### B. Broken internal links (High)

A section was reorganised — automation pages now live at `/automation/overview`, `/automation/flow-builder`, `/automation/generate-with-ai`, and appointments moved to `/ai-and-more/appointments` — but several links still point at the **old** paths. These 404 for the reader.

**B1 — `/automation/chatbot-flows` does not exist.** Confirmed instances:
- `index.mdx:134` — card "Let a bot answer for you" — `href="/automation/chatbot-flows"`
- `getting-started/concepts.mdx` — Automation accordion, *"See [Automation Flows](/automation/chatbot-flows)."*
*Fix:* change all to `/automation/overview`. Grep the repo (§2 cmd 2) for any others.

**B2 — `/automation/appointments` does not exist.**
- `index.mdx:142` — card "Take bookings in the chat" — `href="/automation/appointments"`
*Fix:* change to `/ai-and-more/appointments`. (See also Finding F1 — this link is a symptom of the folder taxonomy.)

*Why:* Broken links in the two most-read pages (Quick start and Key Concepts) are the worst place to have them. Add a `mint broken-links` check to CI before launch.

### C. Brand, banned words & plan naming

All rules below are from the house style guide (§1 banned words, §2 brand & plan naming). None are cosmetic — they exist to match the marketing site the visitor arrives from, and to keep customer pages jargon-free.

**C1 — Banned word "tenant" in body prose** (customer word is "workspace"):
- `overview.mdx:10` — *"separate from your **tenant** subdomain."* → "workspace subdomain."
- `settings/inbound-media.mdx:18` — *"downloaded to your **tenant** storage"* → "workspace storage."

**C2 — Bare "WaMatrix" instead of "WaMatrix.io"** (allowed only in `keywords:`):
- `overview.mdx:8` — heading *"What is the WaMatrix API"* → "the WaMatrix.io API"
- `overview.mdx:10` — *"The WaMatrix REST API"* → "The WaMatrix.io REST API"
- `settings/woocommerce.mdx:32` — *"WaMatrix will verify your credentials"* → "WaMatrix.io will verify…"

**C3 — "Free plan" instead of "Free Forever"** (the free plan has a name; use it):
- `getting-started/free-vs-paid.mdx:44` — *"AI flow generation is on the **Free plan** too."* → "Free Forever plan."
- `overview.mdx:15` — *"not available on the **Free plan**"* and *"**Free-plan** workspaces"* → "Free Forever plan" / "Free Forever workspaces."

**C4 — Jargon drift on the WhatsApp connection page.** `core-setup/channels/whatsapp.mdx` is the least on-brand page on the site and it's one of the most important (nothing works until a number is connected). It leans developer-y where the audience is a shop owner:
- Opening line: *"Connect a **WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)** so you can…"* — guide bans both "WABA" and unqualified "WhatsApp Business Account." Use "your WhatsApp Business account" or "your number."
- *"connect using Meta's secure **Embedded Signup** flow"* — guide's phrasing: "Meta's own pop-up window walks you through it."
- *"a **webhook subscription** is set up automatically"* — reword without "webhook," e.g. "incoming messages are routed to your workspace automatically."
- Headings/prose "**WABA health**", "under the same **WABA**" — reword to "account health," "the same WhatsApp account." Keep literal field labels (**WABA ID**, **Access Token**, **App Secret**) only inside the *Manual* connection step, where they are the actual UI labels.

*Note:* On `overview.mdx` (the **Api** tab), developer terms like *quota*, *endpoint*, and *webhook* are **fine** — the guide exempts the API pages. Only the C1/C2/C3 items there are real.

**C5 — "WhatsApp Business Platform" (optional).** `concepts.mdx` — *"Every tool built on the WhatsApp Business Platform follows them."* Mild jargon; consider "Every business messaging on WhatsApp follows them." Low priority.

### D. Component & formatting consistency

**D1 — Bare markdown images instead of `<BrowserFrame>`.** The guide says: *"Use the shared BrowserFrame snippet, never a bare image."* Several pages mix both, sometimes on the same page:
- `core-setup/channels/whatsapp.mdx` — 3 bare `![...]()`: `whatsapp-connect-methods.png`, `waba-health.png`, `business-profile.png`.
- `ai-and-more/whatsapp-flows.mdx` — 2 bare `![...]()`: `flow-form.png`, `flow-library.png`.
*Fix:* wrap each in `<BrowserFrame src=… url=… alt=… caption=… />` to match the rest of the site. Grep with §2 cmd 6.

**D2 — Title Case vs sentence case is inconsistent site-wide.** The guide mandates **sentence case** for titles and headings, but most page titles are Title Case:
- Titles: "Managing Contacts", "Free vs. Paid Plans", "Key Concepts", "WhatsApp Templates", "Bulk Actions & Merge", "Add-ons & Upgrades", etc.
- In-page heading example: `campaigns.mdx` mixes them — "Creating a campaign" and "Campaign statuses" (sentence case) sit alongside "**Common Reasons for Failed Messages**" (Title Case).
*Fix:* Decide one convention. If you keep the guide's rule, this is a bulk rename of frontmatter `title:` and a few `##` headings. (Low reader impact, high consistency signal — worth doing once, pre-launch, so nav labels match.)

**D3 — UI-label casing not consistent with itself.** `whatsapp-flows.mdx` calls the same toggle "**Enable live booking**" in the build step and "**Enable Live Booking**" in the appointments section. Match the app's exact label and use it verbatim everywhere (the guide: "Use the real UI labels… exactly as they appear").

**D4 — Verify Meta links.** `campaigns.mdx` links error codes at `developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/support/error-codes/` — the older `/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/…` path, whereas the rest of the site uses `/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/…` path. The guide warns Meta serves soft-404s (HTTP 200 on dead URLs). Fetch it and confirm real content before launch, and align to the verified link set in the style guide.

### F. Information architecture (needs a product decision)

**F1 — The `ai-and-more/` folder is a vague catch-all.** It holds Appointments and WhatsApp Flows, surfaced under the nav group "Appointments & Flows." Tellingly, the Quick-start author linked to `/automation/appointments` (Finding B2) — the natural mental model is *appointments = automation*. The broken links are a symptom of the taxonomy.
*Options:*
- **(a)** Move `appointments.mdx` and `whatsapp-flows.mdx` under a clearer home (e.g. `automation/`), add `redirects` in `docs.json` from the old paths, and fix the links. *Best long-term.*
- **(b)** Keep the location but rename the folder/group to something concrete and fix the stale links. *Cheaper.*
Either way, retire the "ai-and-more" name — it tells the reader nothing.

**F2 — Likely duplication: the WhatsApp activity log is documented twice.**
- `core-setup/whatsapp-activity-log.mdx` — "View and monitor all outbound messages sent on your behalf."
- `reporting/activity-logs.mdx` — description explicitly covers "the WhatsApp message send log" *and* the general audit trail.
- The same `whatsapp-activity-log.png` sits in **two** `img/` folders (`core-setup/img/` and `reporting/img/`).
*Why:* Two pages describing one feature drift out of sync and split search traffic.
*Fix:* Pick one canonical page (Reporting is the more natural home), reduce the other to a short cross-link, and delete the duplicate image.

## 7. Time-promise check (house rule §2)

The guide bans setup-time promises. Two items to review:

- **Remove:** `getting-started/register.mdx:3` (description) — *"… ready to send — **in a couple of minutes**, no card needed."* This is a setup-clock promise; delete the timing.
- **Soften (judgment call):** *"Approval usually takes a few minutes"* appears in `concepts.mdx` and `index.mdx` about **Meta's** template approval, which the guide notes "varies." Your own Quick-start FAQ already hedges it well — *"Usually a few minutes. Occasionally longer."* Match that wording everywhere approval time is mentioned.
- *Leave as-is:* `settings/custom-domain.mdx` — "DNS changes can take a few minutes to propagate" is a factual note, not a promise.
