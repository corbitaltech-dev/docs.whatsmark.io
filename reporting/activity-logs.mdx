The Activity Log shows all activity in your workspace, including what happened, who performed the action, and when it occurred. Use it to review changes and monitor team activity.

## Activity log list

<Figure
  src="/reporting/img/activity-log.png"
  alt="The Activity Log page with All, Created, Updated and Deleted tabs, a Refresh and Clear Logs button, and a table of entries showing description, event, who performed it, subject and when"
  caption="Image shows the Activity Log."
/>

Each entry has:

- **Description** — Describes the action that was performed, such as "Signed in".
- **Event** — The type of action, such as **Member Added**.
- **Performed By** — The user who performed the action. If the action was performed automatically, **System** is shown.
- **Subject** — The item that was changed, such as a contact or campaign.
- **When** — When the action happened.

## Activities that are logged

You will find entries for things like:

- [Signing in](/getting-started/log-in) and out, and [password or two-factor](/settings/profile) changes.
- [Team members](/core-setup/team-and-roles) added, edited or removed, and [role](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions) changes.
- [Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts) created, edited, deleted or [merged](/contacts/bulk-actions-and-merge).
- [Campaigns](/messaging/campaigns), [templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates) and [channel](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp) changes.
- [API tokens](/core-setup/api-keys) created, regenerated or revoked.
- Billing changes, like a new [plan](/billing/plans-and-subscription), an [addon](/billing/addons-and-upgrades), or a paid [invoice](/billing/payments-and-invoices).
- Someone trying to open a page their role does not allow.

<Note>
Some entries are protected and cannot be deleted — they show a shield instead of a checkbox. Protected entries stay even when you clear the log.
</Note>

Logs stay here until you delete them. They are not removed automatically.
