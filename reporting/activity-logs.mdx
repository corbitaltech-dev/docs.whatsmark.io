The Activity Log shows what happened in your workspace, who did it, and when. Use it to check changes and to see what your team has done.

Open **Activity Log** in the main menu.

## The list of activities

<Figure
  src="/reporting/img/activity-log.png"
  alt="The Activity Log page with All, Created, Updated and Deleted tabs, a Refresh and Clear Logs button, and a table of entries showing description, event, who performed it, subject and when"
  caption="Image shows the Activity Log."
/>

Use the **All**, **Created**, **Updated** and **Deleted** tabs to choose which entries you see. Click **Filters** to show one **Event**, or only what one person did (**Performed By**).

Each entry has:

- **Description**: what was done.
- **Event**: the type of action, such as **Member Added**.
- **Performed By**: the person who did it. If WaMatrix.io did it automatically, you see **System**.
- **Subject**: the item that changed, such as a contact or a campaign.
- **When**: when it happened.

## What is logged

You find entries for things like:

- [Signing in](/getting-started/log-in) and out, and changes to your [password or two-factor security](/settings/profile).
- [Team members](/core-setup/team-and-roles) added, changed or removed, and [role](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions) changes.
- [Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts) created, changed, deleted or [merged](/contacts/bulk-actions-and-merge).
- Changes to [campaigns](/messaging/campaigns), [templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates) and [channels](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp).
- [API tokens](/core-setup/api-keys) created, made again or removed.
- Billing changes, like a new [plan](/billing/plans-and-subscription), an [add-on](/billing/addons-and-upgrades), or a paid [invoice](/billing/payments-and-invoices).
- Someone trying to open a page their role does not allow.

## How long entries stay

WaMatrix.io does not delete these entries by itself. They stay until someone deletes them.

To delete entries, tick them and click **Delete**. To remove everything you can delete, click **Clear Logs**. Deleting needs the **Delete** permission for **Activity Log**.

<Note>
Some entries are protected, such as records of AI usage and appointment bookings. They show a shield instead of a checkbox. Nobody can delete them, and **Clear Logs** leaves them in place.
</Note>
