Notifications tell you about important things in your workspace. For example, Meta approved a template, a bulk action finished, or a customer sent you a message.

Click the **bell icon** to see the latest ones. A **red badge** on the bell shows how many you have not read yet. Click **See all notifications** to open the full list.

<Note>
There is no Notifications link in the main menu. Open the full list from the bell icon.
</Note>

## What you are told about

You get notifications such as these:

- **Templates**: Meta approves, rejects, pauses, disables, flags or deletes a [template](/messaging/whatsapp-templates), or changes its category.
- **Chat**: a customer writes to you, a chat is given to you, or a teammate adds a note. See [Chat notifications](#chat-notifications) below.
- **Bulk actions**: a bulk action has finished.
- **Appointments**: a customer books a new appointment.
- **Your account**: your account is created, activated, suspended or put on hold.
- **Payments**: an offline payment is approved or rejected.

When a new notification arrives, a small pop-up also shows on your screen.

## Chat notifications

Chat notifications tell you about activity in **[Chat](/messaging/live-chat)**. Each one has one of these titles:

| Title | When you get it |
| --- | --- |
| **New message from** *name* | A customer sends a message in a chat that nobody is assigned to. |
| *name* **replied** | A customer replies in a chat assigned to you. |
| **Conversation assigned to you** | A chat is assigned to you, or to a **Team Inbox** you belong to. A Team Inbox is a team, such as Sales, that shares chats. |
| **New note on** *name* | A teammate adds a note to a chat that is yours. |

### Who gets them

- If a chat is assigned to people, only those people get its notifications.
- If nobody is assigned but the chat belongs to a [Team Inbox](/messaging/live-chat#assign-it-to-a-team), the team's members get them.
- If neither is true, the workspace owner and the admins get them.
- You are not told about your own actions, such as a note you wrote.
- You are not told about a chat you have open at that moment.
- Members only get notifications for the platforms they can use. See [Team](/core-setup/team-and-roles#choose-which-platforms-a-member-can-use).

The pop-up for a chat notification shows on every screen except **Chat**. In **Chat**, the new message already shows in your chat list.

### Choose which chat notifications you get

Each person chooses their own. Click your name at the bottom of the main menu, then **My Profile**. In the **Notifications** card, turn each of these on or off:

- **New message**
- **Reply on my conversation**
- **Assigned to me**
- **Internal note**

All four are on at first.

The workspace owner sees two more switches in this card:

- **Daily digest**: a morning summary of the day's appointments. Nothing is sent on days with no bookings.
- **Product tips & activity reminders**: emails with tips, and reminders when the workspace has not been used for a while.

See [Profile & Sessions](/settings/profile) for your other profile settings.

## Managing notifications

<Figure
  src="/reporting/img/notification.png"
  alt="The Notifications page with a selection bar offering Mark as Read and Delete, above a table listing notifications with their type, title, status and created time"
  caption="Image shows the Notifications page."

On the **Notifications** page, open the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of a row. Then choose **Mark as read** or **Delete**. To do this for several at once, tick them first.

Click **Filters** to show only **Read** or **Unread** notifications, or one **Type**. The search box looks in the title and the message.
