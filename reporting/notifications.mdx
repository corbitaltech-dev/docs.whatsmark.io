The **notification** shows important updates, such as approved templates, completed bulk actions, or account changes. A **red badge** shows how many notifications are unread. Click the **bell icon** to see recent notifications, or click **See all notifications** to view the full list.

<Note>
There is no Notifications link in the sidebar. Open the full page from the bell icon.
</Note>

## Notification types

- **Templates** — when Meta approves, rejects, pauses, disables or flags a [template](/messaging/whatsapp-templates), when it changes a template's category, or when a template is deleted in WhatsApp Manager.
- **Chat** — when a customer messages you, a conversation is assigned, or a teammate adds a note. See [Chat notifications](#chat-notifications) below.
- **Bulk actions** — When a bulk action has finished.
- **Your account** — When your account is created, activated, suspended, or put on hold.
- **Offline payments** — When an offline payment is approved or rejected.

## Chat notifications

Chat notifications tell you about activity in the **[Inbox](/messaging/live-chat)**. You see one of these titles:

| Title | When you get it |
| --- | --- |
| **New message from** *name* | A customer messages a conversation that is not assigned to anyone. |
| *name* **replied** | A customer replies in a conversation assigned to you. |
| **Conversation assigned to you** | A conversation is assigned to you or to your team inbox. |
| **New note on** *name* | A teammate adds a note to your conversation. |

- The owner and admins are always told, even when a conversation is assigned to someone else.
- Other members are told about the conversations assigned to them or to their team inbox.
- Members only get notifications for platforms they can use. See [Team](/core-setup/team-and-roles#choose-which-platforms-a-member-can-use).
- An alert shows on any screen, not only in the **Inbox**. For example, you see it while you are on the dashboard.
- If WaMatrix.io is open in several tabs, you hear the sound once, not once per tab.

### Turn on browser notifications and sound

Each person chooses their own settings. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **My Profile**. In the **Notifications** card:

- Turn each chat notification on or off.
- Turn on **Browser notifications** to get alerts on this device even when the site is closed. Your browser asks for permission first. *(Available when your plan includes browser notifications. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).)*
- Turn on **Notification sound** to hear a sound when a new chat notification arrives.

Your workspace owner can turn a chat notification off for everyone. If they do, you can't turn it back on in your profile.

## Managing notifications

<Figure
  src="/reporting/img/notification.png"
  alt="The Notifications page with a selection bar offering Mark as Read and Delete, above a table listing notifications with their type, title, status and created time"
  caption="Image shows the Notifications page."

On the **Notifications page**, use the `⋮` menu to **Mark as read** or **Delete** a notification. You can also select multiple notifications to perform these actions together.

Use **Filters** to show only read or unread ones, or a single type. The search box looks at the title and message.
