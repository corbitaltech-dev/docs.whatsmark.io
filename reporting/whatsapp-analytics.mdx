This page shows your **Meta WhatsApp analytics**, including **message charges, messages sent,** and **template performance**.

All data comes directly from **Meta**, so the numbers are the same as Meta's billing records.

## Choosing what you see

Use the controls at the top of the page to choose the data you want to view.

- **Account** — Select a connected WhatsApp channel. Each channel has its own analytics.
- **Date range** — Filter data by **Today, Yesterday, Last 2 Days, Last 7 Days, Last 14 Days, Last 30 Days,** or **Last 90 Days**.
- **Refresh** — Get the latest data from Meta.

## The three numbers at the top

| Card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Amount spent** | Total amount Meta charged during the selected date range. |
| **Cost per paid message** | Average cost of each **paid** message. |
| **Messages** | Total messages counted by Meta, including free messages. |

## Cost by category

This section breaks down your Meta charges by message category:

- **Marketing** — Promotional and sales messages.
- **Utility** — Order updates, reminders, and receipts.
- **Authentication** — One-time passcodes (OTPs).
- **Service** — Replies inside the [24-hour window](/getting-started/concepts#two-whatsapp-rules-that-shape-everything) after a customer messages you. **These are free.**

Each one shows what you spent, how many messages it covered, and its share of your bill.

<Note>
Meta sets these rates, and they differ by country and category. For current rates, see [WhatsApp Platform pricing](https://business.whatsapp.com/products/platform-pricing) from Meta.
</Note>

## The two charts

- **Cost over time** — Shows how much Meta charged for your billable messages over the selected period.
- **Messages over time** — Compares the number of **sent** and **delivered** messages over time.

## Template performance

See how each approved WhatsApp [templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates) performed during the selected date range.

<Figure
  src="/reporting/img/wp-analytics-template.png"

  caption="Image shows the Template performance table."
/>

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Template** | Template name |
| **Sent** | Number of messages sent |
| **Delivered** | Number of messages delivered |
| **Delivery rate** | Percentage of sent messages that were delivered |
| **Read** | Number of messages opened |
| **Read rate** | Percentage of delivered messages that were read |
| **Clicked** | Number of button or link clicks |
