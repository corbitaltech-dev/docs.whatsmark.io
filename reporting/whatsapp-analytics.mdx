This page shows what Meta charges you for WhatsApp messages, how many messages you sent, and how your templates performed. *(Needs a paid plan. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).)*

All the numbers come from Meta. Costs can take a day or more to show, because Meta books them later.

Open **WhatsApp → Analytics** in the menu. Analytics covers WhatsApp only. Messenger and Instagram don't have this page. To open it, a team member needs access to WhatsApp and the **View** permission for **Analytics**.

## Choose what you see

Use the controls at the top of the page:

- **Account**: choose a connected WhatsApp channel. Each channel has its own numbers.
- **Date range**: choose **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 2 days**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 14 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days**.
- **Refresh**: get the latest numbers from Meta.

## The three numbers at the top

| Card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Amount spent** | How much Meta charged in the date range. It is shown in your WhatsApp account's billing currency. |
| **Cost per paid message** | The average cost of each message Meta charged for. |
| **Messages** | All messages Meta counted, including free ones. |

## Cost by category

Meta puts each message in a category and charges by category:

- **Marketing**: offers, sales and other promotions.
- **Utility**: order updates, reminders and receipts.
- **Authentication**: one-time passcodes (OTPs).
- **Service**: your replies in the [24 hours after a customer writes to you](/getting-started/concepts#two-whatsapp-rules-that-shape-everything). Meta does not charge for these.

For each category, you see what you spent, how many messages it covered, and its share of your bill.

<Note>
Meta sets these prices, not WaMatrix.io. They are different for each country and category. Meta lists them on [WhatsApp Business Platform pricing](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/).
</Note>

## The two charts

- **Cost over time**: how much Meta charged over the date range.
- **Messages over time**: how many messages were **Sent** and **Delivered** over the date range.

## Template performance

See how each approved WhatsApp [template](/messaging/whatsapp-templates) did in the date range.

<Figure
  src="/reporting/img/wp-analytics-template.png"
  alt="The Template performance table listing templates with sent, delivered, delivery rate, read, read rate and clicked columns, plus a search box and column picker"
  caption="Image shows the Template performance table."
/>

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Template** | The template name. |
| **Sent** | How many messages were sent. |
| **Delivered** | How many messages reached the customer. |
| **Delivery rate** | The share of sent messages that were delivered. |
| **Read** | How many messages were opened. |
| **Read rate** | The share of delivered messages that were read. |
| **Clicked** | How many times people clicked a button or link. |

You can also show a **Click rate** column. It is hidden at first, because only templates with buttons get clicks.

If you see "Template insights are turned off", turn on **Template analytics** in your Meta Business settings. Then the table fills in.
