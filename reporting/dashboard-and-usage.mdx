The **Dashboard** gives you a quick overview of your workspace. Check your recent activity, monitor plan usage, track messaging performance, and see what's coming up—all from one place.

## What you see first

<Figure
  src="/reporting/img/dashboard/dashboard-greeting-usage-limits.png"

  caption="Image shows the greeting card and the Usage & Limits panel."
/>

At the top of the Dashboard, you'll see today's date and quick actions:

- **Open Inbox** — go to [Chat](/messaging/live-chat).
- **New Campaign** — start a [campaign](/messaging/campaigns).
- **Refresh Data** — Update the dashboard with the latest data.

## Usage & Limits

This section shows your current **plan usage**.

At the top, you'll see how many features are tracked, how many are unlimited, and which tracked feature has the highest usage.

Each card shows how much of a feature you've used, its limit, and the percentage used. Most cards also link to the related page.

There are two types of limits:

- **Period quota** — Resets at the start of each billing cycle, such as AI tokens and campaigns.
- **Plan limit** — Does not reset and counts the total allowed by your plan, such as contacts and team members.

Click **View all** to open the full [Feature Usage](#feature-usage) page.

## Upcoming Campaigns

This section shows the next five [campaigns](/messaging/campaigns) that are **scheduled or currently sending**. For each campaign, you can see its **name, current status, how many contacts will receive it**, and the **scheduled date and time.**

## Analytics

This section contains your workspace charts. Each chart has its own **date range filter**, which defaults to **Last 14 days**. You can change it to **Today, Last 7 days, Last 15 days, Last month, or Last year.**

<Figure
  src="/reporting/img/dashboard/dashboard-messages-contacts-charts.png"
  alt="Three Dashboard charts side by side: a Messages line chart plotting sent against received over the last 14 days, a Leads and Customers donut showing a converted percentage with customers, leads, and guests counts, and a New contacts by source donut split between whatsapp and Instagram"
  caption="Image shows the Messages, Leads & Customers, and New contacts by source charts."
/>

- **Messages** — Compare the number of messages sent and received over time.
- **Leads & Customers** — See how many new contacts became customers, leads, or guests, along with the percentage that converted into customers.
- **New contacts by source** — See where your new contacts came from by source.

<Figure
  src="/reporting/img/dashboard/dashboard-channels-message-sources.png"

- **Channels** — See number of incoming and outgoing messages for each connected [channel](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp).
- **Sent messages by source** — See the source of your outgoing messages: [**Templates**](/messaging/whatsapp-templates), [**Flows**](/automation/overview) or [**Direct chat**](/messaging/live-chat).

<Figure

## Feature Usage

View all your plan limits in one place.

Open it from **Usage & Limits → View all**.

<Figure
  src="/reporting/img/feature-usage.png"
  alt="The Feature Usage page with a Current Plan card, Features, Unlimited and Highest Usage stats, and features grouped by AI, Messaging, Channel and Integration, each row showing a used-of-limit figure, a progress bar and a status badge"
  caption="Image shows the Feature Usage page."
/>

At the top, you'll see your **current plan**, the total number of **features, unlimited features,** and the feature with the **highest usage**. Click Manage Plan to open the [Billing](/billing/plans-and-subscription).

Below, your plan features are grouped into categories such as **Core, Messaging, Automation, AI, Channels, Integrations, Team, Users, API, and Advanced.** Only the categories included in your plan are shown.
