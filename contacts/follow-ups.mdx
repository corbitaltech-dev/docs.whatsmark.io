Assign each follow-up to one or more team members, set a due date and time, and receive a reminder when it's time to take action.

A **follow-up** is a reminder to take action on a contact. For example, you can create a reminder to *"Call the customer about the Gold plan on Monday at 10:00 AM."* Assign the task to the relevant team member, set the schedule, and WaMatrix.io will remind them when the follow-up is due.

## Designed for your team

Follow-ups are private reminders for your team. They are not visible to contacts, and creating a follow-up does not send any messages to them.

## Available on all plans

Team follow-ups are available on every plan, including the **Free Forever** plan. You can create follow-ups for yourself on any plan. To assign follow-ups to multiple team members, you need more than one team member, which requires a paid plan.

See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid) for more details.

## Schedule a follow-up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Follow-ups** in the main menu, then click **New follow-up**.

    You can also start from a chat or a contact. See [Other ways to schedule](#other-ways-to-schedule).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a title and a contact">
    In **Title**, write what needs to happen. For example: "Call back about the Gold plan".

    In **Contact**, search by name, phone or email, and choose the contact.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: /contacts/img/follow-up-form.png — The Schedule a follow-up form with Title, Contact, Due (with the In 1 hour, Tomorrow 10:00 and Next Monday buttons and the "Times are in" line), Remind, Assigned to and Notes fields, and the Cancel and Schedule buttons */}

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose when it is due">
    In **Due**, pick a date and time. You can also click **In 1 hour**, **Tomorrow 10:00** or **Next Monday**.

    The time must be in the future.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a reminder">
    In **Remind**, choose when to get a reminder before the due time. For example, **15 minutes before** or **1 day before**.

    You always get an alert at the due time as well. If you don't want an earlier reminder, choose **Due-time alert only**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose who should do it">
    In **Assigned to**, pick one or more team members. You are picked by default. Everyone you pick gets the reminders.

    Add anything useful in **Notes**, such as what to discuss. Then click **Schedule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Go to **Follow-ups → New follow-up**. Enter a title, select a contact, set the due date and time, assign the follow-up to the relevant team member, and click **Schedule** to save it.
</Check>

<Note>
  When you assign a follow-up to someone who doesn't yet have access to the contact's chat, WaMatrix.io automatically adds them to the chat so they can view the conversation. A system message, such as **"Assigned to Priya by Ravi,"** appears in the chat to record the assignment. Removing someone from the follow-up later does not remove them from the chat.
</Note>

### Other ways to schedule

- **From a chat.** In [Chat](/messaging/live-chat), click the calendar icon (**Schedule follow-up**) at the top of the conversation. On a phone, it is in the `⋮` menu. The contact is filled in for you.
- **From the contact panel in a chat.** Open the contact panel and click the calendar icon (**Follow-ups**). Click **Schedule**.
- **From a contact.** Open a contact from [Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts) and click the **Follow-ups** tab. Click **Schedule**.
- **From the calendar.** Click an empty hour in the **Day** or **Week** view, or a day in the **Month** view. The time is filled in for you.

## Find your follow-ups

The **Follow-ups** page shows four numbers at the top: **Due today**, **Upcoming**, **Overdue** and **Completed this week**.

Use the **List** and **Calendar** buttons to switch between the two views.

### The list

{/* SCREENSHOT: /contacts/img/follow-ups-list.png — The Follow-ups page in List view: the four number cards (Due today, Upcoming, Overdue, Completed this week), the All, Upcoming, Today, Overdue, Completed and Cancelled tabs, and a table with Follow-up, Contact, Due, Assigned to and Status columns */}

Use the tabs to choose what you see:

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Upcoming** | Follow-ups that are not done yet and are due now or later. This tab opens first. |
| **Today** | Follow-ups due today that are not done yet. |
| **Overdue** | Follow-ups whose time has passed but that are not done yet. |
| **Completed** | Follow-ups someone marked as done. |
| **Cancelled** | Follow-ups someone cancelled. |
| **All** | Everything. |

The search box looks at the title and the notes. It does not search contact names.

If you can see the whole team's follow-ups, click **Filters** and use **Assigned to** to choose between **Assigned to me**, **Everyone** or one teammate. The list starts on **Assigned to me**. In the calendar, the **Assigned to** menu is at the top.

### The calendar

{/* SCREENSHOT: /contacts/img/follow-ups-calendar.png — The Follow-ups page in Calendar view, Week tab selected, with the Previous, Today and Next buttons and several follow-ups shown as coloured blocks in the time grid */}

Choose **Day**, **Week** or **Month**. Use the arrows and **Today** to move around. Click a follow-up to see its details.

The colours tell you the status:

- **Blue**: not done yet.
- **Red**: overdue.
- **Green, crossed out**: completed.

Cancelled follow-ups are not shown on the calendar. Find them in the **Cancelled** tab of the list.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Not done yet. |
| **Overdue** | Still pending, and the due time has passed. |
| **Completed** | Someone marked it as done. |
| **Cancelled** | Someone cancelled it. No more reminders are sent. |

## Complete, change or delete a follow-up

Click a follow-up's title to see its details. From there, click **Chat** to open the conversation, or **Profile** to open the contact.

To change a follow-up, use the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of its row. It is in the list and in a contact's **Follow-ups** tab. You need the **Manage** permission for these actions.

- **Mark complete**: the work is done.
- **Reschedule**: move it to **In 1 hour**, **Tomorrow, same time** or **Next week, same time**. These count from today, not from the old due date. Choose **Pick a date…** for any other time.
- **Edit**: change the title, time, reminder, people or notes.
- **Cancel follow-up**: stop it without deleting it.
- **Reopen**: bring back a completed or cancelled follow-up. You must reopen it before you can edit it.
- **Delete**: delete it forever. It disappears from every calendar and no more reminders are sent. This cannot be undone.

## Reminders and alerts

Everyone the follow-up is assigned to gets:

- **A reminder** at the time you chose in **Remind**.
- **An alert** at the due time.
- **One email**, sent with the first of these.

Reminders and alerts show under the [bell icon](/reporting/notifications). Click one to open the follow-up. Alerts can arrive up to a minute late. If an alert is more than 6 hours late, it is not sent.

When someone gives you a follow-up, you also get a notification and an email. The person who assigned it does not get one.

No reminders are sent for completed or cancelled follow-ups.

### Turn reminders on or off

Each person can choose for themselves. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **My Profile**. Find the **Notifications** card. See [Profile](/settings/profile).

- **Follow-up reminders**: reminders and due-time alerts, by notification and email.
- **Follow-up assigned to me**: when a teammate gives you a follow-up.

Both are on when you start.

## Who can see and change follow-ups

The workspace owner and admins can see and change every follow-up. For everyone else, it depends on their [role](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions). In the role, find the **Follow-ups** row. It has three boxes:

| Permission | See | Schedule new ones | Complete, change or delete |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **View Own** | Follow-ups assigned to them or created by them | Yes | No |
| **View** | The whole team's follow-ups | Yes | No |
| **Manage** | The whole team's follow-ups | Yes | Yes, anyone's |

Ticking **Manage** also ticks **View**.

When follow-ups were added, every existing role was given **View Own**, so your team can start using them straight away. Give **Manage** to people who should be able to mark follow-ups complete or change them.

<Warning>
  With only **View Own** or **View**, a team member can schedule follow-ups but cannot mark them complete, even their own. If your team should close their own follow-ups, give them **Manage**.
</Warning>

You can assign a follow-up only to people who have access to follow-ups.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the customer get a message?">
    No. A follow-up is only a reminder for your team. WaMatrix.io never messages the contact about it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is this different from Appointments?">
    [Appointments](/appointment-and-flow/appointments) are bookings your customers make with you. Follow-ups are reminders your team sets for itself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a follow-up repeat every week?">
    No. Each follow-up happens once. Schedule a new one. Or, instead of marking it complete, use **Reschedule → Next week, same time**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I mark my follow-up as complete?">
    You need the **Manage** permission to complete, change or delete a follow-up. Without it, the menu only shows **View details**. Ask the person who manages your team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why don't I see a follow-up I created?">
    The list and calendar show follow-ups assigned to you. If you created one for a teammate and did not include yourself, find it in the contact's **Follow-ups** tab. If you can see the whole team's follow-ups, choose **Everyone** under **Assigned to**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did I get a reminder late, or not at all?">
    Check that **Follow-up reminders** is on in **My Profile → Notifications**. Reminders can arrive up to a minute late. If a reminder is more than 6 hours late, it is not sent.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When I click Chat, it says the chat isn't assigned to me. What do I do?">
    You can open a chat only if it is assigned to you or your team. Ask an admin to assign it to you. If you see "This contact has no chat yet", nobody has messaged with this contact yet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to follow-ups if I delete a contact?">
    Deleting a contact also deletes their follow-ups.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
