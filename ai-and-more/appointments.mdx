When a customer books an appointment through your [WhatsApp booking form](/ai-and-more/whatsapp-flows#appointment-booking-flows), it appears here. You can view and manage all appointments from this page.

## Appointment list

In appointment page, you can see your Upcoming, Today, and Completed bookings. You can switch between List and Calendar view, and use the tabs and Filters to find the bookings you need.

## Appointment statuses

| Status | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | The appointment is booked. |
| **Confirmed** | You have confirmed the appointment. |
| **Completed** | The appointment is finished. |
| **Cancelled** | The appointment was cancelled. The time slot becomes available again. |
| **No Show** | The customer did not attend the appointment. |

## Appointment details

Click an appointment to view its details. You can see the appointment date and time, the channel, the booking form, and any notes. If the customer filled in extra fields in the booking form, their answers are also shown here.

## Updating an appointment

Use the booking's Actions menu to change its status to Scheduled, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled, or No Show.

<Note>
You can only change the booking status here. You cannot change the appointment time, edit the booking details, or create a new booking. Customers can reschedule their appointments through WhatsApp.
</Note>

## Setting your available times

The times a customer can pick come from two settings working together.

- **Business hours** — Set your business opening and closing times, and add any closed dates. This is a workspace [Settings](/settings/business-hours) and only needs to be set once.
- **Booking settings** — Set the appointment duration, the number of bookings allowed per time slot, and how far in advance customers can book. These settings are configured separately for each booking form.

## Confirmation message

In Appointment Settings, you can choose an approved template to send when a customer books an appointment. Select None if you don't want to send a confirmation message.

<Figure
  src="/ai-and-more/img/appointment/appointment-setting.png"

## Reminders and rescheduling

You can also choose a reminder template in Settings. Customers can use the reminder message to reschedule or cancel their appointment directly in WhatsApp.

A customer can have only one upcoming appointment for each booking form. To book another one, they must first cancel or reschedule the current appointment. Past dates and times cannot be booked.

## Limits

Your plan limits the number of **booking forms**, **upcoming bookings**, and **monthly bookings** you can have. This page shows how much of each limit you have used.
