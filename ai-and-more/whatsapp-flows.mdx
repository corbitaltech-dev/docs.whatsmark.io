A WhatsApp Flow is a form that opens inside a WhatsApp chat. Customers can fill in details, choose options, and submit the form without leaving WhatsApp.


## Flow statuses

Every flow shows a status so you know if it is ready to send.

| Status | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Created but not published yet. You can still edit it before publishing. |
| **Published** | Live and ready to send to customers. |
| **Deprecated** | Disabled. The data is still saved, but you can't send it. |
| **Blocked** | Meta has blocked it. |
| **Throttled** | Meta is limiting it because of high error rates. |

## Building a flow

Click **Create Flow**, enter details.

- **Name** — for your own reference.
- **WhatsApp Account** — Choose the channel for this flow.
- **Enable live booking** — Enable this for [appointment booking](#appointment-booking-flows). Leave it off for a normal form.
- **Categories** — what the flow is for, like Lead Generation or Survey.

![The New WhatsApp Flow form with fields for name, WhatsApp account, an Enable live booking toggle, and categories](/ai-and-more/img/whatsapp-flows/flow-form.png)

A flow is made up of one or more form pages. Customers fill in one page, tap Next, and then move to the next page.

The builder has three sections: the list of form pages on the left, the page you are editing in the middle, and a phone preview on the right.

Drag a field onto a screen to add it. The field types are:

- **Text** — a single line.
- **Text Area** — a longer, multi-line answer.
- **Phone** — a phone number.
- **Date** — a date picker.
- **Dropdown** — select one from a list.
- **Radio** — select one from a few options.
- **Checkboxes** — select more than one.
- **Opt-in** — a check box for consent.

Each screen has a button at the bottom — **Continue** to move to the next screen, or **Submit** on the last screen to finish.

When your flow is ready, click Publish to send it to Meta for approval. If Meta finds any issues, the flow stays as a draft so you can fix them and publish it again.

<Note>
If you no longer want to use a published flow, Deprecate it. You can only Delete a flow that has never been published. Published flows can only be deprecated.
</Note>

## Start from a ready-made flow

You do not have to build from scratch. Click **Flow Library** to see ready-made flows you can copy:

![Flow Library](/ai-and-more/img/whatsapp-flows/flow-library.png)

Select one and **Use This** copies it into your workspace as a draft you can change and publish.

## Sending a flow

Once a flow is published, there are five ways to send it to a customer.

- From a contact's row, choose **Send flow**.
- In [live chat](/messaging/live-chat), use the **Send form** button under the message box.

## Submissions

When a customer fills in a flow and submits it, their answers are saved.

Open **Submissions** to see everyone who submitted the flow. You can view their name, phone number, status, and submission date. Click on name to see all the answers, or export the list as a CSV or Excel file.

## Appointment booking flows

Turn on Enable Live Booking to create an appointment booking form. Customers can see available dates and times, book an appointment, reschedule, or cancel it directly in WhatsApp.

You can configure the booking form using these two options:

- **Customize form** — Change the text shown to customers, such as the date and time labels.
- **Booking settings** — Set the appointment duration, the number of bookings allowed per time slot, and how far in advance customers can book.

## Flow limits

Your plan limits how many flows you can create. If you reach the limit, you won't be able to create or copy a new flow until you upgrade your plan or delete a draft flow.
