Templates can include text, buttons, cards, and files, allowing you to deliver consistent messages across Chat, Campaigns, Flows, and Reply Bots.

## How Instagram templates work

Save frequently used messages as templates and reuse them whenever needed. Instead of creating the same message repeatedly, select a saved template to simplify communication and improve workflow efficiency.

## No Meta approval required

Unlike [WhatsApp message templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates), Instagram templates do not require Meta approval. Once saved, they are ready to use across supported WaMatrix.io features.

<Note>
You can send an Instagram template only while **24 hours open window**. After that, you must wait for them to message you again. This is Meta's rule, not a WaMatrix.io one. Meta explains it in the [Instagram messaging guide](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api).
</Note>

## Create a template

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Instagram templates">
  In the sidebar, open **Instagram → Templates**. Then click **Add Template**.

  A list called **Select an Instagram account** opens. Click the account the template is for.
</Step>

<Step title="Fill in the details">
  The form has three steps: **Details**, **Content** and **Review**. On the **Details** step, fill in:

  - **Template name**. Only you and your team see this name. Customers don't.
  - **Instagram account**. The template can be sent from this account only.
  - **Message type**. Choose **Text**, **Quick replies**, **Buttons**, **Carousel** or **Media**. Each type is explained [below](#message-types).

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /messaging/img/instagram/instagram-template-details.png — The Details step of the New Instagram Template form, with Template name, Instagram account and Message type fields, the three-step list (Details, Content, Review) on the left and the Live Preview on the right */}

  <Warning>
  You can't change the message type after you save the template. To use a different type, create a new template.
  </Warning>

  Click **Continue**.
</Step>

<Step title="Write the content">
  On the **Content** step, write what the customer receives. What you see here depends on the message type. The **Live Preview** on the right shows how the message looks in Instagram.

  To put a person's name or other details in the message, use variables. See [Variables](#variables).

  Click **Continue**.
</Step>

<Step title="Check and save">
  The **Review** step shows the name, account, type and a short summary. Check everything.

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /messaging/img/instagram/instagram-template-review.png — The Review step showing Name, Account and Type in a summary box, the note about the 24-hour rule, and the Create template button */}

  Click **Create template**. When you edit a template later, the button says **Save changes**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
**Instagram → Templates → Add Template** → choose the account → **Details** → **Content** → **Review** → **Create template**.
</Check>

## Message types

There are five types. Each one suits a different kind of message.

### Text

A plain message, such as a greeting or an order update.

Write your message in the **Message** box. It can be up to **1,000 bytes** long. Most English letters use 1 byte. An emoji uses up to 4 bytes, and letters in some languages, such as Hindi, use 3. The counter under the box shows how much you have used.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /messaging/img/instagram/instagram-template-text.png — The Content step for a Text template, with a Message box containing "Hi {{1}}, how can we help you today?", the byte counter and the Add variable button */}

### Quick replies

A message with up to **13** short answers under it. The customer taps one answer instead of typing. The answers disappear after the customer taps one.

Click **Add quick reply** to add an answer. For each answer, fill in:

- **Quick reply**. The text on the answer, up to **20** characters.
- **When tapped**. What happens when the customer taps it. See [What happens when someone taps a button](#what-happens-when-someone-taps-a-button).

{/* SCREENSHOT: /messaging/img/instagram/instagram-template-quick-replies.png — The Quick replies section with two answers, "Track my order" and "Talk to us", each with a When tapped list, and the counter showing 2 of 13 */}

### Buttons

A message with up to **3** buttons under it. Unlike quick replies, the buttons stay visible after a tap. Click **Add button** to add a button.

- The message text can be up to **640** characters.
- Each **Button** title can be up to **20** characters.
- Choose what each button does in **When tapped**.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /messaging/img/instagram/instagram-template-buttons.png — The Buttons section with Button 1 set to Open a website and a Website link field, and Button 2 set to Start a flow with a Flow to start list */}

### Carousel

A row of up to **10** cards. The customer swipes from one card to the next. Use it to show products, offers or services.

Click **Add card** to add a card. Each card has:

- **Image** *(optional)*. Click **Add image** and pick a picture from your [Media Library](/media/media-library).
- **Title** *(required)*. Up to **80** characters.
- **Subtitle** *(optional)*. Up to **80** characters.
- **Card link** *(optional)*. A website link that opens when the customer taps the card's image or title. It must start with **https://**.
- **Buttons on this card** *(optional)*. Up to **3** buttons. They work the same way as in a [Buttons](#buttons) template.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /messaging/img/instagram/instagram-template-carousel.png — The Carousel cards section showing card 1 with an image, Title "Summer collection", a Subtitle, a Card link and one button, plus the Add card button and the counter 1 of 10 */}

### Media

Upload a single file, such as an image, video, audio clip, or PDF. Select the **File Type**, then upload a file from your device or choose an existing file from your [Media Library](/media/media-library).

| File type | Formats WaMatrix.io accepts | Largest size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Image** | PNG, JPEG or GIF | 8 MB |
| **Video** | MP4, MOV, WebM, OGG or AVI | 25 MB |
| **Audio** | AAC, M4A or WAV | 25 MB |
| **PDF file** | PDF only | 25 MB |

You can also add a **Caption**. This is optional text. WaMatrix.io sends it as a separate message, right after the file.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /messaging/img/instagram/instagram-template-media.png — The File section with File type set to Image, an uploaded picture, and the optional Caption box below it */}

<Note>
Most limits on this page come from Meta. Meta lists them in its guides for [quick replies](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api/quick-replies), [buttons](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api/button-template), [carousels](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api/generic-template) and [text and files](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api).
</Note>

## What happens when someone taps a button

Each quick reply and button has a **When tapped** setting that determines what happens when a customer interacts with it. You can choose from the following actions:

- **Quick reply (default):** Sends the selected button text to your inbox as if the customer had typed it. If the text matches a keyword in one of your [automations](/automation/overview), the corresponding automation responds automatically.
- **Send a message:** Automatically sends a predefined response when the customer taps the button. Enter your message in the **Message to send** field.
- **Start a flow:** Starts a selected flow as soon as the customer taps the button. Choose the flow from the **Flow to start** list. Only active flows for the connected Instagram account are available.
- **Open a website:** Opens a web page within Instagram when the customer taps the button. Enter the destination URL in the **Website link** field. The URL must start with `https://`. This action is available for buttons and carousel buttons, but not for quick replies.

## Variables

A variable is a blank space in your message that is filled in later. It is written as a number in double curly brackets, such as `{{1}}`. For example, `Hi {{1}}` can become `Hi Priya`.

- Click **Add variable** to add the next variable where your cursor is.
- Number them in order with no gaps: `{{1}}`, `{{2}}`, `{{3}}`.
- You can use variables in the message text, the media caption, and the card titles and subtitles.

When your template has variables, a **Sample values** box appears. Type an example for each one, such as a name. The sample is only for the preview. Customers never see it.

You fill in the real values each time you send the template. You can type fixed text or choose a [merge field](/messaging/merge-fields), such as the contact's name.

## Where you can use a template

- **Chat.** Click on the **Send template** button in the message box. The **Send Instagram template** window opens. Choose the template, fill in any variables, and send it. See [Chat](/messaging/live-chat).
- **Campaigns.** Choose **Template Campaign** and pick the template. Only people who messaged you in the last 24 hours receive it. See [Campaigns](/messaging/campaigns).
- **Flows.** Add the **Send Template** step to a flow. See [Basic flow steps](/automation/flow-steps/basic).
- **Reply Bots.** A Reply Bot can send a saved template when someone mentions you in a story or replies to your story. See [Reply Bots](/automation/reply-bots). *(Needs a paid plan.)*

For a template with a file or carousel images, you can pick a different file when you send it. The new file is used for that message only.

<Info>
You can't use a template to answer a comment. When a Reply Bot answers a comment with a direct message (DM), WaMatrix.io sends it as plain text only.
</Info>

## Manage your templates

The **Instagram Templates** page lists all your templates. It shows the **Template Name**, **Type**, **Account** and when each one was **Updated**.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /messaging/img/instagram/instagram-templates-list.png — The Instagram Templates page with the Add Template button, a list of templates showing Template Name, Type, Account and Updated, and a row menu open with Edit and Delete */}

- **Filter the list.** Open the filters to show one **Account** or one **Type**. Choose **All accounts** or **All types** to see everything again.
- **Edit a template.** Open the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of the row and click **Edit**. You can change everything except the message type.
- **Delete a template.** Open the three-dot menu (⋮) and click **Delete**. A campaign that has not sent yet can't send it. Flows and Reply Bots that send it stop sending it.
