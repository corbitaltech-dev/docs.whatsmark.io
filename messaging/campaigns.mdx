A campaign sends the same message to many contacts at once. Use it for an offer, an announcement, or a follow-up to a group of people.

<Note>
Before you start, connect a channel: [WhatsApp](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp), [Messenger](/core-setup/channels/messenger) or [Instagram](/core-setup/channels/instagram). Add **[contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts)**. For a template campaign, have a template ready: an approved [WhatsApp template](/messaging/whatsapp-templates), a [Messenger template](/messaging/messenger-templates) or an [Instagram template](/messaging/instagram-templates).
</Note>


## The two campaign types

You select one of two types when you create a campaign.

### Template campaign

Sends one of your saved **templates**. A template is a ready-made message you save once and send many times. Use it for offers, announcements, and reminders.

- On **WhatsApp**, the template must be approved by Meta first. A template campaign can reach **any** contact on the channel.
- On **Messenger** and **Instagram**, it can only reach people who messaged you recently. See [Messenger campaigns](#messenger-campaigns) and [Instagram campaigns](#instagram-campaigns) below.

### Message campaign

A message you create yourself using text, images, videos, or buttons. On WhatsApp and Instagram, you can send it only to people who messaged you in the [last 24 hours](/getting-started/concepts#two-whatsapp-rules-that-shape-everything). On Messenger, the limit is the last 7 days. No template is needed.

## Creating a campaign

Each platform has its own campaigns page.

1. In the main menu, open the platform you want, for example **WhatsApp**. Then click **Campaigns**.
2. Click **Create Campaign**. A list of your channels opens, such as **Your WhatsApp channels**.
3. Choose the channel to send from. The campaign form opens with that channel already chosen.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /messaging/img/campaign/campaign-add-channel-list.png — WhatsApp → Campaigns with the Create Campaign button clicked, showing the "Your WhatsApp channels" list */}

The form has four steps. A live preview of your message shows on the right as you go.

<Steps>
<Step title="Basics">

    caption="Image shows the basics step of the wizard."
  />

  - **Campaign name** — a name only you see, like *Diwali sale 2026*.
  - **Campaign type** — choose **Template Campaign** or **Message Campaign** (see the two types above).
  - **Channel** — the channel you chose is already filled in, for example **WhatsApp channel**. You can pick another channel of the same platform here.
  - **Template** — for a template campaign, select the [template](/messaging/whatsapp-templates) to send.
</Step>

<Step title="Audience">
  Select the exact contacts who should receive the campaign.

  <Figure
    src="/messaging/img/campaign/campaign-audience.png"
    alt="The audience step with filters for status, source, group and type, a search box, and a contact list with checkboxes and a Select all filtered option"
    caption="Image shows the audience step of the wizard."
  />

  Filter the list with the **[status](/core-setup/statuses)**, **[source](/core-setup/sources)**, **[group](/core-setup/groups)** and **type** filters, or search by name or phone. Select the contacts you want, or use **Select all filtered** to tick everyone in the current filtered list. You need at least one contact to continue.
</Step>

<Step title="Content">
  Write what you want to send.

  <Figure
    src="/messaging/img/campaign/campaign-content.png"
    alt="The content step showing a message body field with merge field support and options to add buttons or media"
    caption="Image shows the content step of the wizard."
  />

  - **Template campaign** — fill in the [template](/messaging/whatsapp-templates)'s variables. If the template has a media header, upload the image or file here. If a **Website URL** button ends in a variable, a field called **Button "*button text*" URL parameter** appears. Type the end part of the link there.
  - **Message campaign** — write a **header** (optional), a **body**, and a **footer** (optional). You can also add up to three reply **buttons**, a single **call-to-action** link, or attach an image, video or document.

  Type `@` in any text field to add a [merge field](/messaging/merge-fields) like the contact's name.
</Step>

<Step title="Schedule">
  Choose when to send it.

  <Figure
    src="/messaging/img/campaign/campaign-schedule.png"
    alt="The schedule step with a choice between Send now and Schedule for later, a date and time picker, and a summary of the campaign"
    caption="Image shows the schedule step of the wizard."
  />

  - **Send now** — the campaign is sent as soon as you create it.
  - **Schedule for later** — select a date and time. Confirm your [timezone](/settings/general) first, so it sends at the right time.

  <Warning>
  Once a campaign starts sending you cannot edit it. Check the summary before you send.
  </Warning>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Messenger campaigns

To send a Messenger campaign, go to **Messenger → Campaigns** and click **Create Campaign**. The steps are the same as [above](#creating-a-campaign).

Messenger has one important difference: **both** message and template campaigns can only reach contacts who messaged your Facebook Page in the **last 7 days**.

## Instagram campaigns

To send an Instagram campaign, go to **Instagram → Campaigns** and click **Create Campaign**. The steps are the same as [above](#creating-a-campaign). *(Instagram needs a paid plan. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).)*

You have the same two types:

- **Template Campaign** — one of your saved [Instagram templates](/messaging/instagram-templates), to people who messaged you in the last 24 hours.
- **Message Campaign** — text, an image, a video or buttons, to people who messaged you in the last 24 hours.

Instagram campaigns have stricter rules than WhatsApp:

- **Who you can reach.** Only people who messaged your Instagram account in the last 24 hours. This is Meta's rule, not WaMatrix.io's. See [Instagram has its own rule](/getting-started/concepts#instagram-has-its-own-rule).
- **No spreadsheet upload.** You cannot upload a CSV or Excel sheet. Instagram finds people by their Instagram account, not by phone number.
- **What you can send.** Text, an image or a video. You can add reply buttons, or one link button. There is no header or footer.
- **No documents.** WaMatrix.io doesn't send documents in Instagram campaigns. In a chat, you can still send a PDF.
- **Text length.** Up to 1,000 bytes (emojis use more). See [Instagram rules to know](/core-setup/channels/instagram#instagram-rules-to-know).

{/* SCREENSHOT: /messaging/img/campaign/campaign-instagram-audience.png — The Audience step for an Instagram campaign, with the notice that Instagram campaigns can only reach people who messaged your Instagram account in the last 24 hours */}

### How Instagram reports results

- **Delivered** means Meta accepted the message. Instagram does not send a separate "delivered" signal.
- **Read** means the person has seen the message.
- A campaign takes some time to reach everyone. If a person's 24 hours run out before their message goes out, it is not sent. Their row shows as failed, with a reason such as **This person has not messaged you in the last 24 hours.**

## Campaign statuses

The status shows the current stage of the campaign.

| Status |  It means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Made but not sent yet. You can still edit it, and send it when you're ready. |
| **Queued** | Scheduled and waiting for its send time. |
| **Preparing** | Getting the list of recipients ready. This lasts a moment. |
| **Sending** | Messages are going out right now. |
| **Paused** | Sending is on hold. Resume it to carry on. |
| **Completed** | Every recipient has been handled. |
| **Failed** | The campaign could not run, often because no one was eligible to receive it. |
| **Cancelled** | You stopped it. Anyone not yet sent to will not be sent to. |

## Campaign reports

Open any campaign to see its report.

<Figure
  src="/messaging/img/campaign/campaign-detail.png"
  alt="A campaign report with cards for total sent, delivered, not received, read and failed, an engagement breakdown chart, and a preview of the message"
  caption="Image shows a campaign report with its delivery figures."
/>

At the top you see the campaign **name**, its **status**, and its **type**. On the right, **Recipients** opens the list of customers in the campaign, and **Delete** removes it.

Below, you can see how many messages were sent, delivered, not received, read, and failed. You can also see a chart of these results and a preview of your message.

While a campaign is running, you can **Pause**, **Resume**, or **Cancel** it. After it finishes, **Resend Failed** sends the message again only to contacts who did not receive it.

<Note>
Campaign messages don't show up in [Chat](/messaging/live-chat).
</Note>

## Recipients

Click **Recipients** to see everyone in the campaign. You can filter the list by message status (All, Pending, Sent, Delivered, Read, Replied, or Failed) and see the reason if a message failed.

<Figure
  src="/messaging/img/campaign/recipients.png"
  alt="The campaign recipients page with cards for sent, delivered, read, replied, pending and failed, status filter tabs, and a table of contacts with their status and timestamps"
  caption="Image shows the recipients list with the status filter tabs."
/>

Open the **Replied** tab to see all contacts who replied to your campaign. Click **New Campaign for Replied** to create a new campaign for only those contacts.

## Common reasons for failed messages

If a message fails, open the campaign's **Recipients** list. The **Error** column shows the reason Meta gave. It usually starts with Meta's error number, for example **131026**.

| Error number | What it means and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| **131026** | The message could not be delivered. The number may not be on WhatsApp, or the person may use an old version of WhatsApp. Check the number. |
| **131047** | More than 24 hours have passed since this person last wrote to you. A message campaign can only reach people who wrote in the last 24 hours. Use a template campaign instead. |
| **131049** | Meta held back this marketing message, so that people don't get too many. Try again later. |
| **131050** | This person has chosen to stop marketing messages on WhatsApp. |
| **131021** | You tried to send a message to your own sending number. Check the number. |
| **131031** or **368** | Meta has restricted your WhatsApp Business account because of a policy problem. Check the account status in Meta's WhatsApp Manager. |
| **131042** | There is a problem with your payment method at Meta. Check the payment method in Meta Business Manager. |
| **131048** | Meta has limited how many messages your number can send. Send fewer messages, and only to people who want them. |
| **131056** | Too many messages went to the same person in a short time. Wait a short time, then try again. |
| **131053** | Meta could not use the file attached to your message. Check that the file still exists, then try again. |
| **131057** | Your WhatsApp Business account is in maintenance mode. Try again later. |
| **This person has not messaged you in the last 24 hours.** | Instagram only. Their 24 hours ran out before the message went out. You can reach them again after they message you. |

<Note>
For any other number, see Meta's [error codes](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/support/error-codes) page.
</Note>

## Campaign limits

Your plan includes a number of campaigns each month. Message campaigns and template campaigns count toward the same monthly allowance. The **Campaigns (this month)** counter at the top of the Campaigns page shows how many you have used. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
