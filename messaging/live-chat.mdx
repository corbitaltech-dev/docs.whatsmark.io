When a customer messages you on WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram, it shows up here instantly, and anyone from your team can reply. Everyone can work from the same screen, so nothing gets missed.

<Note>
  Before you start, connect at least one **WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram channel**. Without one, the screen stays empty because there are no messages to receive. To set one up, see [Connecting WhatsApp](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp), [Connecting Messenger](/core-setup/channels/messenger) or [Connecting Instagram](/core-setup/channels/instagram).
</Note>

## What you see on the chat screen

To open your chats, click **Inbox** in the main menu. The screen is split into four parts:

1. **Sidebar** — filters to show only the chats you want.
2. **Chat list** — all your conversations, newest at the top.
3. **Message thread** — the chat you're currently reading, with the reply box at the bottom.
4. **Contact panel** — details about the person you're talking to. It opens when you want it.

## How a chat starts

A chat can begin in two ways.

### A customer messages you

This is how most chats start. The customer messages you first and then it shows up in your list, and you can open it and reply.

If the customer is not in your contacts, they are added automatically. You do not need to add them.

### You message first

You can also start the conversation yourself. Click **New chat** at the top of the sidebar (owners and admins only) and search for a contact by name, phone, or email.

After you pick a contact, one of these happens:

- If you already have a chat going with them, it opens and you can type as normal.
- If there is no chat going yet, you are asked to pick a template to start one.

<Warning>
On **WhatsApp** you cannot send a plain message to start a chat. You have to use an **approved template**, so keep at least one ready template before you try. See [WhatsApp Templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates) to create one and get it approved. See [Meta's policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messages/send-messages#customer-service-windows) for more details.

On **Messenger** you cannot message first at all, so wait for the customer to message you.

On **Instagram** you cannot message first either. **New chat** only offers WhatsApp and Messenger. Wait for the customer to send you a direct message (DM), which is a private message on Instagram.
</Warning>

## Perform actions on a message

Hover over any message to open its menu:

<Figure
  src="/messaging/img/chat/chat-message-menu.png"
  alt="An open chat with the per-message menu showing Reply, Copy, and Delete options on a message bubble"
  caption="Image shows the menu that opens on a single message."
/>

- **Reply** — quote it in your answer. Click a quoted message to jump to the original.
- **Copy** — copy the text.
- **Delete** — remove it from your inbox.

<Note>
Deleting message only removes the message from your screen. The customer still sees it on their phone. You have 2 minutes to restore it.
</Note>

### Edited messages

Sometimes a message changes after it was sent. You then see the new text with a small **Edited** label next to the time.

- **WhatsApp:** if your business edits a message in the WhatsApp Business app, the new text shows here. When a customer edits their own message, you keep seeing the first version. Meta does not send customer edits to WaMatrix.io.
- **Instagram:** when a customer edits a message, the new text shows here.

### When a message fails

If a message cannot be sent, a short note in red appears under it. The note says why it failed and, where possible, what to do next.

## The reply time limit

WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram do not let you message a customer any time you want. You can only reply for a set time after they message you. This stops businesses from spamming people.

### On WhatsApp: 24 hours

After a customer messages you, you get 24 hours to reply. You can send anything you want in that time. A countdown at the top of the chat shows how much time is left. It turns amber in the last hour and red in the last few minutes.

When the time runs out, the reply box goes away. In its place you get a **Send template** button. Use it to send an approved template and reach them again.

<Figure
  src="/messaging/img/chat/chat-screen-expired.png"
  alt="A WhatsApp chat after 24 hours: a notice in place of the reply box says the 24-hour reply window has closed, with a Send template button on the right"
  caption="Image shows the chat when the messaging window has expired."
/>

### On Messenger: 7 days

On Messenger you get 7 days. After that the reply box closes too. There is no template option here, so you have to wait for the customer to message you again.

### On Instagram: 24 hours

On Instagram you also get 24 hours. When the time runs out, the reply box closes. There is no template to reach them again. You can reply once the customer sends a new message. See [Instagram chats](#instagram-chats) below.

<Note>
The countdown restarts every time the customer sends you a new message. See [Meta's policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messages/send-messages#customer-service-windows)
</Note>

## Replying

Type your reply in the box at the bottom and press Enter to send.

Above the box you get basic formatting: bold, italic, strikethrough, and lists.

### The tools under the box

<Figure
  src="/messaging/img/chat/chat-composer-annotated.png"
  alt="The reply box with its formatting bar on top, nine tool icons below numbered 1 to 9, and the microphone button on the right numbered 10"
  caption="Image shows the reply box with its formatting bar and tool icons."
/>

1. **Emoji** — add an emoji.
2. **Attach** — send an image, video, audio file, or document. This opens your [Media Library](/media/media-library), where you can upload a new file or pick one you have already used.
3. **Location** — send a location pin. (Not on Instagram.)
4. **Contact card** — send contact details to the customer. (Not on Instagram.) You can pick one of your saved contacts, or type in new details by hand. Details you type here are only sent in the message and are not saved to your contacts.
5. **Canned reply** — pick a saved reply you use often. You can also type `/` to open this. See [Canned Replies](/core-setup/canned-replies).
6. **Run a flow** — start an automation in this chat. See [Chatbot Flows](/automation/overview).
7. **Send form** — send a form the customer can fill in, like a booking form. (WhatsApp only) See [WhatsApp Flows](/appointment-and-flow/whatsapp-flows).
8. **AI assist** — rewrite or translate your message with AI. See [AI assist](#ai-assist) below.
9. **Send template** — send a template. See [WhatsApp Templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates), [Messenger Templates](/messaging/messenger-templates) and [Instagram Templates](/messaging/instagram-templates).
10. **Voice notes** — When the box is empty, the send button turns into a microphone. Click it to record, then send it as a voice note. (Not on Instagram.)


## AI assist

Click the sparkle icon to have AI change your message before you send it.

You get:

- **Quick fixes** — improve the writing, fix grammar, make it formal or casual, or make it shorter.
- **Translate** — turn your message into another language.
- **Your own prompts** — saved instructions you write yourself. See [AI Prompts](/core-setup/ai-prompts).

After AI rewrites your message you can **Undo** it or **Regenerate** for a different version.

A meter shows how many AI tokens you have left from your plan's limit.

## Instagram chats

Instagram chats work like your other chats, with a few differences. These are Instagram rules set by Meta, not WaMatrix.io rules. Meta explains them in its [Instagram messaging guide](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api). To connect an account, see [Connecting Instagram](/core-setup/channels/instagram).

- **Reply within 24 hours.** You can reply for 24 hours after the customer's last message. After that, the reply box closes until they write again. There is no template to reopen the chat.
- **The customer writes first.** You cannot start a new chat on Instagram.
- **Text length.** One message can hold up to 1,000 bytes of text. Most letters use 1 byte, but an emoji uses 4. A counter under the box shows how many bytes you have used.
- **Files.** You can send images (PNG, JPEG or GIF, up to 8 MB). You can also send videos (MP4, OGG, AVI, MOV or WebM), audio (AAC, M4A or WAV) and PDF files, up to 25 MB each.
- **Captions.** Instagram cannot put a file and text in one message. If you add a caption, it arrives as a separate message just after the file.
- **Not available.** Location, contact card and voice notes are hidden in Instagram chats.
- **Templates.** **Send template** sends one of your saved Instagram templates. See [Instagram Templates](/messaging/instagram-templates).
- **Read ticks.** Instagram only tells you when the customer has seen your message. It then shows as read. There is no "delivered" tick on Instagram.
- **Quoted replies.** **Reply** quotes the message only for accounts connected with **Continue with Facebook**. For other accounts, your answer arrives as a normal message without the quote.

### Special Instagram messages

Some Instagram messages show as a small card in the chat:

- **Mentioned you in their story** — the customer tagged your account in their story.
- **Replied to your story** — the customer answered one of your stories.
- **Shared a post** or **Shared a reel** — the customer sent you a post or reel. Click the link on the card to open it.

Instagram stories disappear after a while. When a story is gone, the card says **Story no longer available**.

## Searching in a chat

Long chats are hard to scroll through. Click the search icon at the top of the chat to search inside it.

Type at least two letters. Matching messages appear in a list with your search term highlighted. Click one to jump straight to it in the chat.

## The contact panel

Click the person icon at the top right to open details about the customer you are talking to. It has three tabs.

### Contact

Their name, phone, email, source, type, who they are assigned to, labels, and the channel they use. Click **Edit** to change any of it without leaving the chat.

See [Managing Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts) for what each field means.

### Notes

Private notes about this customer that only your team can see. The customer never sees them. Use them to record things like "asked for a bulk order quote".

### Media

Every photo, video, file and audio clip shared in this chat, in one place. Filter by type, click any image to open it full size, or download a file.

## Filtering your chats

Once messages start coming in, the list gets long. Here is how to find what you need.

<Figure
  src="/messaging/img/chat/chat-inbox-layout.png"
  alt="The Chat sidebar with All, Mine, Unassigned, Not Replied, Status and Team Inbox filters, next to the chat list showing contact rows with status labels, avatars and last-message previews"
  caption="Image shows the Chat sidebar filters next to the chat list."
/>

### Filters in the sidebar

The sidebar splits your chats into groups. Each one shows a live count, so you can see how much is waiting.

- **All** — every chat you can see.
- **Mine** — chats assigned to you.
- **Unassigned** — chats nobody has picked up yet.
- **Not Replied** — a switch that shows only chats where the customer is still waiting for an answer.
- **Status** — your contact statuses, like New Lead or Customer. See [Statuses](/core-setup/statuses).
- **Team Inbox** — chats sent to a team you belong to, like Sales or Support.

Click a filter again to turn it off.

### Search, sort and channel

Above the chat list:

- **Search** — type a name or number to find a chat.
- **Sort** — switch between newest and oldest first.
- **Channel** — show chats from one WhatsApp number, Facebook Page or Instagram account instead of all of them.

If the Inbox opens with one platform already chosen, a filter chip such as **All Instagram** shows at the bottom of the sidebar. Click its **x** to see all your chats again.

### What you see in the chat list

Every row gives you a quick summary:

- Photo of the customer, with a small icon showing if they used WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram.
- Name and their status label.
- Time of the last message.
- On the right, a small photo of the team member handling the chat, and the profile photo of the channel it came in on.
- One line of the last message.
- A coloured badge with the number of unread messages.

## Assigning chats

### Assign it to a person

Open a chat and use the assign dropdown at the top. Tick one or more team members, then click **Apply**. Their photos then show on the chat row so everyone knows who is handling it.

### Assign it to a team

You can also group people into teams like Sales or Support, and send a chat to the whole team instead of one person. Everyone in that team sees it in their sidebar.

To create a team, click the **+** next to Team Inbox in the sidebar, give it a name, and pick the members.

### Closing, clearing and deleting

- **Close** — close the chat you have open and go back to the empty screen.
- **Clear chat** — delete all the messages but keep the chat.
- **Delete conversation** — remove the whole chat from your inbox.

<Note>
Only owners and admins can assign chats or manage teams. Other actions depend on your role — see [Roles & Permissions](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions).

Owners and admins see every chat. Everyone else only sees chats assigned to them or sent to their team.
</Note>

### Stop the AI assistant in a chat

If an AI assistant is answering a customer, a red robot icon with a line through it shows at the top of the chat. Its label is **Stop AI for this chat**. On a phone, find it in the **...** menu. Click it and the assistant stops answering that customer right away. See [AI Assistants](/automation/ai-assistants). *(AI assistants need a paid plan.)*

## Looking at older chats

Click the calendar icon at the top of the sidebar to pick a date range. The chat list and the open chat both jump to that period. Clear the date chip to come back to now.

<Note>
How far back you can go depends on your plan. Dates outside your limit are greyed out in the picker, with a link to upgrade.
</Note>

## Live updates

You never need to refresh the page. New messages, delivery ticks, unread counts and assignment changes all appear as they happen.
