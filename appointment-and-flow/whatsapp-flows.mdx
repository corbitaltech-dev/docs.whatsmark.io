A WhatsApp Flow is a form that opens inside a WhatsApp chat. Customers can fill in details, choose options, and submit the form without leaving WhatsApp.

## Building a flow

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the flow">
  Click **Create Flow** and fill in the details.

  - **Name** — for your own reference.
  - **WhatsApp Account** — choose the channel for this flow.
  - **Enable live booking** — turn this on for [appointment booking](#appointment-booking-flows). Leave it off for a normal form.
  - **Categories** — Select a flow category, such as Lead Generation or Survey. You cannot create a new category.

  <Figure
    src="/appointment-and-flow/img/whatsapp-flows/flow-form.png"
    alt="The New WhatsApp Flow form with fields for name, WhatsApp account, an Enable live booking toggle, and categories"
    caption="Image shows the New WhatsApp Flow form."
  />
</Step>

<Step title="Design its screens">
  Click a flow name to open the builder.

  The builder has three sections: the list of **form pages** on the left, the **page editor** in the middle, and a **phone preview** on the right.

  <Figure
    src="/appointment-and-flow/img/whatsapp-flows/form-creation.png"
    alt="The WhatsApp Flow builder with a screens list and field palette on the left, the screen being edited in the middle, and a phone preview on the right"
    caption="Image shows the flow builder."
  />

Drag a field to a form page to add it.

  - **Text** — A single-line text field.
  - **Text Area** — A multi-line text field.
  - **Phone** — A phone number field.
  - **Date** — A date picker.
  - **Dropdown** — Select one option from a list.
  - **Radio** — Select one option from a few options.
  - **Checkboxes** — Select one or more option.
  - **Opt-in** — A checkbox for customer consent.

Each field includes:

  - **Label** — The name shown to the customer, such as *Your name*.
  - **Name (answer key)** — A unique name used to identify the answer. You can see it later in [submissions](#submissions) and [field mapping](#field-mapping).
  - **Required** — Turn it on to make the field mandatory.

  Each form page has a button at the bottom. Continue opens the next page, and Submit finishes the form.
</Step>

<Step title="Publish">
  Click **Publish** to send the flow to Meta for approval. If Meta returns any issues, the flow will remain in Draft status. Click the flow name to view the errors, fix the issues, and publish the flow again.
</Step>
</Steps>

You do not have to build from scratch. Click **Flow Collection** to see ready-made flows you can copy:

## The flows list

The WhatsApp Flows page shows all your flows. Each flow shows its **name**, **channel**, **status**, **category**, and an **Active** switch to turn it on or off.

<Figure

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Flow Builder** | Open the builder to edit the flow.|
| **View** | View the flow details. |
| **Edit details** | Change the name, account, or categories. |
| **Mapping** | Save answers into contact records. See [Field mapping](#field-mapping). |
| **Submissions** | See who [submitted](#submissions) the flow. |
| **Publish** | Publish the draft flow. |
| **Deprecate** | Deprecate a published flow. |
| **Duplicate** | Make a copy of the flow. |
| **Delete** | Remove a draft flow. |

Appointment booking flows have a few extra actions — **Customize form**, **Booking settings**, **Booking messages**, and **View bookings**. See [Appointment booking flows](#appointment-booking-flows).

## Flow statuses

Every flow shows a status so you know if it is ready to send.

| Status | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | The flow is not published yet. You can still edit it. |
| **Published** | The flow is ready to use. |
| **Deprecated** | The flow is no longer active, but its data is still available. |
| **Blocked** | Meta has blocked the flow. |
| **Throttled** | Meta is temporarily limiting the flow because of too many errors. |

## Field mapping

The answers people give in a flow can go straight into your contact records. From the flow table `⋮` menu, choose **Mapping** to set it up.

For each field in the flow, choose what to do with its answer:

- **Skip** — do not save it.
- **Contact field** — save it to a built-in contact field, like name or email.
- **Custom field** — save it to one of your [custom fields](/core-setup/custom-fields).
- **Label** — add a label to the contact.

<Warning>
Set up mapping before you send the flow. Answers only save to contacts once mapping is done — anything submitted before that is not added automatically.
</Warning>

## Sending a flow

Once a flow is published, there are two ways to send it to a customer.

- From a [contact's](/contacts/managing-contacts#actions-on-a-single-contact) row, choose **Send flow**.
- In [live chat](/messaging/live-chat), use the **Send form** button under the message box.

## Submissions

When a customer fill form and submits it, their answers are saved, and a card in the [chat](/messaging/live-chat).

From the flow table `⋮` menu, choose **Submissions** to see everyone who filled it in — their name, phone number, status, and the date. Click a row to read all of their answers, or use **Export** to download the list as a CSV file.

## Appointment booking flows

Turn on **Enable Live Booking** when you [create a flow](#building-a-flow) to turn it into a booking form. Customers can book, reschedule, or cancel an appointment right inside WhatsApp.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Customize form](#customize-form) | Edit the form's wording and questions. |
| [Booking settings](#booking-settings) | Set slot length, capacity, and booking window. |
| [Booking messages](#booking-messages) | Override the confirmation, cancellation, or reminder message for this form. |
| [View bookings](#view-bookings) | Jump to this form's bookings on the Appointments page. |

### Customize form

Change the form's text and questions, then click **Save & sync** to update it on WhatsApp.

<Figure
  src="/appointment-and-flow/img/whatsapp-flows/appointment-form.png"
  alt="The appointment booking designer with fields for the booking screen labels and a live phone preview of the booking form"
  caption="Image shows the appointment booking designer with a live preview."
/>

**Booking screen**

- **Screen title**, **Book button**, **Date label**, **Time label** — your own wording.
- **Notes field** — on by default for new forms, so customers can add a note. Turn it off, or rename its **Notes label**.
- **Default service** — auto-tag every booking with one of your [appointment services](/appointment-and-flow/appointments), so customers don't have to pick one. Set to **None** to skip.

**Extra fields**

Add up to 3 extra questions. Each one needs a **Field name** (lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only, starting with a letter — used internally, not shown to the customer), a **Label** (what the customer sees), a **Type** (Text, Text area, Phone, Email, or Number), and whether it's **Required**.

**Screening questions**

Ask a question before the customer can continue in the booking form. Based on the answer, they either continue to book, or see a message instead and can't continue.

<Figure
  src="/appointment-and-flow/img/whatsapp-flows/screening-questions.png"
  alt="The Screening questions section with a question, its saved answer name, a list of answers each marked Can book or Cannot book, and the Confirmation screen fields below it"
  caption="Image shows a screening question set to block existing customers from booking, plus the Confirmation screen fields."
/>

1. Turn on the switch at the top to use this.
2. Click **Add question** and type your **Question**, such as "Are you an existing customer?"
3. Type a short name in **Save answer as** — start with a letter, then use only lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores, such as `customer_status`. You can use it later as a [merge field](/messaging/merge-fields).
4. Click **Add answer** for each option the customer can pick, such as "Yes" and "No".
5. For every answer, choose **Can book** or **Cannot book**:
   - **Can book** — the customer continues to the booking form.
   - **Cannot book** — a message box appears; write what the customer sees instead, such as "Sorry, only existing customers can book appointments online." Leave it blank to use a default message.
6. Repeat with **Add question** for as many questions as you need.

<Note>
If you turn Screening questions on or off, click **Save & sync**, then publish the form again if it's already live.
</Note>

**Confirmation screen**

Shown right after a customer books. It has a title and a message, plus two buttons — one to reschedule, one to cancel.

- **Screen title** — the top bar text.
- **Heading** — the message below it, such as "You're booked!"
- **Reschedule link** — text for the reschedule button, such as "Reschedule." Clicking it opens a new date/time picker; submitting it updates their appointment to the new time.
- **Cancel link** — text for the cancel button, such as "Cancel appointment." Clicking it cancels the appointment immediately.
- **Reschedule screen title** — the top bar text on that date/time picker screen.


### Booking settings

This is where you decide what time slots customers see and how far ahead they can book.

<Note>
Your opening hours and holidays are set once, in [Settings → Business Hours](/settings/business-hours) — not here.
</Note>

<Figure
  src="/appointment-and-flow/img/whatsapp-flows/booking-settings.png"
  alt="The Booking settings panel with fields for slot length, capacity per slot, custom time blocks, and the earliest and latest a customer can book"
  caption="Image shows the Booking settings panel, including custom time blocks."
/>

- **Slot length (minutes)** — how long one appointment takes. Type `30` and customers see a new time slot every 30 minutes.
- **Capacity per slot** — how many customers can book the same time. Type `1` if only one customer can book each time slot.
- **Earliest booking** — how many days from now customers can start booking. `0` lets them book for today.
- **Latest booking** — how many days from now booking stops. `30` keeps it open for one month.
- **Custom time blocks** *(optional)* — offer only a few fixed times, like `10:00–11:00 AM`, instead of slots every 30 minutes. Add one, and only your times are shown.

Changes here take effect right away — you don't need to republish the form.

### Booking messages

Your workspace has one global message for each of Confirmation, Cancellation, and Reminder, set under [Appointment settings](/appointment-and-flow/appointments#confirmation-message). Use **Booking messages** here to set a different message just for this one form — it overrides your global setting, only for bookings made through this form.

<Figure
  src="/appointment-and-flow/img/whatsapp-flows/booking-messages.png"
  alt="The Booking messages panel with Confirmation, Cancellation, and Reminder sections, each with an override switch and a choice between Approved template and Plain message"
  caption="Image shows the Booking messages panel with all three overrides turned on."
/>

You can set three messages here:

- **Confirmation** — sent when someone books or reschedules.
- **Cancellation** — sent when a booking is cancelled.
- **Reminder** — sent before the appointment.

For each one:

- Turn on its switch to override the global setting for this form. Leave it off and this form just uses your global message.
- Pick an approved [template](/messaging/whatsapp-templates), or write your own **Plain message**. Reminder only offers a template.

### View bookings

Opens the [Appointments](/appointment-and-flow/appointments) page, filtered to this form's bookings.
