When a customer books an appointment through your [WhatsApp booking form](/appointment-and-flow/whatsapp-flows#appointment-booking-flows), it appears here. You can view and manage all appointments from this page.

## Appointment list

This page shows your Upcoming, Today, and Completed bookings at a glance. Switch between List and Calendar view, and use the tabs and Filters to quickly find the bookings you're looking for.

## Appointment statuses

| Status | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | The appointment is booked. |
| **Confirmed** | You have confirmed the appointment. |
| **Completed** | The appointment is finished. |
| **Cancelled** | The appointment was cancelled. The time slot becomes available again. |
| **No Show** | The customer did not attend the appointment. |

## Appointment details

Open a booking's `⋮` menu and click **View details** to see the contact, status, start and end time, service, channel, booking form, how it was booked (WhatsApp or web link), notes, and any answers the customer filled in.

## Updating an appointment

Use the booking's Actions menu to change its status to Scheduled, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled, or No Show.

<Note>
You can only change the booking status here. You cannot change the appointment time, edit the booking details, or create a new booking. Customers can reschedule their appointments through WhatsApp.
</Note>

## Setting your available times

The times a customer can pick come from two settings working together.

- **Business hours** — Set your business opening and closing times, and add any closed dates. This is a workspace [Settings](/settings/business-hours) and only needs to be set once.
- **Booking settings** — Set the booking times separately for each form. See [Booking settings](/appointment-and-flow/whatsapp-flows#booking-settings).

## Confirmation message

Open the Appointments page's **Settings** to set the message sent when a customer books or reschedules, and the message sent when a booking is cancelled.

<Figure
  src="/appointment-and-flow/img/appointment/appointment-setting.png"
  alt="The Appointment settings panel with a Confirmation section showing a Send for audience dropdown and a template or plain message editor"
  caption="Image shows the Appointment settings panel."
/>

First, choose **Send for** — who this message is for:

- **Both booking sources** — every booking, whether it came in through WhatsApp or your public booking link.
- **Public booking link only** — only bookings made through your public web booking page.
- **WhatsApp flow only** — only bookings made through a WhatsApp booking form.

Then choose the message itself:

- For a WhatsApp booking, you can pick an approved [template](/messaging/whatsapp-templates) or write your own **Plain message**. In a Plain message, type **@** to insert a [merge field](/messaging/merge-fields), like the contact's name or phone number.
- For a booking made through your public web booking link, you can only use a template.

<Note>
To use a different message for each form, set it up under that form's [Booking messages](/appointment-and-flow/whatsapp-flows#booking-messages).
</Note>

## Reminders, public booking link, booking defaults, and services

<Figure
  src="/appointment-and-flow/img/appointment/public-booking-link.png"
  alt="The Reminder, Public booking link, Booking defaults, and Services sections of Appointment settings, showing a copyable web booking URL for channel WP 1, Slot length and Bookings per slot fields, and a list of service name tags"
  caption="Image shows the Reminder, Public booking link, Booking defaults, and Services sections of Appointment settings."
/>

**Reminder**

A reminder is a message sent automatically to the customer shortly before their appointment, so they don't forget it.

- Pick a [template](/messaging/whatsapp-templates) to use as the reminder. Leave it as None to not send one.

**Public booking link**

- A link to a web page where customers can book, without using WhatsApp.
- Customers still enter their WhatsApp number, and get their confirmation there.

<Figure
  src="/appointment-and-flow/img/appointment/public-booking-page.png"
  alt="The public booking page with Date, Available times, Name, WhatsApp number, Service, and Notes fields, and a Book appointment button"
  caption="Image shows the public booking page a customer sees after opening the link."
/>

<Note>
For customers to get a WhatsApp confirmation after booking on this page, set **Send for** to **Both booking sources** or **Public booking link only** in [Confirmation message](#confirmation-message).
</Note>

**Booking defaults**

These control the time slots offered on your public booking link.

- **Slot length** — how long each appointment takes, in minutes.
- **Bookings per slot** — how many customers can book the same time slot.

**Services**

If you offer more than one kind of appointment — like "Consultation" and "Follow-up" — add them here so you can tell them apart on your bookings.

- On your public page, customers see this list and pick one.
- On WhatsApp, there's no picker — instead, set a **Default service** in [Customize form](/appointment-and-flow/whatsapp-flows#customize-form) so every booking through that form is tagged with it automatically.

<Note>
A customer can have only one upcoming appointment at a time per WhatsApp number, across all your booking forms. They must cancel or reschedule it before booking another. Past dates and times can't be booked.
</Note>
