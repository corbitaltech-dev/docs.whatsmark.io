Build your own form for enquiries, bookings, or sign-ups. Share its link in your Instagram bio, an ad, or an email, or put the form right on your website.

Every response is saved as a contact, so new leads land in WaMatrix.io ready for your team. You can also send each customer a WhatsApp message the moment they submit, while you're still fresh in their mind.

<Note>
To send WhatsApp replies, you need a connected WhatsApp number. In WaMatrix.io, a connected number is called a **channel**. See [Channels](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp) to connect yours.
</Note>

## Creating a form

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Form Builder">
  In the sidebar, click **Form Builder**.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Create Form">
  It's at the top right. A panel opens on the right.

  ![The Create Form panel with the hint Give your form a name. You can add and change fields in the next step, and a required Form name field with the example Free consultation request](/appointment-and-flow/img/form-builder/create-form.png)
</Step>

<Step title="Name your form">
  Enter a **Form name**, like *Free consultation request*, and click **Create**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The form editor opens. Your form already asks for **Full name**, **WhatsApp number**, and **Email**, and each answer is already set to save to the contact. You can publish it as it is, or make it your own first.

The editor has four tabs: **Build**, **Lead mapping**, **Auto-reply**, and **Design and share**. Click **Save** at the top to keep your changes. If you try to leave with unsaved changes, WaMatrix.io asks before you go.

## Adding fields

On the **Build** tab, click a field in **Add a field**, or drag it onto the form.

![The Build tab of the Free WhatsApp Consultation Request form. On the left, Add a field lists Inputs and Choices such as Short text, Phone, Dropdown and Consent. In the middle, two steps named Profile Details and Security with + Step and Preview buttons, and fields for Full name, WhatsApp number, Email and Join Date. On the right, Field settings for Full name shows Label name, Field key, Placeholder, Help text, the Required switch, and Full row or Half row width](/appointment-and-flow/img/form-builder/build-tab.png)

| Group | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Inputs** | Short text, Long text, Phone, Email, Number, Date |
| **Choices** | Dropdown, Single choice, Multiple choice, Consent |
| **Other** | File upload |
| **Layout** | Heading, Paragraph, Divider |

Click a field on the form to change it in **Field settings**:

- **Label name** — the question customers see.
- **Required** — customers can't submit without answering it.
- **Width** — **Full row**, or **Half row** to put two fields side by side.
- **Options** — the answers to pick from, for Dropdown, Single choice, and Multiple choice.
- **Show only when** — show the field only when an earlier answer matches. For example, show *Which city?* only when *Delivery needed* is **Yes**. Customers only see the questions that apply to them.

To move a field, drag it by its handle. Hover over a field to duplicate or delete it.

### Steps and preview

Long form? Click **+ Step** to split it into pages. Customers answer one page at a time, which feels quicker to fill in.

Click **Preview** to see the form exactly as customers will.

## Saving answers to the contact

On the **Lead mapping** tab, choose where each answer is saved on the contact: **Full name**, **Phone (WhatsApp)**, **Email**, or one of your [custom fields](/core-setup/custom-fields).

![The Lead mapping tab. On the left, Save answers to the contact lists each form field with the contact detail it saves to: Full name to Full name, WhatsApp number to Phone (WhatsApp), Email to Email, Join Date to the custom field Company Join Date, and the rest to Don't save to contact. On the right, Contact mapping has Use custom mapping for this form turned on, with Type set to Customer, Status to Qualified, Source to Website Form, Assign to Round robin, the labels Returning Customer and Wholesale, and a Save Changes button](/appointment-and-flow/img/form-builder/lead-mapping-tab.png)

Pick **Don't save to contact** for answers you only need to read once. They're still kept with the response, so nothing is lost.

<Warning>
Every contact is identified by their WhatsApp number, so your form needs a phone field that is mapped to **Phone (WhatsApp)**, set to **Required**, and not hidden with **Show only when**. The starter form already has one. If something's missing, WaMatrix.io tells you exactly what to fix when you click **Publish**.
</Warning>

**Already a contact?** If the WhatsApp number is already in your contacts, the response is linked to that contact. No duplicate is created, and their existing details stay as they are. WaMatrix.io only fills in an email if the contact didn't have one, and updates any custom fields you've mapped.

### Contact type, status, and source

New contacts from a form get their type, status, and source from [Contact Defaults](/settings/contact-configuration), the same as other new contacts.

To set them for one form, go to **Contact mapping** and turn on **Use custom mapping for this form**. Choose the **Type**, **Status**, **Source**, and **Assign to**, and add **Labels** or **Groups** if you like. Then click **Save Changes**. This panel has its own save button, so the **Save** at the top doesn't save it.

<Tip>
Pick a **Source** just for this form, and set **Assign to** to **Round robin** to share new leads evenly across your team. You'll always know which leads came from the form, and nobody gets them all.
</Tip>

## Sending a WhatsApp reply

Thank every customer on WhatsApp the moment they submit. Go to the **Auto-reply** tab and turn on **WhatsApp auto-reply**.

![The Auto-reply tab with WhatsApp auto-reply turned on, Send from set to a WhatsApp channel, and Template set to video_offer. Under Template content are a video header and three body variables filled with the customer's first name, email and last name, and a note to type @ to insert a contact detail or a form answer. On the right, Reply preview shows the message with its video as the customer will see it in WhatsApp](/appointment-and-flow/img/form-builder/auto-reply-tab.png)

The reply is a **template**: a message format approved by WhatsApp in advance, with blanks for details like the customer's name.

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the WhatsApp channel">
  In **Send from**, pick the number the reply comes from.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose the template">
  In **Template**, pick one of your approved templates.
</Step>

<Step title="Fill in the template">
  Fill in the blanks under **Template content**. Type **@** to insert a contact detail, like the customer's name, or any answer from the form. **Reply preview** shows the message as the customer will see it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
WhatsApp requires an approved template whenever a business messages first. This is a WhatsApp rule, not a WaMatrix.io one. Meta explains it under [templates](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview).
</Note>

## Design and share

On the **Design and share** tab, make the form look and feel like your business:

![The Design and share tab. On the left, Design has Intro text, a Thank-you message reading Thank you. We will be in touch shortly, an empty Redirect after submit box, Button text set to Submit, a purple Button colour, Default phone country set to India +91, and Spam protection turned on. On the right, Share shows the Public link with copy and open buttons, Allow embedding turned on, and the Embed code with a Copy button](/appointment-and-flow/img/form-builder/design-and-share-tab.png)

- **Intro text** — a short welcome line under the form's name.
- **Thank-you message** — what customers see after they submit.
- **Redirect after submit** — send customers to a page on your website instead, like a thank-you or offer page.
- **Button text** and **Button colour** — match the submit button to your brand.
- **Default phone country** — the country code the phone field starts with, so most customers don't need to change it.
- **Spam protection** — shows a CAPTCHA check before submitting, where available. Every form also blocks common spam bots on its own.

### Sharing your form

Copy the **Public link** and share it anywhere, like your Instagram bio, an ad, or a WhatsApp message. The link works once the form is published.

To show the form on your website, turn on **Allow embedding** and click **Save**. Then copy the **Embed code** and paste it into your website. The form resizes itself to fit your page.

## Publishing your form

Click **Publish** at the top of the editor. Any unsaved changes are saved first, and your form's link goes live.

Need a break from new responses? Click **Stop responses**. Anyone who opens the link sees that the form is no longer accepting responses. Click **Receive responses** to open it again.

## Managing your forms

All your forms are listed on the **Form Builder** page. The counter at the top shows how many forms you've made and how many your plan includes.

![The Form Builder page with a Form Builder 6 / 10 counter, a Create Form button, and a table of six forms. Customer Feedback Survey and Test Form are Draft with 0 submissions, Free WhatsApp Consultation Request, Diwali Offer Sign-up and Book a Product Demo are Published, and Job Application – Sales Executive is Closed. Each row shows its Submissions count, Last submission date, Created date, and an actions menu](/appointment-and-flow/img/form-builder/forms-list.png)

- **Name** — the form's name. Click it to edit the form.
- **Status** — whether customers can fill in the form:
  - **Draft** — not published yet, so the link doesn't work.
  - **Published** — live and taking responses.
  - **Closed** — you stopped responses. The link shows that the form is closed.
- **Submissions** — how many responses the form has received. Click it to see them.
- **Last submission** — the date of the latest response.
- **Created** — the date you created the form.
- **Actions** — open the menu at the end of a row:
  - **Edit** — open the form editor.
  - **Submissions** — see every response.
  - **Copy link** — copy the form's link.
  - **Duplicate** — make a copy as a new draft, a quick start for a similar form.
  - **Stop responses** or **Receive responses** — close or reopen the form.
  - **Delete** — remove the form. Contacts it created stay in your contacts. Its link stops working right away and its responses can no longer be opened, so download them first if you need them.

## Seeing responses

To see a form's responses, open the menu at the end of its row and click **Submissions**.

![The submissions page for Free WhatsApp Consultation Request with CSV and Excel buttons at the top. The table lists each response's Name, Phone, Email, Status and Submitted date. Most show Converted, and Vikram Singh shows Contact Limit Reached](/appointment-and-flow/img/form-builder/submissions.png)

Each response shows a **Status**:

- **Converted** — the customer is saved as a contact.
- **Contact Limit Reached** — the response is saved, but your plan's contact limit was reached, so no contact was created and no reply was sent.

Click a customer's name, or **View** in the row's menu, to see all their answers, any files they uploaded, and, where available, the page or ad they came from. Then click **Open chat** to reply to them on WhatsApp.

Click **CSV** or **Excel** at the top to download every response.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Can I use the form on my own website?">
  Yes. Turn on **Allow embedding** on the **Design and share** tab, save, and paste the **Embed code** into your website. The form fits itself to your page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if a customer fills in the form twice?">
  Both responses are kept, and both are linked to the same contact. No duplicate contact is created.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I tell which page or ad a customer came from?">
  Often, yes. Open a response with **View** to see whether the customer used the form link or the form on your website, and, when that information was passed along, the page or ad they came from.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens when I reach my contact limit?">
  Responses are still saved, so you can read and download every answer on the form's **Submissions** page. They show **Contact Limit Reached**, and those customers aren't added as contacts or sent a reply. To keep every new lead flowing in, see [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
