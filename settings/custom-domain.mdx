By default, your workspace uses a WaMetrix.io address, such as `workspace.WaMetrix.io`. With a custom domain, you can use your own address instead, such as `app.yourcompany.com`, so your workspace matches your brand.

Set it up under **Settings → Custom Domain**.

<Note>
Custom Domain is a paid feature. If it's locked on your plan, you can buy it as an [add-on](/billing/addons-and-upgrades). Only the workspace owner can set it up.
</Note>

## Connecting your domain

<Figure
  src="/settings/img/custom-domain-form.png"
  alt="The Custom Domain page with a domain name field and a Connect Domain button"
  caption="Image shows the Custom Domain page."
/>

<Steps>
<Step title="Enter your domain">
  Type the domain you want to use, like `app.yourcompany.com`, without `http://` or `https://`. Then click **Connect Domain**.
</Step>

<Step title="Add a CNAME record">
  Go to your DNS provider and add a **CNAME** record using the name and target shown on the page. Click the copy icon to copy the target exactly.

  <Warning>
  If you use Cloudflare, set the record to **DNS only** (grey cloud), not **Proxied** (orange cloud). A proxied record stops the security certificate from being issued.
  </Warning>
</Step>

<Step title="Click Verify">
  Your domain is checked, and a security certificate is set up for you. This usually takes 1 to 5 minutes.

  DNS changes can take a while to spread. If it does not work first time, wait 5 to 10 minutes and click **Retry**.
</Step>
</Steps>


## Domain status

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Not verified** | Add the CNAME record, then click Verify. |
| **Pending** | Your certificate is being set up. This usually takes 1 to 5 minutes. |
| **Verified** | Your domain is live and working. |
| **Failed** | Something is wrong with the DNS record. Check it and try again. |

## After it is live

<Figure
  src="/settings/img/custom-domain-verified.png"
  alt="The Custom Domain page showing a verified domain and a primary domain selector"
  caption="Image shows a verified domain with the primary domain choice."
/>

Once verified, your custom domain becomes your workspace address straight away. Your old address still works — it sends people to your new one.

Use **Primary domain** to switch back to your original address if you ever need to.

<Warning>
If you have shared any booking links, copy them again after switching. Links copied before the change may stop working.
</Warning>

## Changing or removing your domain

Click the trash icon next to your domain to remove it. Your workspace goes back to its original address.

<Note>
You cannot edit a domain. To use a different one, remove this one first and then add the new one. If your custom domain is set as primary, switch back to your original address before removing it.
</Note>
