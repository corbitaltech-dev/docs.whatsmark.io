Go to **Settings → Business Hours** to set when you are open. Customers can only book [appointment](/appointment-and-flow/appointments) during these hours.

<Note>
Business hours are used for appointment booking. They do not change anything else, such as when campaigns send or when your team can reply in chat.
</Note>

## Weekly hours

Each day has an On/Off switch. Turn it on to set your opening and closing times. Turn it off to mark the day as Closed, so customers cannot book appointments on that day.

New workspaces are open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00, with Saturday and Sunday closed by default.

All business hours use your workspace timezone, which you can change in [General](/settings/general).

## Closed dates

Use **Closed Dates** to block specific dates, such as public holidays or special closures.

Select a date and click **Add**. To remove a date, click the × next to it. Customers cannot book appointments on closed dates.
