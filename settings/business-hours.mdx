## Where your business hours are used

- **Appointments.** Customers can only book [appointments](/appointment-and-flow/appointments) during these hours.
- **AI Assistants.** An [AI Assistant](/automation/ai-assistants) can check these hours before it answers. Turn on **Respect business hours** in the assistant's **Behaviour** tab. Then choose what it does under **Outside business hours**:
  - **Reply as usual** — the assistant still answers. It knows you are closed, so it does not promise a fast reply from your team.
  - **Hand off to a human** — the assistant stops and passes the chat to your team.
  - **Stay silent** — the assistant does not answer.

  This also applies to the [AI Assistant step](/automation/flow-steps/advanced#ai-assistant) in an automation flow. Closed dates count as outside your hours.

The times follow your workspace time zone.

<Note>
Business hours do not change when campaigns send, or when your team can reply in chat.
</Note>

## Weekly hours

Each day has an On/Off switch. Turn it on to set your opening and closing times. Turn it off to mark the day as Closed, so customers cannot book appointments on that day.

New workspaces are open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00, with Saturday and Sunday closed by default.

All business hours use your workspace timezone, which you can change in [General](/settings/general).

## Closed dates

Use **Closed dates (holidays)** to block specific dates, such as public holidays or special closures.

Select a date and click **Add**. To remove a date, click the × next to it. Customers cannot book appointments on closed dates. AI Assistants that respect business hours treat these dates as closed.
