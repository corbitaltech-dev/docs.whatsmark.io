If you no longer need your workspace, you can permanently close it from **Settings → Account**, under the **Danger Zone** section.

## Before you start

Closing your account will:

- Cancel your subscription immediately.
- Remove all team members.
- Delete all uploaded files.
- Delete all workspace settings.
- Delete all contacts, chats, and everything else in your workspace.

## Closing the account

<Figure
  src="/settings/img/account-danger-zone-form.png"
  alt="Danger Zone card listing what account closure deletes, with a subdomain confirmation field, password field, and Close My Account button"
  caption="Image shows the Danger Zone card on the Account page."
/>

<Steps>
<Step title="Type your subdomain">
  Type your subdomain exactly as shown above the box.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter your password">
  Enter your account password to confirm it's really you.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Close My Account">
  Your subscription is cancelled immediately, and your workspace is scheduled for deletion.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

You are signed out immediately, and so is everyone else on your team.

<Figure
  src="/settings/img/account-closed.png"
  alt="Account Closed page showing the scheduled deletion date, a note to contact support, and a Log out button"
  caption="Image shows the page your team sees after the account is closed."
/>

Anyone who tries to sign in now sees this page instead of the app, showing the date your workspace will be deleted. Your data stays safe until that date, but no one can access the workspace.

<Warning>
Changed your mind? You can't undo this yourself, since you can no longer sign in. Contact support before the deletion date to stop it.
</Warning>
