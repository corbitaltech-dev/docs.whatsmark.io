If you no longer need your WhatsMark workspace, you can permanently close it here. Find this option in Settings → Account, under the Danger Zone section.

<Warning>
Only the workspace **owner** can close the account. Nobody else sees this option.
</Warning>

## Before you start

Closing your account will:

- Cancel your subscription immediately.
- Remove all team members.
- Delete all uploaded files.
- Delete all workspace settings.
- Delete all contacts, chats, and everything else in your workspace.

## Closing the account

  src="/settings/img/account-danger-zone-form.png"

<Steps>
<Step title="Type your subdomain">
  Type it exactly as it is shown above the box. The **Close My Account** button stays greyed out until it matches.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter your password">
  Your own account password, as a last check that it is really you.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Close My Account">
  Your subscription is cancelled immediately, and your workspace is scheduled for deletion.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

You are signed out immediately, and so is everyone else on your team.

<BrowserFrame
  src="/settings/img/account-closed.png"
  url="whatsmark.io"
  alt="Account Closed page showing the scheduled deletion date, a note to contact support, and a Log out button"
  caption="Image shows the page your team sees after the account is closed."
/>

Anyone who tries to sign in sees this page instead of the app. It shows the date your workspace will be deleted, a **Contact Support** button and **Log out**.

Your data is still there until that date, but nobody can get into the workspace.

<Warning>
Changed your mind? You cannot undo this yourself, because you can no longer sign in. Contact support before the deletion date and ask them to stop it.
</Warning>
