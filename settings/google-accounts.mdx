Connect your Google account once here, and WhatsMark.io can then work with your Google for you:

- **Google Sheets** — An [automation flow](/automation/overview) can add a new row to a Google Sheet, for example when someone submits a form or answers a question.
- **Google Calendar** — [Appointments](/ai-and-more/appointments) can automatically add a customer's booking to your Google Calendar.

One connection covers both. Find it under **Settings → Google Accounts**.

Google is a paid feature. If your plan does not include it, this page shows **Google integrations are not in your current plan** with an **Upgrade Plan** button instead.

## Connecting an account

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Connect Google Account">
  You are taken to Google's own sign-in page.
</Step>

<Step title="Sign in and allow access">
  Choose the Google account you want to use and allow the access it asks for. This covers your spreadsheets and your calendars.
</Step>

<Step title="Check it worked">
  You come back to this page and the account is listed with its name, email address and the date you connected it.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can connect more than one Google account. You pick which one to use when you build a flow or set up appointments.

<Note>
If you see **Google connection unavailable**, the platform's own Google setup is not ready. Contact support — there is nothing to fix on your side.
</Note>

## Using a connected account

Nothing else happens on this page. The account is used in two other places:

- In the [flow builder](/automation/flow-builder), a Google Sheets step lets you choose a connected account, then pick one of your sheets or make a new one.
- In [Appointments](/ai-and-more/appointments), you choose the account and the calendar that bookings go into.

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** next to an account. You are asked to confirm first, because anything using it stops working.

<Warning>
Disconnecting does not fix the flows and appointments that were using the account. They keep failing until you point them at another connected account.
</Warning>
