Connect your Google account once, and WhatsMark.io can use it in two ways:

- **Google Sheets** — When a customer submits a form or answers a question, an [automation](/automation/overview) can add their details as a new row in a Google Sheet.
- **Google Calendar** — When a customer books an [appointment](/ai-and-more/appointments), it's added to your Google Calendar automatically.

Find it under **Settings → Google Accounts**.

## Connecting an account

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Connect Google Account">
  You are taken to Google's own sign-in page.
</Step>

<Step title="Sign in and allow access">
  Pick the Google account you want to connect and approve the permissions it asks for — access to your Google Sheets and Google Calendar.
</Step>

<Step title="Check it worked">
  You come back to this page and the account is listed with its name, email address and the date you connected it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can connect more than one Google account. You pick which one to use when you build a [flow](/automation/flow-builder) or set up [appointments](/ai-and-more/appointments).
</Note>

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** next to an account to remove it. You'll be asked to confirm first, since any flow or appointment using that account will stop working.
