Go to **Settings → General** to update your **Organization Information**. General is on the **Core Platform** tab. This tab opens first when you open Settings.

<Figure
  src="/settings/img/general-form.png"
  alt="The Organization Information form with fields for organization name, subdomain, timezone, date format, time format and default country"
  caption="Image shows the General settings page."
/>

- **Organization Name** — your organization's name, shown around the dashboard and in emails.
- **Subdomain** — your workspace address. You cannot change it here; contact support if you need it changed.
- **Timezone** — the timezone your workspace uses.
- **Date Format** — choose from `2026-02-13`, `13/02/2026`, `02/13/2026`, or `February 13, 2026`.
- **Time Format** — **24 Hours** or **12 Hours**.
- **Default Country** — the country already chosen on every phone number field. You do not have to pick it each time.

## Where it's used

Your **timezone** is used throughout the workspace. Scheduled campaigns, business hours, and appointment times all use this timezone.

The date and time formats control how dates and times are displayed across the workspace, including contacts, campaigns, chats, invoices, and reports.

Your **default country** is chosen for you whenever you type a phone number. You can still choose a different country for any number.
