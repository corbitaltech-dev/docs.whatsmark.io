When someone fills in the form on your Facebook lead ad, WaMatrix.io adds them to your contacts. An automation can then send them a WhatsApp message straight away. *(Needs a paid plan.)*

These forms are called **instant forms**. They open inside the ad, so the person stays on Facebook or Instagram. Leads from Instagram lead ads also belong to your Facebook Page, so connecting the Page is enough. Meta explains this in its [lead ads guide](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/guides/lead-ads).

Find it under **Integrations → Facebook Lead Ads**. **Integrations** is in the main menu. It opens a page with one card for each tool you can connect.

## Step 1: Get your details from Facebook

You do these steps on Meta's developer site, not in WaMatrix.io.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create a Facebook app">
  Go to [developers.facebook.com/apps](https://developers.facebook.com/apps) and create an app. When Meta asks what the app is for, choose **Capture & manage ad leads with Marketing API**. Meta lists this use case in its [Marketing API use cases](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/development/create-an-app/marketing-api-use-cases).

  Copy the app's **App ID** and **App Secret** from the app's settings. The App Secret works like a password, so keep it private.
</Step>

<Step title="Create a user access token">
  An **access token** is a long code that lets an app read data for you. It works like a password.

  Open Meta's **Graph API Explorer**. At the top right, pick your app under **Meta App**. Add the permissions listed below, then create the token and allow what Facebook asks. Meta explains the tool in its [Graph API Explorer guide](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/guides/explorer).

  <Accordion title="The permissions to tick">
    - `leads_retrieval`
    - `pages_show_list`
    - `pages_read_engagement`
    - `pages_manage_ads`
    - `pages_manage_metadata`
    - `ads_management`
  </Accordion>
</Step>

<Step title="Make the token last longer">
  The token you just made stops working within a few hours. If you skip this step, your leads stop soon after you connect.

  Open Meta's **Access Token Debugger**, paste the token and click **Debug**. Then click **Extend Access Token** and copy the new, longer token. Meta explains why in its guide to [long-lived tokens](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/facebook-login/guides/access-tokens/get-long-lived).
</Step>

<Step title="Copy your Page's own token">
  Back in Graph API Explorer, paste the longer token. In the box at the top, type `me/accounts` and click **Submit**. You see a list of the Pages you manage.

  Find your Page. Copy its `access_token` and its `id`. These are your **Page Access Token** and your **Page ID**.

  <Warning>
  Copy the token listed **for your Page**, not the user token from the step before. A user token does not work.
  </Warning>
</Step>

<Step title="Switch your app to Live">
  While your app is in **Development** mode, you only get test leads from people with a role in the app, such as you. Switch it to **Live** to get leads from real customers. Use the app mode switch at the top of your app's dashboard.

  Meta may ask you to finish some checks first, such as App Review. This is Meta's rule, not ours. See Meta's [lead ads guide](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/guides/lead-ads) and [app modes](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/development/build-and-test/app-modes).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Connect your Page

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Facebook Lead Ads card">
  In WaMatrix.io, go to **Integrations**. On the **Facebook Lead Ads** card, click **Connect**. The **Connect Facebook Lead Ads** panel opens on the right.

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/img/facebook-lead-ads-connect-form.png — The Integrations page with the Connect Facebook Lead Ads panel open on the right, showing the App ID, App Secret, Page ID, Page Access Token and Default country code fields, and the Cancel and Connect Page buttons */}
</Step>

<Step title="Fill in the form">
  Paste the **App ID**, **App Secret**, **Page ID** and **Page Access Token** you copied from Facebook.

  **Default country code** is optional. WaMatrix.io adds it to phone numbers that people type without a country code. For example, enter `91` for India.

  WaMatrix.io stores your App Secret and Page Access Token safely and never shows them again.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Connect Page">
  WaMatrix.io checks your token with Facebook. When it works, the card says **Connected** and its button changes to **Manage**.
</Step>
</Steps>

If something is wrong, a message appears under the field. See [Fix a problem](#fix-a-problem).

## Step 3: Tell your Facebook app where to send leads

Your Facebook app must send each new lead to WaMatrix.io. Without this step, no new leads arrive.

<Steps>
<Step title="Copy the two codes">
  On the **Facebook Lead Ads** card, click **Manage**. The **Webhook setup** card at the top shows a **Callback URL** and a **Verify token**. Click the copy icon next to each one.

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/img/facebook-lead-ads-webhook-setup.png — The Webhook setup card on the Facebook Lead Ads page, with the Callback URL and Verify token fields and a copy icon next to each */}
</Step>

<Step title="Paste them into your Facebook app">
  In your app's dashboard on Meta's developer site, open the **Webhooks** settings. Choose the **Page** object. Paste the Callback URL and the Verify token, and save. Then subscribe to the **leadgen** field.

  Meta describes this step in its [lead ads setup guide](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/guides/lead-ads/quickstart/webhooks-integration).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Step 4: Choose what to capture

On the Facebook Lead Ads page, the **Lead forms & field mapping** card controls which leads come in and how they are saved. Change what you need, then click **Save Changes**.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/img/facebook-lead-ads-lead-capture.png — The Lead forms & field mapping card with the Load forms from Facebook button, the Capture leads from list, a Field mapping row with Form question key and Contact custom field, and the Default country code field */}

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Capture leads from: which forms to capture" icon="list-checks">
  Click **Load forms from Facebook** to get the list of forms on your Page. Later, the button says **Refresh forms from Facebook**. Click it after you create a new form.

  - **Tick nothing** to capture every form. This includes forms you create later.
  - **Tick some forms** to capture only those.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Field mapping: save answers into custom fields" icon="file-text">
  Your form may ask extra questions, like budget or city. You can save each answer into a contact [custom field](/core-setup/custom-fields).

  Click **Add mapping**. On the left, enter the **Form question key**. This is the short name Facebook gives each question, such as `city`. You can pick it from a list once your forms are loaded. On the right, choose the contact custom field.

  If you have no custom fields yet, you see a note instead. Create a custom field first.

  Answers you don't map are still kept with the lead, and you can use them in messages.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Contact mapping: status, source and owner" icon="user-cog">
  The **Contact mapping** card is below. When **Use custom mapping for Facebook Lead Ads** is **off**, new contacts get your usual [Contact Defaults](/settings/contact-configuration).

  Turn it **on** to give these contacts their own settings, then click **Save Changes**:

  - **Type**: **Guest**, **Lead** or **Customer**.
  - **Status** and **Source**: choose from your [statuses](/core-setup/statuses) and [sources](/core-setup/sources).
  - **Assign to**: **Unassigned**, **Round robin**, **Online team member** or **Specific team member**. For **Specific team member**, pick the person in **Team member**.
  - **Labels** and **Groups**: optional.

  When the switch is on, WaMatrix.io uses only what you choose on this card. It does not fill gaps from your Contact Defaults. For example, if you leave **Labels** empty, these contacts get no labels.

  <Tip>
  Turn this on and pick a **Source**, such as one called Facebook Lead Ads. Then **View contacts** lists exactly these contacts. When the switch is off, new contacts get the source from your Contact Defaults, so **View contacts** may not show them.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What the Connected Page card shows" icon="info">
  The **Connected Page** card shows your Page name, its status and when the last lead came in. Until the first lead, it says **No leads yet**. It also shows your **App ID** and a **Disconnect** button.

  - **View contacts** opens your contact list, filtered by the **Source** that these contacts get.
  - **Import last 90 days** brings in older leads. See [Bring in older leads](#bring-in-older-leads).

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/img/facebook-lead-ads-connected.png — The Connected Page card with the Page name, Connected, Last lead and App ID, the Disconnect button, and the View contacts and Import last 90 days buttons */}
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How a lead becomes a contact

<Accordion title="What WaMatrix.io does with each new lead" icon="workflow">
  1. Gets the answers from Facebook.
  2. Looks for a contact with the same phone number.
  3. If there is none, creates a new contact with the person's **name**, **phone number** and **email**.
  4. Copies any answers you mapped into custom fields.
  5. Starts any WhatsApp automation that uses the **Leads** trigger.
</Accordion>

A few cases work differently:

- **The phone number is already in your contacts.** No new contact is made. The lead is added to that contact. If the contact has no email yet, the email from the form is added. An email you already have is never replaced.
- **The form has no phone number.** No contact is made and no automation starts, because there is no number to message.
- **Your plan's contact limit is full.** WaMatrix.io keeps the lead, but no contact is made. A red banner says **leads waiting on your contact limit**. Upgrade your plan, then click **Import waiting leads**. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).

## Bring in older leads

Click **Import last 90 days** on the Connected Page card. WaMatrix.io brings in leads from the last 90 days, from the forms you capture. Older leads can't be imported. Leads you already have are not added twice.

Leads you bring in with **Import last 90 days** or **Import waiting leads** do not start automations. They may be days old, so a "thanks for your enquiry" message would arrive late.

## Reply to new leads on WhatsApp

Build a WhatsApp automation and set its **Start Trigger** to **Leads**. Under **Lead Source**, choose **Facebook Lead Ads**, or keep **Any lead source**. The **Leads** option shows only on WhatsApp automations, because a new lead has never messaged your Page or Instagram account. See [Leads trigger](/automation/flow-builder#leads).

Make the first message a WhatsApp **template**: a message format that Meta approves in advance. WhatsApp lets a business message someone first only with a template. This is a WhatsApp rule, not a WaMatrix.io one. See Meta's [templates overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview).

To use the lead's details, type `@` in a message box and pick from the **Lead** group. For an answer you did not map, type its question key, for example `{{lead.field.city}}`. See [Merge fields](/messaging/merge-fields).

## Fix a problem

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Could not verify this Page and token with Facebook" icon="circle-alert">
  The token is wrong or has expired. Follow [Step 1](#step-1-get-your-details-from-facebook) again to get a new Page Access Token, then try again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="This token does not belong to the Page ID you entered." icon="circle-alert">
  The **Page ID** and the token are from different Pages. Type `me/accounts` in Graph API Explorer again. Copy the `id` and the `access_token` from the **same** row.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Could not connect this Page" icon="circle-alert">
  Facebook did not accept part of the setup. The text after the message gives Facebook's reason. Check that you pasted the **Page** token, not the user token. Also check that you ticked every permission in Step 1.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="This Facebook Page could not be connected" icon="circle-alert">
  The Page may already be connected to another workspace. A Page's leads can go to only one workspace. If the Page is yours, contact support.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The card says Needs attention" icon="triangle-alert">
  **Needs attention** means leads have stopped coming in. Usually, Facebook no longer accepts your Page Access Token. To fix it:

  1. Follow [Step 1](#step-1-get-your-details-from-facebook) again to get a fresh Page Access Token.
  2. On the **Facebook Lead Ads** card, click **Connect**. Fill in the form for the same Page and click **Connect Page**. Your form choices and field mapping stay, because they are saved with the Page.
  3. Click **Manage**, then **Import last 90 days** to catch up on missed leads. These leads do not start automations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My test lead is not showing up" icon="search">
  Check these things:

  - The card says **Connected**.
  - You finished [Step 3](#step-3-tell-your-facebook-app-where-to-send-leads) in your Facebook app.
  - Under **Capture leads from**, the form is ticked, or nothing is ticked.
  - If your app is still in **Development** mode, you sent the test lead yourself.
  - The form asks for a phone number.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A lead came in, but my automation did not start" icon="bot">
  The automation starts only when the lead has a phone number and fits in your contact limit. Also check that the automation uses a WhatsApp channel and is turned on.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Disconnecting

On the Facebook Lead Ads page, click **Disconnect** on the Connected Page card. The **Disconnect this Page?** box opens. Click **Disconnect** to confirm.

Contacts made from past leads stay, and your automations stay too. New leads stop arriving. If the same Page is also connected as a Messenger channel, that channel keeps working.
