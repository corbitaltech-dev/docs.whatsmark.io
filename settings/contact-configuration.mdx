In **Settings → Contacts**, you decide the default type, status, source, labels, groups, and assignee given to each new contact that's created automatically.

## Global Defaults

These apply to every new contact, whichever channel they come from.

One channel can also have its own defaults. On the **Channels** page, click the ⋮ menu on that channel's row, choose **Contact defaults**, and turn on **Override global defaults**. The fields, including **Team groups**, work the same way there.

- **Type** — what a new contact is called: **Guest**, **Lead** or **Customer**. See [Managing Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts).
- **Status** — the [status](/core-setup/statuses) a new contact starts in, like *New*.
- **Source** — where the contact came from. See [Sources](/core-setup/sources).
- **Assign To** — who handles the new contact. See [Ways to assign](#ways-to-assign).
- **Labels** — optional. Add one or more [labels](/core-setup/labels) to every new contact.
- **Groups** — optional. Put every new contact into one or more [groups](/core-setup/groups).

<Note>
Status, Source and Assign To are required. You cannot save until each one is filled in.
</Note>

### Ways to assign

- **Unassigned** — No one is assigned. Any team member can reply to the contact.
- **Round Robin** — New contacts are assigned to team members one by one.
- **Online Team Member** — Assigns the contact to a team member who is currently online. If no one is online, it assigns the contact to any team member.
- **Custom Team Member** — Assigns every new contact to the selected team member. Choose the team member from the list.

### Share contacts within a team

When you choose **Round Robin** or **Online Team Member**, a **Team groups** box appears. Use it to share new contacts only among the people in one or more teams, such as Sales. Teams are the groups you make in **Chat**, under **Team Inbox**. See [Assign it to a team](/messaging/live-chat#assign-it-to-a-team).

Each team in the list shows how many members it has, like *Sales (3 members)*.

- Only members of the teams you pick take turns.
- If you leave **Team groups** empty, everyone in your workspace takes turns, including the owner.
- If you pick teams, the owner takes turns only if they are in one of those teams.
- Someone who is in more than one of your teams still gets one turn. Their chats are put under the first team you picked, so pick your main team first.
- If every team you picked has been deleted, new contacts stay unassigned. WaMatrix.io does not share them with everyone instead.

## New chats follow the contact

When a new chat starts, WaMatrix.io gives it to the team member assigned to the contact. If you use **Team groups**, the chat is also put under that person's team, so everyone in the team sees it in **Team Inbox**.

This happens only when the chat is first created. If you later change who a contact is assigned to, their existing chats stay where they are. To move a chat, assign it again in [Chat](/messaging/live-chat#assigning-chats).

If a contact is **Unassigned**, their new chat is unassigned too.

## Where these defaults are used

A contact is created with these settings whenever a new person reaches you, and not only through chat:

- A message on [WhatsApp](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp) or [Messenger](/core-setup/channels/messenger).
- An order from [Shopify](/settings/shopify) or [WooCommerce](/settings/woocommerce).
- A booking made through [Appointments](/appointment-and-flow/appointments).
