## Global Defaults

These apply to every new contact, whichever channel they come from. One channel can also have its own defaults. On that platform's **Channels** page, open the three-dot menu (⋮) at the end of the channel's row and choose **Contact defaults**.

- **Type** — what a new contact is called: **Guest**, **Lead** or **Customer**. See [Managing Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts).
- **Status** — the [status](/core-setup/statuses) a new contact starts in, like *New*.
- **Source** — where the contact came from. See [Sources](/core-setup/sources).
- **Assign To** — who handles the new contact. See [Ways to assign](#ways-to-assign).
- **Labels** — optional. Add one or more [labels](/core-setup/labels) to every new contact.
- **Groups** — optional. Put every new contact into one or more [groups](/core-setup/groups).

<Note>
Type, Status, Source and Assign To are all required. You cannot save until each one is filled in.
</Note>

### Ways to assign

- **Unassigned** — No one is assigned. Any team member can reply to the contact.
- **Round Robin** — New contacts go to your team members in turn, one after another.
- **Online Team Member** — The contact goes to a team member who is online now. If nobody is online, it goes to your team members in turn, like Round Robin.
- **Custom Team Member** — Assigns every new contact to the selected team member. Choose the team member from the list.

Round Robin and Online Team Member only choose people who can use the platform the message came from. For example, a team member without Instagram in their **Platform access** never gets a new Instagram chat. See [Choose which platforms a member can use](/core-setup/team-and-roles#choose-which-platforms-a-member-can-use).

## Where these defaults are used

A contact is created with these settings whenever a new person reaches you, and not only through chat:

- A message on [WhatsApp](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp), [Messenger](/core-setup/channels/messenger) or [Instagram](/core-setup/channels/instagram). *(Instagram needs a paid plan.)*
- An order from [Shopify](/settings/shopify) or [WooCommerce](/settings/woocommerce). *(Needs a paid plan.)*
- A booking made through [Appointments](/appointment-and-flow/appointments).
- A lead from [Facebook Lead Ads](/settings/facebook-lead-ads) or [IndiaMART](/settings/indiamart), unless that lead source has its own defaults. *(Needs a paid plan.)*
