Whenever there is activity on your WhatsApp account — like an incoming message, a template approval, or a delivery update — WaMetrix.io can send the details of that event to your own server, CRM, or automation tools.

Find it under **Settings → Webhooks**.

<Figure
  src="/settings/img/webhooks-form.png"
  alt="Global Webhook Config form showing webhook URL, method, signing secret, events, and a list of channel overrides"
  caption="Image shows the Webhooks page, with the global settings on top and channel overrides below."
/>

## Turning it on

<Steps>
<Step title="Switch on Global Webhook Config">
  Use the switch at the top right of the card. The fields below unlock.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter your Webhook URL">
  The web address where events are sent, such as `https://your-server.com/webhook`.

  <Warning>
  The address has to be reachable from the internet. A local address such as `localhost`, `127.0.0.1` or an address inside your own network is refused when you save, for security reasons.
  </Warning>
</Step>

<Step title="Choose a Method">
  You can use POST or GET. POST is recommended. In both cases, the event data is sent as JSON in the request body.
</Step>

<Step title="Add a Signing Secret">
  Click the refresh icon to generate a secret key. Your server uses this key to confirm that a webhook truly came from WaMetrix.io and wasn't sent by someone else.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick your Events">
  Choose which events to forward to your server — from 30 types like Messages, Template Status Updates, and Phone Number Quality Updates. Only the events you select are sent.
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
  Click **Save** to turn on forwarding. From now on, every event you selected is sent to your webhook URL as it happens.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Per-channel webhook settings

By default, all your channels forward their events to the same webhook URL. If you want a specific channel to send its events somewhere else instead, you can give that channel its own webhook settings.

<Figure
  src="/settings/img/webhooks-channel-override.png"
  alt="The Channel Overrides section with a channel expanded, showing an Override toggle, Enable forwarding switch, and its own Webhook URL, Method, Signing Secret, and Events fields, with a Save Channel button"
  caption="Image shows the Channel Overrides section with a channel's own settings."
/>

<Steps>
<Step title="Find the channel">
  Scroll to **Channel Overrides**. Only connected, active channels are listed.
</Step>

<Step title="Turn on Override">
  The channel's own fields open up.
</Step>

<Step title="Turn on Enable forwarding for this channel">
  Then fill in its own **Webhook URL**, **Method**, **Signing Secret** and **Events**. These are separate from the global ones.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Save Channel">
  That channel now uses its own webhook settings. All your other channels keep using the default settings.
</Step>
</Steps>
