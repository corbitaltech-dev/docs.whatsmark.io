When a customer sends you a photo, a voice note or a PDF on WhatsApp, the file itself stays on Meta's servers. WhatsMark.io only holds a link to it.

Meta deletes those files about **30 days** later. After that the link stops working, and the message in your chat shows nothing.

Turn this setting on and WhatsMark.io saves its own copy of every file as it arrives, so it is still there months later. Find it under **Settings → Inbound Media**.

## Turning it on

Switch on **Download inbound media**, then click **Save**.

From then on, every photo, video, voice note, document and sticker a customer sends is saved as it arrives.

Switch it off and new files stop being saved. Anything already saved stays as it is.

<Note>
This covers WhatsApp only. Files sent to you on Messenger are not saved.
</Note>

## What it costs you in storage

Every saved file uses part of the storage on your plan.

Check how much you have left on the [Dashboard](/reporting/dashboard-and-usage).

<Warning>
If your storage is full, new files are quietly skipped. Nothing warns you, and the message keeps only the Meta link — which still stops working after 30 days. Keep an eye on your storage if you receive a lot of files.
</Warning>
