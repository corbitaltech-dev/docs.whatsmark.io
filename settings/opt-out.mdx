Customers must be able to stop receiving your marketing messages.

In **Settings → Opt-out**, enter the words customers can use to unsubscribe. When a customer sends one of these words, they are automatically unsubscribed from your marketing messages.

WhatsMark.io includes **STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, OPT OUT, and OPTOUT** by default. You can edit them or add your own keywords.

<Warning>
If you ignore customer's opt-out, they may report your messages as spam. This can lower your WhatsApp quality rating, reduce your messaging limit, or even cause Meta to suspend your phone number.
</Warning>

## What stops, and what does not

Only marketing messages stop. Important messages are still sent.

| Still sent | Stopped |
| --- | --- |
| Order and delivery updates | Marketing [campaigns](/messaging/campaigns) |
| Appointment reminders | Any marketing template |
| One-time passcodes | Plain text campaigns sent to a list |
| Your team replying in [chat](/messaging/live-chat) | |

<Note>
Opting out applies to your whole workspace. If someone unsubscribes from one of your WhatsApp numbers, they will stop receiving marketing messages from all your WhatsApp numbers and Messenger.
</Note>

## Unsubscribe words

Enter the words customers can use to unsubscribe, separated by commas. You can keep the default words, edit them, or add your own.

How the matching works:

- **Capital letters do not matter.** `stop`, `Stop` and `STOP` all work.
- **The message must contain only the keyword**. For example, **stop sending me the wrong size** is treated as a normal message, not an unsubscribe.
- **Only text messages work.** Buttons, images, or captions containing the keyword do not unsubscribe the customer.

## Resubscribe words

The words that start your marketing again. The defaults are **START**, **SUBSCRIBE**, **UNSTOP**, **OPT IN** and **OPTIN**.

## The automatic reply

By default, WhatsMark.io sends a confirmation message when someone unsubscribes or subscribes again.

- **Send a confirmation** — Turn this off if you don't want to send a confirmation message.
- **Unsubscribe confirmation** — The message sent when a customer unsubscribes.
- **Resubscribe confirmation** — The message sent when a customer subscribes again.

## Seeing who has opted out

Only customers can unsubscribe or subscribe again by sending the keyword. You can't change their status manually.

- **Contact Details** – Shows an Opted Out of Marketing badge.
- **Chat Header** – Shows whether the contact has opted out.
- **Campaign Audience** – Shows how many contacts were excluded because they opted out.
