Customers can stop receiving your marketing messages by replying with a keyword, like `STOP` or `UNSUBSCRIBE`. When they send one of these words, they're unsubscribed automatically.

In **Settings → Opt-out**, you choose which keywords count. WaMatrix.io comes with **STOP**, **UNSUBSCRIBE**, **OPT OUT**, and **OPTOUT** ready to use — you can edit these or add your own.

## Which messages are stopped

Only marketing messages stop. Important messages are still sent.

| Still sent | Stopped |
| --- | --- |
| Order and delivery updates | Marketing [campaigns](/messaging/campaigns) |
| Appointment reminders | Any marketing template |
| One-time passcodes | Plain text campaigns sent to a list |
| Your team replying in [chat](/messaging/live-chat) | |

<Note>
When a customer unsubscribes, it applies across your whole workspace — they stop getting marketing from all your WhatsApp numbers and Messenger.
</Note>

## Unsubscribe words

Enter the words customers can use to unsubscribe, separated by commas. You can keep the default words, edit them, or add your own.

How the matching works:

- **Capital letters do not matter.** `stop`, `Stop` and `STOP` all work.
- **The message must contain only the keyword**. For example, **stop sending me the wrong size** is treated as a normal message, not an unsubscribe.
- **Only typed text works.** The keyword must be sent as a text message — clicking a button or sending it in an image caption won't unsubscribe the customer.

## Resubscribe words

The words that start your marketing again. The defaults are **START**, **SUBSCRIBE**, **UNSTOP**, **OPT IN** and **OPTIN**.

## The automatic reply

<Figure
  src="/settings/img/opt-out-automatic-reply.png"
  alt="Automatic reply settings with a Send a confirmation toggle turned on, and Unsubscribe confirmation and Resubscribe confirmation message fields"
  caption="Image shows the automatic reply settings."

By default, WaMatrix.io sends a confirmation message when someone unsubscribes or subscribes again.

- **Send a confirmation** — Turn this off if you don't want to send a confirmation message.
- **Unsubscribe confirmation** — The message sent when a customer unsubscribes.
- **Resubscribe confirmation** — The message sent when a customer subscribes again.
