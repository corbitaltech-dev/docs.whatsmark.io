Connect your Shopify store to automatically send WhatsApp messages when customers **place an order, their order ships, their order is delivered, or they leave without completing a purchase**.

Find it under **Settings → Shopify**.

## Connecting your store

<Steps>
<Step title="Enter your store domain">
  Enter your Shopify store domain exactly as Shopify provides it, ending with `.myshopify.com`. For example, `your-store.myshopify.com`.

  <Figure
    src="/settings/img/connect-shopify.png"
    alt="The Connect Shopify card with a Store domain field and a Connect Shopify button"
    caption="Image shows the Connect Shopify form."
  />
</Step>

<Step title="Click Connect Shopify">
  You are taken to Shopify.
</Step>

<Step title="Approve the permissions">
  Shopify asks you to allow WaMetrix.io to access your **orders, checkouts,** and **customers**.
</Step>

<Step title="You come back automatically">
  The status shows **Connecting…** and then **Connected** a moment later.

  <Figure
    src="/settings/img/shopify-connected.png"
    alt="The Shopify Store card showing a connected store, a Disconnect button and the Sync contacts section"
    caption="Image shows a connected Shopify store."
  />
</Step>
</Steps>


## What you can build

After you connect your store, Shopify events such as a **new order, shipping updates, order cancellation, or an abandoned cart** can automatically start an automation flow.

Choose the event when creating your flow. See [Shopify triggers](/automation/flow-builder#shopify) for the full list and when each one runs.

## Your first message must be a template

Every Shopify flow starts when something happens in your store, not when a customer messages you. For events like **abandoned carts, new orders, and shipping updates,** the customer usually hasn't contacted you yet, so you can't send a regular WhatsApp message.

Start every Shopify flow with a [**Template Message**](/automation/flow-steps/basic#whatsapp-template). Once the customer replies, you can continue the conversation with normal text messages within [**the 24-hour messaging window**](/getting-started/concepts#two-whatsapp-rules-that-shape-everything).

## How abandoned carts work

An **abandoned cart** is a customer who reached your checkout and left without paying.

If they have not paid **one hour later**, the cart counts as abandoned and your flow runs. Paying stops it, and only one reminder is sent per cart. The one hour is fixed and cannot be changed.

Add `order.recovery_url` to your message to send them straight back to their checkout, with everything still in the basket.

## Order details in your messages

Your messages can include the customer's own order details, what they bought, the total, tracking link and more. Type `@` while writing and pick what you need.

See [Merge fields](/messaging/merge-fields#available-fields) for everything you can use.

## Bringing in your existing customers

Click **Sync contacts** to import the customers already in your store into [Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts).

Only customers **with a phone number** are imported. The rest are skipped.

  src="/settings/img/shopify-connected.png"

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** and confirm. Shopify events stop triggering your automation flows immediately. Your contacts stay in your workspace. Automation flows are not deleted. If you reconnect later, your existing flows will work again.
