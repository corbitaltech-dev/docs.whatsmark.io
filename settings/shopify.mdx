Connect your Shopify store to automatically send WhatsApp messages when customers **place an order, their order ships, their order is delivered, or they leave without completing a purchase**.

Find it under **Integrations → Shopify**. **Integrations** is in the main menu. It opens a page with one card for each tool you can connect.

<Note>
The Shopify integration is available on the **Professional plan**. If your plan does not include it, the Shopify card says **Not in your plan**.
</Note>

## Connecting your store

Before you connect, make sure your Shopify checkout asks for a phone number. WaMatrix.io can only message a shopper on WhatsApp if it has their number.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Shopify card">
  Go to **Integrations**. On the **Shopify** card, click **Connect**. The **Connect Shopify** panel opens on the right.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter your store domain">
  In the **Shop domain** field, enter your Shopify store domain exactly as Shopify gives it. It ends with `.myshopify.com`. For example, `your-store.myshopify.com`.

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/img/connect-shopify-panel.png — The Integrations page with the Connect Shopify panel open on the right, showing the Shop domain field and the Cancel and Continue to Shopify buttons */}
</Step>

<Step title="Click Continue to Shopify">
  You are taken to Shopify.
</Step>

<Step title="Approve the permissions">
  Shopify shows you what WaMatrix.io needs access to — your **customer data**, your **staff and contributor data**, and your **store data**, which covers customers, products, orders and your online store. Click **Install** to approve.

  <Figure
    src="/settings/img/shopify-install.png"
    alt="Shopify's install screen for WaMatrix.io, listing View customer data, View staff and contributor data, and View store data, with Cancel and Install buttons"
    caption="Image shows the Shopify permissions screen."
  />
</Step>

<Step title="You come back automatically">
  You land on the **Shopify** page in WaMatrix.io. Your store domain is shown with **Connecting…** under it. After a moment, this changes to **Connected**. If it still says **Connecting…**, refresh the page.

  <Figure
    src="/settings/img/shopify-connected.png"
    alt="The Shopify Store card with the store domain marked Connected, a Disconnect button on the right, and a Sync contacts row below with its last synced date"
    caption="Image shows a connected Shopify store."
  />
</Step>
</Steps>

To open this page again later, go to **Integrations** and click **Manage** on the Shopify card.

## What you can build

After you connect your store, Shopify events such as a **new order, shipping updates, order cancellation, or an abandoned cart** can automatically start an automation flow.

Choose the event when creating your flow. See [Shopify triggers](/automation/flow-builder#shopify) for the full list and when each one runs.

## Your first message must be a template

Every Shopify flow starts when something happens in your store, not when a customer messages you. For events like **abandoned carts, new orders, and shipping updates,** the customer usually hasn't contacted you yet, so you can't send a regular WhatsApp message.

Start every Shopify flow with a [**Template Message**](/automation/flow-steps/basic#whatsapp-template). Once the customer replies, you can continue the conversation with normal text messages within [**the 24-hour messaging window**](/getting-started/concepts#two-whatsapp-rules-that-shape-everything).

## How abandoned carts work

An **abandoned cart** is a customer who reached your checkout and left without paying.

If they have not paid **one hour later**, the cart counts as abandoned and your flow runs. Paying stops it, and only one reminder is sent per cart. The one hour is fixed and cannot be changed.

Add `order.recovery_url` to your message to send them straight back to their checkout, with everything still in the basket.

## Order details in your messages

Your messages can include the customer's own order details, what they bought, the total, tracking link and more. Type `@` while writing and pick what you need.

See [Merge fields](/messaging/merge-fields#available-fields) for everything you can use.

## Bringing in your existing customers

On the **Shopify** page, click **Sync contacts**. This brings the customers already in your store into [Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts).

Only customers **with a phone number** are imported. The rest are skipped.

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** next to your store. Then click **Disconnect** again to confirm. Shopify events stop triggering your automation flows immediately. Your contacts stay in your workspace. Automation flows are not deleted. If you reconnect later, your existing flows will work again.
