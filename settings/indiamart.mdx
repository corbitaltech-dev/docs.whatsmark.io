When a buyer sends you an enquiry on IndiaMART, WaMatrix.io adds them to your contacts. An automation can then reply to them on WhatsApp. *(Needs a paid plan.)*

Find it under **Integrations → IndiaMART**. **Integrations** is in the main menu. It opens a page with one card for each tool you can connect.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Enquiries become contacts" icon="contact">
    The buyer's name, phone number and email go into your contact list.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reply on WhatsApp" icon="message-circle">
    Start an automation the moment a new enquiry arrives.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A backup check" icon="refresh-cw">

## Before you start

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="A paid plan" icon="lock">
  IndiaMART is not part of the Free Forever plan. If your plan does not include it, the card on the Integrations page says **Not in your plan**, with an **Upgrade to unlock** button. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A paid IndiaMART seller account" icon="building-2">
  IndiaMART lets only paid sellers send enquiries to other tools. On some IndiaMART plans, this is an extra add-on. This is IndiaMART's rule, not ours. See IndiaMART's guides to the [Push API](https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/integration-of-indiamarts-lead-manager-crm-push-api-with-third-party-crms-real-time-push-of-leads/) and the [Pull API](https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/lms-crm-integration-v2/).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Your IndiaMART CRM key (for pull)" icon="key-round">
  The **CRM key** is a code from IndiaMART that lets WaMatrix.io read your enquiries. In IndiaMART's **Lead Manager**, open the three-dot menu (⋮), then **Import/Export Leads → Pull API**. You don't need it if you choose **Push only (webhook)**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Permission to change settings" icon="shield">
  To connect, change or disconnect IndiaMART, your role must let you edit settings. See [Roles and permissions](/core-setup/roles-and-permissions).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A connected WhatsApp number" icon="message-circle">
  You need this to message your buyers. See [Connect WhatsApp](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Step 1: Connect IndiaMART

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the IndiaMART card">
  In WaMatrix.io, go to **Integrations**. On the **IndiaMART** card, click **Connect**. The **Connect IndiaMART** panel opens on the right.

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/img/indiamart-connect-form.png — The Integrations page with the Connect IndiaMART panel open on the right, showing How should leads arrive? set to Push + Pull (recommended), the CRM key, Registered mobile and Default country code fields, and the Cancel and Connect IndiaMART buttons */}
</Step>

<Step title="Choose how enquiries arrive">
  In **How should leads arrive?**, choose one option:

  | Option | How it works | What you need |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | **Push + Pull (recommended)** | Both. Push is fast. Pull catches anything push missed. | Both of the below |
  | **Push only (webhook)** | IndiaMART sends each enquiry to WaMatrix.io as soon as it comes in. | A link that you paste into IndiaMART |
  | **Pull only (scheduled)** | WaMatrix.io checks IndiaMART for new enquiries regularly. | Your CRM key |
</Step>

<Step title="Fill in the form">
  - **CRM key**: paste your key from IndiaMART. You need it for pull.
  - **Registered mobile**: the mobile number on your IndiaMART seller account. WaMatrix.io doesn't need it to bring in enquiries, so you can leave it empty.
  - **Default country code**: added to phone numbers that come in without a country code. It starts as `91`, for India.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Connect IndiaMART">
  WaMatrix.io checks your CRM key with IndiaMART before saving it. When it works, the card says **Connected** and its button changes to **Manage**.
</Step>
</Steps>

If you chose a pull option and left **CRM key** empty, you see **A CRM key is required to pull leads from IndiaMART.** If the key is wrong, the message starts with **IndiaMART rejected this CRM key**.

## Step 2: Paste your link into IndiaMART

Skip this step if you chose **Pull only (scheduled)**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Copy the link">
  On the **IndiaMART** card, click **Manage**. The **IndiaMART Lead Manager** card shows a **Push webhook URL**. This is your own private link. IndiaMART uses it to send each new enquiry to WaMatrix.io. Click the copy icon next to it.

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/img/indiamart-connected.png — The IndiaMART Lead Manager card with Connected, Last lead and Last pull, the Disconnect button, the Push webhook URL field with a copy icon, the note about keeping the link secret, and the View contacts, Fetch leads now and Regenerate URL buttons */}
</Step>

<Step title="Paste it into IndiaMART">
  In IndiaMART's **Lead Manager**, open the three-dot menu (⋮), then **Import/Export Leads → Push API**. Choose **Other**, paste the link and confirm with the code IndiaMART sends to your mobile.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Keep this link private. Anyone who has it can add enquiries to your workspace. If someone gets it by mistake, click **Regenerate URL**. The old link stops working at once, so paste the new link into IndiaMART straight away.
</Warning>

## Step 3: Set up new contacts (optional)

The **Contact mapping** card decides how contacts from IndiaMART are set up. When **Use custom mapping for IndiaMART** is **off**, these contacts get your usual [Contact Defaults](/settings/contact-configuration).

Turn it **on** to give them their own settings, then click **Save Changes**:

- **Type**: **Guest**, **Lead** or **Customer**.
- **Status** and **Source**: choose from your [statuses](/core-setup/statuses) and [sources](/core-setup/sources).
- **Assign to**: **Unassigned**, **Round robin**, **Online team member** or **Specific team member**. For **Specific team member**, pick the person in **Team member**.
- **Labels** and **Groups**: optional.

When the switch is on, WaMatrix.io uses only what you choose on this card. It does not fill gaps from your Contact Defaults. For example, if you leave **Labels** empty, these contacts get no labels.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/img/indiamart-contact-mapping.png — The Contact mapping card with the Use custom mapping for IndiaMART switch turned on, the note that every field is applied as a set, and the Type, Status, Source, Assign to, Labels and Groups fields */}

<Tip>
Pick a **Source** here, such as one called IndiaMART. Then **View contacts** lists exactly these contacts. When the switch is off, new contacts get the source from your Contact Defaults, so **View contacts** may not show them.
</Tip>

<Accordion title="What the IndiaMART page shows" icon="info">
  The **IndiaMART Lead Manager** card shows the status and when the last enquiry came in. Until then, it says **No leads yet**. It also shows **Last pull**, which is when WaMatrix.io last checked IndiaMART. A note under the link says how often the regular check runs.

  - **View contacts** opens your contact list, filtered by the **Source** that these contacts get.
  - **Fetch leads now** checks IndiaMART for new enquiries straight away. You see it only when a CRM key is saved.
  - **Regenerate URL** makes a new private link. See the warning above.

  Below it, the **Update connection** card lets you change how enquiries arrive or replace your CRM key. Leave **CRM key** empty to keep the key you saved, then click **Save**.
</Accordion>

## How an enquiry becomes a contact

<Accordion title="What WaMatrix.io does with each new enquiry" icon="workflow">
  1. Looks for a contact with the same phone number.
  2. If there is none, creates a new contact with the buyer's **name**, **phone number** and **email**.
  3. Starts any WhatsApp automation that uses the **Leads** trigger.

  The other details stay with the enquiry, such as the company, city, subject, message and product. You can use them in your messages.
</Accordion>

A few cases work differently:

- **The phone number is already in your contacts.** No new contact is made. The enquiry is added to that contact. If the contact has no email yet, the email from IndiaMART is added. An email you already have is never replaced.
- **The enquiry has no phone number.** No contact is made and no automation starts.
- **Your plan's contact limit is full.** WaMatrix.io keeps the enquiry, but no contact is made. A red banner says **leads waiting on your contact limit**. Upgrade your plan, then click **Import waiting leads**. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
- **The same enquiry arrives by push and by pull.** It is saved only once, and only one contact is made.

New enquiries that **Fetch leads now** brings in start automations, just like any new enquiry. Enquiries you bring in with **Import waiting leads** do not. They may be days old, so an instant reply would arrive late.

## Reply to new enquiries on WhatsApp

Build a WhatsApp automation and set its **Start Trigger** to **Leads**. Under **Lead Source**, choose **IndiaMART**, or keep **Any lead source**. The **Leads** option shows only on WhatsApp automations. See [Leads trigger](/automation/flow-builder#leads).

Make the first message a WhatsApp **template**: a message format that Meta approves in advance. WhatsApp lets a business message someone first only with a template. This is a WhatsApp rule, not a WaMatrix.io one. See Meta's [templates overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview).

To use the enquiry details, type `@` in a message box and pick from the **Lead** group, for example **Company** or **Enquiry Subject**. See [Merge fields](/messaging/merge-fields).

## Fix a problem

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="The card says Needs attention" icon="triangle-alert">
  **Needs attention** means IndiaMART did not accept your CRM key. Until you fix it, no new enquiries come in, by push or by pull. To fix it:

  1. In IndiaMART's **Lead Manager**, open the three-dot menu (⋮), then **Import/Export Leads → Pull API**, and copy your key.
  2. In WaMatrix.io, click **Connect** on the **IndiaMART** card. Choose how enquiries should arrive, paste the key into **CRM key** and click **Connect IndiaMART**.
  3. Click **Manage**, then **Fetch leads now** to check for enquiries you missed.

  {/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/img/indiamart-needs-attention.png — The IndiaMART card on the Integrations page with the Needs attention badge and the Connect button */}
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="IndiaMART rate limit reached. The next scheduled pull will retry." icon="clock">
  IndiaMART limits how often its enquiries can be read. You don't need to do anything. The status stays **Connected**, and the next check tries again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An enquiry came in, but my automation did not start" icon="bot">
  The automation starts only when the enquiry has a phone number and fits in your contact limit. Also check that the automation uses a WhatsApp channel and is turned on.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Disconnect

On the IndiaMART page, click **Disconnect** on the IndiaMART Lead Manager card. The **Disconnect IndiaMART?** box opens. Click **Disconnect** to confirm.

Contacts made from past enquiries stay, and your automations stay too. New enquiries stop arriving, and WaMatrix.io removes your CRM key. If you connect again later, enter your CRM key again. You can also remove the link from **Push API** in IndiaMART.
