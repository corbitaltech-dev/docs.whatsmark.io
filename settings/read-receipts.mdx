When someone on your team opens a customer's chat, WhatsApp can show that customer **blue ticks** on their message, so they know you've seen it. While your team types a reply, it can also show **typing…** on the customer's phone. Customers can see that someone is on it.

In **Settings → Read Receipts**, you decide whether your workspace sends either of these.

<Note>
  Blue ticks and the typing bubble need a paid plan. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
</Note>

## The two switches

Both switches are on by default. They work when your team replies in **Chat**. For [automations](/automation/flow-steps/basic#show-typing-indicator), use **Show typing indicator** in the message step.

| Switch | When it's on | When it's off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Send read receipts** | The customer sees blue ticks when you open their chat. | No blue ticks. The customer doesn't know you've read it. |
| **Show when an agent is typing** | The customer sees "typing…" while you write a reply. | No "typing…". They only see your reply when it arrives. |
