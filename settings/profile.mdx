These settings apply only to your account. They do not affect anyone else in your workspace.

Open them by clicking your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then My Profile.

The page includes Profile and Password tabs, along with Two-Factor Authentication, Active Sessions, and Recent Activity.

## Your details

<BrowserFrame
  src="/settings/img/profile-form.png"
  url="whatsmark.io/settings/profile"

  caption="Image shows the Profile tab."
/>

On the **Profile** tab:

- **Name** — Your display name. Required.
- **WhatsApp number** — your own number.
- **Email Address** — Your email address. You can't change it yourself.
- **Photo** – Click the **camera** icon to upload a new photo or choose one from the **Media Library**. Supports **JPEG**, **PNG**, and **WebP** files up to **5 MB**. Click **Remove Photo** to show your initials again.

<Note>
Your photo uses a little of your workspace storage, and it also shows up in your Media Library.
</Note>

<Warning>
If you change your WhatsApp number, a 6-digit code is sent to the new number first. Type it in to confirm. Your number only changes once you enter the code.
</Warning>

## Changing your password

<BrowserFrame
  src="/settings/img/password-form.png"
  url="whatsmark.io/settings/profile"
  alt="Password tab showing current password, new password, and confirm new password fields"
  caption="Image shows the Password tab."
/>

On the **Password** tab:

- **Current Password** — Enter your current password. This field is not shown if you signed in with Google, because you don't have a password.
- **New Password** and **Confirm New Password** — Enter the same password in both fields. You can also click Generate Strong Password, then Use This Password to fill both fields automatically.
- Click **Update Password**.

## Two-factor authentication

It adds an extra layer of security. After entering your password, you'll also need to enter a code from your phone, so your account stays protected even if someone knows your password.
<BrowserFrame
  src="/settings/img/two-factor-card.png"
  url="whatsmark.io/settings/profile"
  alt="Two-Factor Authentication card showing 2FA status and a Manage 2FA link"
  caption="Image shows the Two-Factor Authentication card."
/>

The card tells you whether it is on or off. Click **Manage 2FA** to change it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Confirm your password">
  A safety check before you can change anything.
</Step>

<Step title="Scan the QR code">
  Open an authenticator app, such as **Google Authenticator**, and scan the QR code. If you can't scan it, enter the setup key instead. Then click Continue.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter the 6-digit code">
  Read it from the app and click **Confirm**.
</Step>

<Step title="Save your recovery codes">
  Click **View Recovery Codes** and save them somewhere safe. If you lose your phone, you can use these codes to sign in. Each recovery code can only be used once.
</Step>
</Steps>

To turn it off again, click **Disable two-factor** on the same page.

<Tip>
Turn this on if you are the workspace owner or an admin. It is the quickest way to make your account much harder to break into.
</Tip>

## Active sessions

All the devices you're signed in on are listed here. For each one, you can see the **browser, operating system, IP address, and last active time**. Your current device is marked as **This device**.

- Click **Log Out** to sign out a specific device. You can't sign out the device you're currently using.
- Click **Sign Out All Others** to sign out every other device. This option only appears if you're signed in on more than one device.

## Recent activity

The last 5 things you did in the workspace. To see everything, and what your team has been doing, open the [Activity Log](/reporting/activity-logs).
