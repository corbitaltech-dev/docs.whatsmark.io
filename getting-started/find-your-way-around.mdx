The menu on the left of WaMatrix.io has three parts. Things you use every day sit at the top. Each platform, such as WhatsApp, has its own group in the middle. Workspace tools sit at the bottom.

## The three parts of the menu

1. **Shared items at the top.** These work across every platform.
   - **Dashboard**: a quick look at how your workspace is doing.
   - **Inbox**: all your chats from every platform, in one place.
   - **All Contacts**: every person you can message, from every platform.
2. **One group per platform.** There is a group for **WhatsApp**, **Messenger** and **Instagram**, if your plan includes them. Each group holds the tools for that platform only.
3. **Workspace tools at the bottom.**
   - **AI Assistants**: AI helpers that learn from your documents and website, then answer customers.
   - **Media Library**: the images and files you upload.
   - **Activity Log**: every action taken in your workspace.
   - **Settings**: your workspace settings, such as business hours.

The last item is **Setup**. Click it to open a side panel for your team and workspace setup: **Team**, **Roles**, **Statuses**, **Sources**, **Labels**, **Groups**, **Canned Replies**, **AI Prompts**, **Custom Fields** and **API Tokens**.

## What is inside each platform group

A **channel** is an account you connect to WaMatrix.io. It can be a WhatsApp number, a Facebook Page for Messenger, or an Instagram account. A **template** is a saved message you can send again and again.

Each group shows only the tools that platform supports.

### Only one group opens at a time

A group opens on its own when you are on one of its pages. The other groups stay closed, so the menu stays short. You can click any group name to open or close it.

Campaigns, automations and templates each belong to one platform. For example, a campaign you send on WhatsApp is under **WhatsApp → Campaigns**. You will not see it under Messenger.

## Adding something new

The **Add Campaign**, **Add Automation**, **Add Reply Bot** and **Add Template** buttons first show a list of your channels on that platform. Choose the channel you want to use. The form then opens with that channel already chosen.

If you have not connected a channel on that platform yet, connect one first.

## All Contacts or a platform's Contacts

- **All Contacts** shows everyone, from every platform.
- **Contacts** inside a platform group shows only the people from that platform.

You can use **Add Contact** and **Import Contacts** only on **All Contacts** and on **WhatsApp → Contacts**.

Messenger and Instagram contacts are added for you. A person appears on the list when they message your Page or account. You cannot add them yourself, because Meta lets a business start these chats only after the person reaches out. This is Meta's rule, not ours. Meta explains it in the [Messenger and Instagram messaging policy](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/policy/policy-overview).

## What you see depends on your plan and your access

### Your plan

The platform groups you see depend on your plan. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid) for what each plan includes.

If your plan includes a platform but nothing is connected yet, its group still shows. Pages in that group show the message **No Instagram account connected** (or WhatsApp, or Messenger) and a **Connect Instagram** button. Click the button to go to that platform's Channels page.

<Note>
  You see the **Connect** button only if your role is allowed to connect channels. If you don't see it, ask the person who manages your team.
</Note>

### Your access, if you are a team member

A team member may see only some platforms. Whoever manages your team chooses this with the **Platform access** setting. It is set on each role, and can be changed for each person. *(Team invites need a paid plan.)*

For example, a support person may see only the **WhatsApp** group. A social media person may see only **Messenger** and **Instagram**.

See [Team and roles](/core-setup/team-and-roles).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did Chat go?" icon="messages-square">
    It is now called **Inbox**. It holds your chats from every platform you have connected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where is the Connect Channel button?" icon="plug">
    Each platform now has its own **Channels** page. Open the platform group, click **Channels**, and use **Connect WhatsApp**, **Connect Messenger** or **Connect Instagram**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where did WhatsApp Activity Log go?" icon="scroll-text">
    It is no longer in **Setup**. Open the **WhatsApp** group and click **Activity Log**. Messenger and Instagram have their own **Activity Log** in their groups too.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does an item have a lock next to it?" icon="lock">
    Your plan does not include that item. Click it to read what it does. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid) to compare plans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see Instagram or Messenger in my menu. Why?" icon="eye-off">
    There are two common reasons:

    - Your plan does not include that platform.
    - You are a team member, and your **Platform access** does not include it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If I change my plan, do I lose a platform's data?" icon="shield">
    No, not while an account on that platform is still connected. Its group stays in your menu, so you can still open your contacts, chats and channels there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
