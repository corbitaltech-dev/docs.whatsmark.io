The left menu has three main sections. At the top, you’ll find the tools you use every day. In the middle, each platform, such as WhatsApp, has its own section. At the bottom, you’ll find your workspace tools.

## The three parts of the menu

1. **Shared items at the top.** These work across every platform.
   - **Dashboard**: a quick look at how your workspace is doing.
   - **Chat**: all your chats from every platform, in one place.
   - **All Contacts**: every person you can message, from every platform.
2. **One group per platform.** There is a group for **WhatsApp**, **Messenger** and **Instagram**. Each group holds the tools for that platform only. *(Instagram needs a paid plan.)*
3. **Workspace tools at the bottom.**
   - **AI Assistants**: AI helpers that learn from your documents and website, then answer customers. *(Needs a paid plan.)*
   - **Media Library**: the images and files you upload.
   - **Activity Log**: changes your team made in the workspace.
   - **Integrations**: connect Shopify, WooCommerce, Facebook Lead Ads and IndiaMART. *(Needs a paid plan.)*
   - **Settings**: your workspace settings, such as business hours.

The last item is **Setup**. Click it to open a side panel where you can manage your team and workspace settings, including **Team**, **Roles**, **Statuses**, **Sources**, **Labels**, **Groups**, **Canned Replies**, **AI Prompts**, **Custom Fields**, **API Tokens**, and **Changelog**.

The **Changelog** shows the latest updates and new features in WaMatrix.io. *(Canned Replies and API Tokens require a paid plan.)*

## Explore each platform group

Each platform group contains the tools you need to manage your connected accounts and conversations. A **channel** is a connected account, such as a WhatsApp number, Facebook Page, or Instagram account. You can manage your channels and access the features available for each platform.

**Templates** let you save frequently used messages and reuse them whenever needed. For WhatsApp, message templates must be approved by Meta before you can send them.

## All Contacts or a platform's Contacts

- **All Contacts** shows everyone, from every platform.
- **Contacts** inside a platform group shows only the people from that platform.

You can use **Create Contact** and **Import Contacts** only on **All Contacts** and on **WhatsApp → Contacts**.

WaMatrix.io adds Messenger and Instagram contacts for you. A person appears on the list when they message your Page or account, or when a Reply Bot answers their comment. You cannot add them yourself. Meta lets a business message a person on Messenger or Instagram only after that person contacts the business first. This is Meta's rule, not ours. Meta explains it in its [Messenger and Instagram messaging policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/policy).

## What you see depends on your plan and your access

### Your plan and available features

Your plan determines which platform groups and menu items are available in WaMatrix.io. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid) to learn what each plan includes.

- **Platforms not included in your plan:** Their groups won't appear in the menu.
- **Paid features:** Some features, such as **API Tokens** in **Setup**, are only available on plans that include them. These features won't appear if your plan doesn't support them.

### Your access, if you are a team member

A team member may see only some platforms. Whoever manages your team chooses this with the **Platform access** setting. It is set on each role, and can be changed for each person. *(Team invites need a paid plan.)*

For example, a support person may see only the **WhatsApp** group. A social media person may see only **Messenger** and **Instagram**.

See [Team and roles](/core-setup/team-and-roles).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is the Connect Channel button?" icon="plug">
    Each platform has its own **Channels** page. Open the platform group, click **Channels**, and use **Connect WhatsApp**, **Connect Messenger** or **Connect Instagram**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where are Shopify and the other integrations?" icon="blocks">
    Click **Integrations** in the main menu. You see one card for each integration: **Shopify**, **WooCommerce**, **Facebook Lead Ads** and **IndiaMART**. Click a card to open its page. *(Integrations need a paid plan.)*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where did WhatsApp Activity Log go?" icon="scroll-text">
    It is no longer in **Setup**. Open the **WhatsApp** group and click **Activity Log**. Messenger and Instagram have their own **Activity Log** in their groups too.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does an item have a lock next to it?" icon="lock">
    Your plan does not include that item. Click it to read what it does. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid) to compare plans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If I change my plan, do I lose a platform's data?" icon="shield">
    No. Changing your plan does not delete your data.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
