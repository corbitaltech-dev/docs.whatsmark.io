The menu on the left of WaMatrix.io has three parts. The things you use every day are at the top. Each platform, such as WhatsApp, has its own group in the middle. Workspace tools are at the bottom.

## The three parts of the menu

1. **Shared items at the top.** These work across every platform.
   - **Dashboard**: a quick look at how your workspace is doing.
   - **Chat**: all your chats from every platform, in one place.
   - **All Contacts**: every person you can message, from every platform.
2. **One group per platform.** There is a group for **WhatsApp**, **Messenger** and **Instagram**. Each group holds the tools for that platform only. *(Instagram needs a paid plan.)*
3. **Workspace tools at the bottom.**
   - **AI Assistants**: AI helpers that learn from your documents and website, then answer customers. *(Needs a paid plan.)*
   - **Media Library**: the images and files you upload.
   - **Activity Log**: changes your team made in the workspace.
   - **Integrations**: connect Shopify, WooCommerce, Facebook Lead Ads and IndiaMART. *(Needs a paid plan.)*
   - **Settings**: your workspace settings, such as business hours.

The last item is **Setup**. Click it to open a side panel for your team and workspace setup: **Team**, **Roles**, **Statuses**, **Sources**, **Labels**, **Groups**, **Canned Replies**, **AI Prompts**, **Custom Fields**, **API Tokens** and **Changelog**. **Changelog** lists what is new in WaMatrix.io. *(Canned Replies and API Tokens need a paid plan.)*

## What is inside each platform group

A **channel** is an account you connect to WaMatrix.io. It can be a WhatsApp number, a Facebook Page for Messenger, or an Instagram account. A **template** is a saved message you can send again and again. On WhatsApp, Meta must approve it first.

Each group shows only the tools that platform supports.

### How the groups open and close

The group for the page you are on opens by itself. The other groups start closed, so the menu stays short. Click a group name to open or close it.

Campaigns, automations and templates each belong to one platform. For example, a campaign you send on WhatsApp is under **WhatsApp → Campaigns**. You will not see it under Messenger.

## Adding something new

To add something inside a platform group, use the button at the top of the page:

- **Campaigns**: **Create Campaign**
- **Automations**: **Create Flow**
- **Reply Bots**: **Add Reply Bot**
- **Templates**: **Create Template** on WhatsApp, **New Template** on Messenger, and **Add Template** on Instagram

Each of these buttons first shows a list of your channels on that platform. Choose the channel you want to use. The form then opens with that channel already chosen.

If you have not connected a channel on that platform yet, connect one first.

## All Contacts or a platform's Contacts

- **All Contacts** shows everyone, from every platform.
- **Contacts** inside a platform group shows only the people from that platform.

You can use **Create Contact** and **Import Contacts** only on **All Contacts** and on **WhatsApp → Contacts**.

WaMatrix.io adds Messenger and Instagram contacts for you. A person appears on the list when they message your Page or account, or when a Reply Bot answers their comment. You cannot add them yourself. Meta lets a business message a person on Messenger or Instagram only after that person contacts the business first. This is Meta's rule, not ours. Meta explains it in its [Messenger and Instagram messaging policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/policy).

## What you see depends on your plan and your access

### Your plan

Your plan decides which platform groups and menu items you see. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid) for what each plan includes.

- **A platform your plan doesn't include** has no group in the menu. The exception is a platform where you still have an account connected. Its group stays, so you can still reach your contacts and chats there.
- **Some paid items show a lock icon.** Click one to read a short note about what it does. On the Free Forever plan, these items show a lock: **AI Assistants**, **Integrations**, **WhatsApp → Analytics** and **Messenger → Reply Bots**.
- **Some paid items don't appear at all** until your plan includes them, such as **API Tokens** in **Setup**.

If your plan includes a platform but nothing is connected yet, its group still shows. Most pages in that group show the message **No Instagram account connected** (or WhatsApp, or Messenger) and a **Connect Instagram** button. Click the button to go to that platform's **Channels** page.

<Note>
  You see the **Connect** button only if your role is allowed to connect channels. If you don't see it, ask the person who manages your team.
</Note>

### Your access, if you are a team member

A team member may see only some platforms. Whoever manages your team chooses this with the **Platform access** setting. It is set on each role, and can be changed for each person. *(Team invites need a paid plan.)*

For example, a support person may see only the **WhatsApp** group. A social media person may see only **Messenger** and **Instagram**.

See [Team and roles](/core-setup/team-and-roles).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is the Connect Channel button?" icon="plug">
    Each platform has its own **Channels** page. Open the platform group, click **Channels**, and use **Connect WhatsApp**, **Connect Messenger** or **Connect Instagram**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where are Shopify and the other integrations?" icon="blocks">
    Click **Integrations** in the main menu. You see one card for each integration: **Shopify**, **WooCommerce**, **Facebook Lead Ads** and **IndiaMART**. Click a card to open its page. *(Integrations need a paid plan.)*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where did WhatsApp Activity Log go?" icon="scroll-text">
    It is no longer in **Setup**. Open the **WhatsApp** group and click **Activity Log**. Messenger and Instagram have their own **Activity Log** in their groups too.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does an item have a lock next to it?" icon="lock">
    Your plan does not include that item. Click it to read what it does. See [Free vs. paid](/getting-started/free-vs-paid) to compare plans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see Instagram or Messenger in my menu. Why?" icon="eye-off">
    There are two common reasons:

    - Your plan does not include that platform. Instagram needs a paid plan.
    - You are a team member, and your **Platform access** does not include it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If I change my plan, do I lose a platform's data?" icon="shield">
    Changing your plan does not delete your data. While an account on that platform is still connected, its group stays in your menu. You can still open your contacts, chats and channels there. If you disconnect every account on a platform your plan no longer includes, its group leaves the menu.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
